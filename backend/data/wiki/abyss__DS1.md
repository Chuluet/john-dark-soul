# Abyss (DS1)

The Abyss is a location in Dark Souls, as well as a storyline concept featured in all three games in the series.

The in-game location is at the heart of the New Londo Ruins, a featureless expanse where the Chosen Undead must slay the Four Kings and can encounter Darkstalker Kaathe. As a concept, "Abyss" is the name given to both a force that consumes or corrupts most forms of life, and to the areas in which it dwells; it is tied to the Age of Dark, and also gave birth to a twisted offshoot known as The Deep.

## Lore

The Abyss is a realm of Dark born from the Dark Soul or its fragments. It's an ecosystem capable of creating life, as generated by one of the core components of the First Flame, and of expanding while slowly absorbing everything in its path; it is uninhabitable to all beings aligned with Fire including the Gods, as they require special equipment or protection to prevent corruption (both in the spiritual and physical sense). In contrast, any beings aligned with or indifferent to the Abyss (such as Humans) are able to roam it with no risk of harm or corruption.

Knight Artorias famously traversed New Londo's Abyss and hunted the Darkwraiths therein. He was protected by the power of a magical ring, which he gained after making a covenant with the beasts there. The ring prevented him from being swallowed by the void, but it led to his sword becoming cursed. In recognition of his actions, he was awarded one of Anor Londo's treasures, a Silver Pendant that allowed him to repel Dark sorceries; eventually he traveled to Oolacile to combat the threat of Manus, the lord of the Abyss. He ultimately failed in his mission he was overtaken by the dark, leaving behind his companion Sif.

## Location

In Dark Souls the Abyss is located at the end of the New Londo Ruins. To reach to the Abyss, the floodgates must be lowered and the Covenant of Artorias ring, a reward for killing Great Grey Wolf Sif, must be worn. After climbing down a spiral staircase within a tower, the path will abruptly end, making it necessary to descend into the darkness of the Abyss.

The DLC Artorias of the Abyss allows us to explore a more recent Abyss, despite being located in a past era, deep within the dungeons of Oolacile.

## General information

Dropping into the Abyss will immediately trigger the area boss, the Four Kings, to spawn. After the Four Kings have been defeated, a bonfire will appear. At this point, the Abyss will cease being hostile, making the Covenant of Artorias ring useless.

## Characters

Darkstalker Kaathe will appear after the boss has been defeated. The player cannot have placed the Lordvessel under Kingseeker Frampt's guidance yet; if they do so before having met Kaathe, Kaathe will not appear.
