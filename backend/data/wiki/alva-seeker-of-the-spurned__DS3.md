# Alva, Seeker of the Spurned (DS3)

Alva, Seeker of the Spurned is a dark spirit in Dark Souls III. He also appears as a dark spirit in The Ringed City DLC, where he is known simply as .

## Locations

He will invade on the cliffside before reaching Irithyll Dungeon, as soon as the player leaves the manor's doorway.

He can be later encountered in the Ringed City as the player approaches an alleyway with a lootable corpse, which is found past a single Ringed Knight.

## Plot

Alva is an optional encounter that can be battled if the player is a Host of Embers in his invasion area found between Irithyll of the Boreal Valley and its homonymous dungeon. He must be defeated before killing Yhorm the Giant, as that will prevent him from invading any longer. If the player is successful, they'll earn his weapon, the Murakumo, and his armor set, situated on a corpse that will appear in the lower level of the dungeon, just in front of Karla's cell.

Unlike his base game counterpart, The Ringed City DLC's Seeker of the Spurned will invade the player regardless of if they are a Host of Embers or not, a trait shared with other dark spirits in this expansion. Defeating him here nets them the Wolf Ring +3 and allows them easier access to the corpse he was guarding, which holds the Black Witch Set.

## Lore

Alva is a renowned wandering knight who is very popular among troubadours, who sing the tales of his many tragic adventures, which also involve the figures of Saint Serreta and Zullie the Witch. Eventually, Alva ceased his aimless wayfaring, equipped a peculiar eastern blade, and began a search for the spurned in a journey with seemingly no end. This led him to the forsaken prison of Irithyll, where Karla, a spurned child of the Abyss, had been locked away. The knight seemed to be interested in the witch, possibly even wishing to protect her. This puts him at odd with the Ashen One, who is forced to engage him in a battle and vanquish him, causing him to seemingly perish near the poor witch's cell.

This, however, was not the end for the Seeker of the Spurned, now nameless, as he is later encountered in the Ringed City of legend, long after the events of Irithyll Dungeon. It seems that his journey had ended there, where he had finally found the one he was looking for. Unfortunately, his beloved had been dead all along, and the knight could do nothing more than protect her resting place from anyone who'd dare loot her remains. Once again the Ashen One would battle the knight, conquering the ordeal and slaying him for good.

However, the White-Faced Locusts preach of a way for the lovers to be reunited in the Abyss, though their words are hard to fully trust.
