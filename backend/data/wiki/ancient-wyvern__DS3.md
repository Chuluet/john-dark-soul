# Ancient Wyvern (DS3)

Boss =

|-|Miniboss =

The Ancient Wyvern (Japanese: 古の飛竜) is a boss, and later a miniboss, in Dark Souls III.

## Location

Two are found in Archdragon Peak:
In the giant courtyard after the first bonfire, being the first boss of this area.
A miniboss variant with lesser defenses blocks the path leading to the Great Belfry.

## Description

An imposing beast that will hinder the Ashen One's progress through Archdragon Peak. Typical of wyverns, it has two legs and two wings along with thick, ashen scales that are not easily penetrated. Its body features varying contours of green, red, and blue, which can perhaps be attributed to its age, speaking to the longevity of the wyvern. On its head it appears to have dense, horn-like scales, with its mouth complete with an elongated "mustache".
