# Archdeacon McDonnell (DS3)

Archdeacon McDonnell, or McDonnell of the Boreal Valley, is a character in Dark Souls III. Praying to his remains allows access to the Aldrich Faithful covenant.

## Location

Archdeacon McDonnell's remains can be found in Irithyll of the Boreal Valley, at the Water Reserve.

From the Pontiff Sulyvahn bonfire, proceed onward until reaching the building at the end of the big courtyard with several dead Giant Slaves. Upon entering, hit the left wall to uncover an illusory wall. Descend the ladder and dispose of the two Sulyvahn's Beasts waiting at the bottom. Archdeacon McDonnell's remains are located at the far-left corner of the hall, near the Water Reserve bonfire, which will appear once both beasts have been defeated.

## Lore

McDonnell, along with Archdeacons Royce and Klimt, was an archdeacon of the Way of White. The three men managed the Cathedral of the Deep, overseeing its rituals of purification from stagnant Darkness.

At one point, the cathedral welcomed Aldrich, an exiled Irithyllian priest who derived pleasure from eating humans. Aldrich shared his perverse behaviour with others in the cathedral, and sacrifices were brought to him to appease his hunger and grant him the strength to link the First Flame and become a Lord of Cinder.

After Aldrich linked the Flame, a cult formed around his figure, but the deacons of the cathedral also found themselves fighting abominations that emerged from the Deep. The Cathedral, once a peaceful and sacred place for purification, was corrupted by the foul Aldrich, as darkness stagnated and accumulated in the Deep until it had become the final rest of abhorrent creatures. McDonnell found delight in the stagnation of darkness and abandoned his faith in the First Flame and in the gods, devoting himself to the Deep. He spread his belief among the clerics of the cathedral; he even created staffs that channeled faith to produce Deep sorceries, a sacrilegious act. In time the cathedral's deacons became both clergymen and sorcerers, embracing the cult of the Deep and the worship of its Saint, Aldrich.

When the First Flame began to fade and Prince Lothric refused to link it, Aldrich was awakened from his tomb to become fuel for it again, but the Lord of Cinder refused the call. Aldrich was in fact intent on returning to Irithyll to devour Dark Sun Gwyndolin and usher the Age of the Deep, ending the Age of Fire and leaving in its place a realm of putrid and stagnant darkness. McDonnell, sharing Aldrich's ideals, cast off his white robes and replaced them with robes of deep blue. The Archdeacon gathered an army of deacons and evangelists, and abandoned the cathedral to follow the Saint of the Deep on his crusade toward Irithyll.

Once Aldrich defeated Sulyvahn and took control of the city, McDonnell remained to watch over the old Cathedral where the saint was busy digesting Gwyndolin, establishing the covenant of Aldrich Faithful, passing on his dark sorceries to followers who brought him evidence of success in their task.

At some point McDonnell was incapacitated and his body ended up in the lower levels of Irithyll, in a cistern guarded by two of Pontiff Sulyvahn's beasts, however he is still able to recognize others, as he manages Aldrich's covenant and teaches sorceries.
