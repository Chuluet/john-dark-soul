# Archdragon Peak (DS3)

Archdragon Peak (古竜の頂 Kōryū no Itadaki, Ancient Dragon Peak) is a hidden, optional location in Dark Souls III.

## Description

Archdragon Peak is an optional location that can be accessed by performing the "Path of the Dragon" gesture near the meditating dragon statue where the Dragon Torso Stone is found in the Irithyll Dungeon.

Archdragon Peak is populated by Man Serpents and Rock Lizards. It was seemingly built as a shrine to worship the Ancient Dragons by their descendants, the Man Serpents. Contrary to what its name implies, this area does not currently contain any true dragons, as those died long ago at the hands of the first Lords. The only dragons seemingly alive is the King of the Storm, the Nameless King's companion stormdrake mount, and the Ancient Wyverns. Archdragon Peak also overlooks on the body of an ancient dragon: the body can be seen clearly on the peak on the opposite side of the great bell.

The main boss of this area is The Nameless King.

## Lore

Nowadays, Archdragon Peak is no longer inhabited by ancient dragons. However, their descendants, the Man Serpents, persisted and they serve as guards of the Peak. Darkeater Midir is also a descendant of the archdragons although he does not reside here but instead near the faraway Ringed City.

Those who desire to worship dragons often end up here, as evidenced by they many corpses of those who died trying. Those who choose the path of the dragons strive for perfect imitation, using the Dragon Head Stone at first, then the torso stone, imbued with the everlasting dragons' power. Special "twinkling" stones existed for the most fervent worshipers, who imbued them not only with a more advanced dragon-like form, but also a hovering mirage of an Archdragon. The way of worship and emulation was long and arduous and only those with the most fortitude could complete it.

Archdragon Peak can be entered only after learning the Path of the Dragon gesture found upon a corpse of a Drakeblood Knight in the Consumed King's Garden; this faction of knights can be summoned in Archdragon Peak by the man serpents there. Drakeblood Knights worshiped the blood of dragons; and the red cloth decorating their armor is said to symbolize their yearning for blood.

In Archdragon Peak, the Calamity Ring is found, reportedly made from the orange eye of a calamitous dragon, the ring considered a symbol of dragon worship. This dragon was none other than Black Dragon Kalameet, said to be the last one of ancient dragons.

The Nameless King, known for once allying with ancient dragons, resides on Archdragon Peak. He is accompanied by a stormdrake, which he tamed and fought alongside him. Dragon Slayer Ornstein's weapon and armor can be found laying around the Nameless King's arena; he was said to have left his guard in Anor Londo cathedral to search for the Nameless King.
