# Armorer Dennis (DS2)

Armorer Dennis is a dark spirit exclusive for Dark Souls II: Scholar of the First Sin.

## Locations

He'll invade the player in the Forest of Fallen Giants, near the Soldier's Rest bonfire.
Iron Keep's main gate. He'll usually invade after Fencer Sharron, although sometimes he can invade first.

## Attacks

Soul Greatsword
Soul Spear
Soul Vortex

## Combat Overview

Armorer Dennis is a very dangerous dark spirit due to his continuous and extensive use of sorceries. He'll relentlessly run toward the player and attack, giving little chance to find an opening. If the player opts to raise a shield, he'll be prone to perform a guard break and follow with two or three slashes from his Blue Flame. Most of the time, however, he'll be casting sorceries which can cover a large area, like Soul Greatsword or Soul Vortex. If the player is far away, he'll cast Soul Spear.
