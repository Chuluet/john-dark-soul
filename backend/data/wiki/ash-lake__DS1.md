# Ash Lake (DS1)

Ash Lake is a location in Dark Souls.

## Description

Ash Lake is an optional area accessed by successfully reaching the bottom of The Great Hollow, through a tunnel past the Mushroom enemies. It is a wide, open space with seemingly endless views of Archtrees amidst deep, dark blue waters and below a sky of dense clouds. A large stretch of sand connects two Archtrees, and the shores abruptly end, leading to a watery grave.

The Stone Dragon is a passive and invulnerable entity that serves as initiator to the Path of the Dragon covenant.

## Bonfires

Two bonfires are at either end of the area. One can be found shortly after arriving from The Great Hollow, and another is directly in front of the Stone Dragon, which is the only one in the area that can be warped to with the Lordvessel.
