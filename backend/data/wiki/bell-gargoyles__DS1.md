# Bell Gargoyles (DS1)

The Bell Gargoyles (鐘のガーゴイル) are dual bosses in Dark Souls.

There are four Gargoyles in the game: the first two are part of the same boss battle atop the Undead Parish, with the second only joining halfway through, while the last two are mini-bosses found in Anor Londo. None of them respawn.

## Description

They appear to be armored gargoyles wielding axes and shields with fire or lightning attacks. They have axes on their tails which they use to swing at you.

## Location

On top of the church in the Undead Parish, reached by passing through the room full of hollows and a Channeler, before climbing two ladders and finally entering the fog gate. They are also found in Anor Londo as mini-bosses: Exit the first bonfire, go straight on and down the lift, and a gargoyle will be straight ahead. The second is beyond the rotating staircase-bridge.

## Lore

Gargoyles are stone creatures created by the Gods through magic to help patrol Anor Londo or sites deemed important. In addition to their tough and hard skin, they are able to breathe fire or lightning and are armed with a bronze shield and a bronze helmet for purely decorative purposes while they use a bronze halberd as a weapon.

After Lord Gwyn sacrificed himself to link the First Flame and prolong the Age of Fire, several Gargoyles were placed to guard the Bell of Awakening placed on the Undead Church of the Undead Burg and act as ritual challenge. The Undead who wished to follow in Gwyn's footsteps to sacrifice themselves to the Flame would have to be tested before obtaining the Lordvessel and seeking powerful souls. Ringing the Bells of Awakening represents only the first of these challenges, and success means gaining access to Sen's Fortress to be tested further.
