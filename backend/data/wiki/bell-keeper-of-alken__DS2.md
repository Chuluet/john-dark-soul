# Bell Keeper of Alken (DS2)

For the enemy that can be found in the same locations, see Bell Keeper (enemy). For the invading Dark Spirit in Belfry Luna, see Bell Keeper (Dark Spirit).
The Bell Keeper of Alken, also known as the Bell Keeper of Venn, is a character in Dark Souls II. He is voiced by Jason Pitt, who also voiced Vengarl of Forossa, Satsuki in Demon's Souls and Valtr, Master of the League in Bloodborne.

The leader of the Bell Keepers covenant, he can found in both areas protected by its members, Belfry Luna and Belfry Sol.

## Locations

Just after the door that leads to Belfry Luna.
Just before the fog door that leads to Belfry Sol.

## Plot

He is the leader of Bell Keepers covenant, which the player can join by talking to him. He will also provide rewards upon ranking up in the covenant. He also warns the player not to touch the bell he's protecting, or his minion will chop them to pieces.

Because the Bell Keeper has little sanity, it is unclear how much his dialogue can be trusted, but it hints that there might in fact be two distinct Bell Keepers, one in Luna and one and Sol, as in Luna he will claim that "the Princess" made him "To guard the Bell of Alken! In Alken!" while, in Sol, he claims that "the Prince" made him, "To guard the Bell of Venn! In Venn!". Killing the Bell Keeper in either location leads to the absence of the Bell Keeper from the other, further hinting at a direct connection and possibly towards them being the same person.

It is likely that Venn once stood where Belfy Sol does; as Belfry Sol, which the Bell Keeper calls "Arken", is atop the Iron Keep, it is possible that the Prince was a member of the Old Iron King's family, perhaps the Old Iron King himself as he was in a loveless marriage to Mytha because he "had feelings for another". The original goal of the Bell Keepers might have been to somehow preserve the love affair between the Old Iron King and the Princess of Ven, or its memory.

It is unclear if the Bell Keeper Dark Spirit that can invade the player in Belfry Luna is the same as the Bell Keeper NPC; unlike the regular Bell Keeper enemies, who wear full helmets, the invader wears the same partial helmet as the NPC that lets most of his face apparent.
