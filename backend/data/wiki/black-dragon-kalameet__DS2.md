# Black Dragon Kalameet (DS2)

Although Kalameet was defeated and killed, a group of knights found his corpse and used his scales to create armor close to the appearance of the dragon, while they used his talons to craft shields and his tail to forge several legendary weapons. As the centuries passed, the Black Dragon was consigned to mere legend, according which he was defeated by a brave warrior in a magnificent battle and that it was his tail that was used to forge these legendary weapons. In the region of Drangleic the name of Kalameet has been long lost and, while the legend of the pale dragon is told in various locales even if each account is fragmentary, the Black Dragon is rarely mentioned; some even say that it is only an an embellishment adopted in later retellings of the story.

Regardless, the Dragon Knights, who wore armor forged from the scales of Kalameet and used weapons created from his tail, persisted, and at some point arrived in Drangleic and settled in the Dragon Shrine. The group of knights would also join the covenant of the Dragon Remnants, apostles who venerated the primordial creatures and desired the perpetuity of their existence, seeking their scales to rise above their limited human existence. This group used the Dragon Eyes to invade the space and time of other knights and challenged them to claim their dragon scales, rewarding the best among them with the stones infused with the powers of the dragons to try to achieve transformation, as well as with the ancient Black Dragon Greatsword forged from Kalameet's tail.
