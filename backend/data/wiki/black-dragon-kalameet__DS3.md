# Black Dragon Kalameet (DS3)

Although the
calamitous ring created with the eye of Kalameet had no useful power and was merely hazardous, the artifact was passed down for thousands of years as a symbol of dragon worship. Its most fervent adherents even became convinced that the task of preserving it had been bestowed upon them as a sacred duty.

Like many other artifacts and beings connected to the ancient dragons, the ring of Kalameet also ended up at Archdragon Peak, a place where the remnants of the men-serpents and the dragon worshippers had gathered to venerate the primordial beings and attempted to achieve their perfect shape using stones imbued with the power of everlasting dragons. The ring was thus kept in front of the statue of one of those who, in meditation, had managed to partially achieve the draconic transformation.

## Theories

It is possible to theorize that at some point Kalameet was corrupted by the Abyss of Manus, as his body shows signs of corruption with his scales that appear covered in a oily substance and his wings that appear consumed. In addition, his flames deal both magic and physical damage just like abyssal sorceries and pyromancies.
