# Black Hand Gotthard (DS3)

Black Hand Gotthard is a phantom in Dark Souls III.

## Locations

Farron Keep
Summonable as a white phantom for fighting against the Abyss Watchers.
Irithyll of the Boreal Valley
Summonable as a white phantom for fighting against Pontiff Sulyvahn.
Lothric Castle
His corpse can be found just before the entrance to the Grand Archives after defeating the first three Lords of Cinder (Abyss Watchers, Aldrich and Yhorm) and holds the Grand Archives Key.

## Lore

A hunter who served several kings of Lothric. He held the title of "Black Hand", a title that is only bestowed upon hunters that serve successive kings. To date, only three have obtained this title (Black Hand Kamui is one and the other is unknown).

He was able to obtain said title due to his expert swordsmanship. Eventually, due to the decaying state of the kingdom, he attempted to escape Lothric Castle, though he did not succeed. He wears the Black Hand Set, and wields the Gotthard Twinswords and a Composite Bow.
