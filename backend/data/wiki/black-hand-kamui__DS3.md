# Black Hand Kamui (DS3)

Black Hand Kamui is an enemy character in Dark Souls III.

## Location

Attacks in the large courtyard after exiting the rooftops section of the Grand Archives, together with Lion Knight Albert and Daughter of Crystal Kriemhild.

## Description

One of the three "Black Hands", together with Black Hand Gotthard and an unknown member. He wears the Black Hand Set and wields Onikiri and Ubadachi.

## Equipment

Onikiri and Ubadachi
Composite Bow (with Poison Arrows)
Poison Throwing Knives
Black Hand Set
