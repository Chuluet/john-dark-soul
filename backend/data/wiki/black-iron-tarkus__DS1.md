# Black Iron Tarkus (DS1)

Black Iron Tarkus, (黒鉄のタルカス Kurogane no Tarukasu) also referred to as Iron Tarkus, is a character and summon-able white phantom in Dark Souls. He can be summoned by the player in the fight against the Iron Golem boss in Sen's Fortress.

## Location

In a tower structure at the end of the walkway to the right from where the fog door to the Iron Golem is located. Like other summon signs, Tarkus's summon sign is only visible if the player has not put down a summon sign and is in human form. He aids the player in taking on the Iron Golem as a white phantom. He is notoriously tough and can serve as a distraction during the battle. In fact, Tarkus can defeat the boss all by himself in the first playthrough and stands a break-even chance of doing so on future playthroughs with minimal support from the player. A Black Iron Set is found on his corpse in Anor Londo, next to the entrance to the Painted World of Ariamis. A Painting Guardian stands next to his corpse, possibly removing it from the room since the body is so far away from the entrance.

## Lore

Black Iron Tarkus is the strongest of the Berenike Knights. Berenike Knights were known for their heavy armor of steel and their ultra-heavy Tower Shields forged with thick plates of iron, favoring hard defense and stability, as well as their gigantic swords capable of causing enormous damage. For their immense strength these knights were extolled as the mightiest of mighty. Tarkus was renowned even among his fellow knights for his great strength; he wore an ultra-heavy armor made of special black iron—one with exceptional fire protection, yet so unwieldy that only he was able to wear it; his greatshield, made of the same fire-resistant material, was even heavier than that of his fellow knights.

As the First Flame began to fade, countless among the ranks of the Berenike Knights became Undead and ventured to Lordran. Here they tried to make their way toward Anor Londo through the only possible access: Sen's Fortress. The knights of Berenike and Tarkus thus entered the dangerous proving ground; after decades of struggling to advance, much of the Knights ended up as Hollows, becoming a threat to the other Undead in the location. Tarkus, after a grueling and lengthy ordeal, eventually reached the peak of Sen's Fortress and challenged the last trial in the form of the Iron Golem. The mighty knight in black iron armor managed to defeat the guardian of Anor Londo, gaining access to the city of the gods. However, after reaching Anor Londo, Tarkus perished at the hands of the Painting Guardians before he could reach Gwynevere and obtain the Lordvessel. The few Berenike Knights that followed him to Anor Londo ended up inside the Painted World of Ariamis.

Many millennia past his demise, Knight Slayer Tsorig found the black iron armor and shield of the deceased Tarkus, and made them his own. The black set would gain notoriety as an omen of the feared Knight Slayer, a display of the terrible strength of one with the vigor to wear it.
