# Blighttown (DS1)

Blighttown is a location in Dark Souls.

## Adjacent locations

Depths
Quelaag's Domain
The Great Hollow
Valley of Drakes

## Description

Blighttown is a rather treacherous area with a high degree of verticality. It can be accessed through either the Depths through defeating the Gaping Dragon and acquiring the Blighttown Key, or the Valley of Drakes by navigating through Darkroot Basin from the Undead Parish.

The upper parts consist of interconnecting bridges and scaffolding, with plenty of places to fall to one's death if not careful. The bottom portion of the area is a poisonous swamp that will slow the player's movements.

Many enemies in the area can inflict poison, while Blowdart Snipers are able to deal Toxic from a great distance.

## Plot

Accessing Blighttown is necessary to ring the second Bell of Awakening, found past Chaos Witch Quelaag in Quelaag's Domain located at the very bottom of the area. Several NPCs visit Blighttown as part of their respective questlines, with Quelana of Izalith and Shiva of the East both acting as merchants.

## Bonfires

There are two bonfires in this area. Neither can be warped to with the Lordvessel.

The first bonfire is located close to the entrance from the Depths, on the bridge that acts as a structural support for the great stone wall.

The other bonfire is located just inside a tunnel accessed through the swamp at the bottom of Blighttown; it is nearest to the Valley of Drakes entrance. A Dragon Scale is located in a chest further into the tunnel.

## Quelaag's Domain

Even though Chaos Witch Quelaag is located in Quelaag's Domain, she is considered to be the boss of Blighttown as well; slaying her will prevent any future summoning and invading in Blighttown. The entrance to Quelaag's Domain can be found on the face of the large mound covered in spider webs in the swamp.

## Merchants

Quelana of Izalith will show up next to a pillar outside of Quelaag's Domain, selling pyromancies.The player must have a Pyromancy Flame that is upgraded to at least +10 in order for her to appear, though she will also appear if a player phantom with a sufficiently upgraded flame is present.
Shiva of the East, along with his bodyguard, will appear near the bottom of the elevator to Valley of Drakes. The player must be part of the Forest Hunter Covenant and have talked to him in Darkroot Garden for him to appear. This is the only area where he acts as a merchant.

## Phantoms

Maneater Mildred invades just outside the swamp bonfire. If defeated, she can be summoned as a white phantom in front of Quelaag's Fog Door.

## Other

Laurentius of the Great Swamp will hollow here if the player tells him how to acquire advanced pyromancies by answering "Yes" to his question in Firelink Shrine. He will be located in the swamp, straight across from the bonfire at the base of Blighttown.
Siegmeyer of Catarina will appear near the ramp leading to the entrance of The Great Hollow after speaking to him at Firelink Shrine. He needs to be assisted in both Sen's Fortress and Anor Londo in order to appear at Firelink.

## Strategies

In general, high poison resistance will serve the player greatly while navigating both the upper and lower parts; it buys more time while navigating the poison swamp before being poisoned and gives a better chance of avoiding becoming toxic from blowdarts.
Care should be taken when using weapons with attacks that move the player, such as curved swords, as the additional movement may lead to unintended falls.
There are multiple bridges in the upper area that will shift and move while the player walks on them. Caution should be used while on these.
The Rusted Iron Ring will negate the slowing effect of the poison sludge, allowing players to traverse the area considerably faster.
Standard rolls are not affected by the slowing effect and can be used to speed up travel through the swamp. However, the Dark Wood Grain Ring's special roll can only be used in slowing terrain if the Rusted Iron Ring is also worn.
If contracted, the swamp's Poison will deal 4 damage per second for 3 minutes.
Poison will not continue to build up once afflicted. As such, poisoned players can trek through the swamp without further penalty, aside from the reduced movement speed, until the poison wears off or is cured.
It is possible to access Blighttown immediately after arriving in Firelink Shrine if the Master Key is chosen as the starting gift, by going through the locked door between New Londo Ruins and Valley of Drakes that would normally require the Key to New Londo Ruins located within Blighttown itself.
