# Blue Sentinel Targray (general)

Blue Sentinel Targray is a character in Dark Souls II.

He's voiced by Daniel Flynn, who previously voiced Solaire of Astora, Vamos and the Giant Blacksmith in Dark Souls, and later Alfred in Bloodborne.

## Description

An apostle of the Blue Sentinels covenant, Targray demands a rigid discipline both from himself and fellow covenant members when acting as a guide (likely for Way of Blue covenant members) from the covenant. While devoting his life to his mission, his actions and words occasionally denote a narrow mind.

He will be dismissive of players who have not yet acquired a Token of Fidelity; this item can be earned in online play while participating as a white phantom or can be found along with a Pharros' Lockstone on a broken pillar in Huntsman's Copse, near the Bridge Approach bonfire.
