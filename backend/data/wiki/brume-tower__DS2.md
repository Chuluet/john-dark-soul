# Brume Tower (DS2)

Brume Tower is a location in Crown of the Old Iron King.

## Overview

The location is actually a number of large towers set within a volcanic wasteland setting. The towers are connected by large chains that can be walked across. The largest and central tower is a giant clockwork contraption that, once activated, sets a number of elevators in motion within the tower and surrounding area, allowing further access.

## Bonfires

Throne Floor: When entering the DLC, cross the massive chain to the tower. The bonfire will be on the rooftop at the end of the chain.
Upper Floor: Following the level path descend two consecutive ladders before entering a hallway with floor-to-ceiling bars on the left side. The bonfire is in a small room to the right at the end of the hallway.
Foyer: Past the outside area with the first Fume Sorcerers. The path splits with a fog wall leading back inside the tower on one side. Go through the fog wall and the bonfire is in the alcove along the tower's wall.
Lowermost Floor: At the bottom of the tower in the room around the Fume Knight arena. Go right at the fog gate and follow the ring around the arena and the bonfire will be in a room at the end.
The Smelter Throne: Take the elevators to the highest ring and past a gate that requires the Tower Key. After the Prowlers invasion the bonfire is up the stairs near the armor stand for the Memory of the Old Iron King.
