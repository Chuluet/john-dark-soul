# Cale the Cartographer (DS2)

Cale the Cartographer is a character in Dark Souls II. He is voiced by Rhys Swinburn.

## Location

Cale is first encountered in a mine-like area in the Forest of Fallen Giants. He will later relocate to the basement of the abandoned house in Majula after his dialogue is exhausted, The Last Giant is defeated, and the basement of the mansion has been cleared of enemies.

## Plot

After speaking to him several times, Cale will give the player the House Key, which unlocks the abandoned house in Majula, and allowing the player a place to stay.

If spoken to in Majula, Cale will tell the player that he is from Mirrah, and he will also recall that a murderer had escaped from prison there. Cale recalls that he met a man very similar to the murderer, and though he is unable to remember the man's name, his stammering implies it to be Creighton of Mirrah.

After lighting all the flames on the map he will give the player his armor, Cale's Set, as a reward. This will unlock the trophy/achievement "Curious Map."

## Questline

In order to complete Cale's questline, the player must first light all the fires on the map found in Majula's mansion. This can be achieved by satisfying the following requirements:
Defeat The Last Giant.
Defeat the Lost Sinner.
Defeat the Old Iron King.
Defeat The Duke's Dear Freja.
Defeat The Rotten.
Obtain the King's Ring.
Defeat the Throne Watcher and Defender.
Obtain the Ashen Mist Heart.

These conditions can be fulfilled in any order and once done, the player may then talk to Cale and receive his armor set as a reward.
