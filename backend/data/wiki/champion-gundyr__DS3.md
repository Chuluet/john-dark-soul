# Champion Gundyr (DS3)

Champion Gundyr, also known as the Belated Champion, is an optional boss in Dark Souls III.

## Location

Found in the Untended Graves, in the same amphitheater where his golem remnants was found in the Cemetery of Ash.

## Description

Champion Gundyr is a large warrior clad in heavy cast-iron armor and armed with a large iron halberd. He is found kneeling inside a circle of candles, guarding the entrance to dark Firelink Shrine.

## Lore

Gundyr was an ancient champion who was charged with linking the First Flame. For his duty he received an iron halberd which would never crumble, and was sent to the Firelink Shrine. However, here the king found himself restrained by a chain that blocked him in his path, and he fell into an abyss, getting lost in the folds of time. Gundyr drifted into a moment of time when the Firelink Shrine had no fire and ended up arriving "late" for the celebrations that awaited his fate. The Fire Keeper who was supposed to welcome her champion never met him and the ensuing tragic farce became a favorite tale of the masses of Lothric, a stain on the reputation of the realm.
thumb|Gundyr's unbreakable halberd, a symbol of his preordained duty.
It was not chance that bound him to this fate, however: The one who had charged him with his duty had also condemned him to eternal service from the beginning. Imprisoned, Gundyr was defeated by an unknown warrior and became sheath to a coiled sword. Transformed into an indestructible golem, he became the ritual test of strength for those who would attempt to link the First Flame after him. Over time an arena complete with stands was built around his remains, in which the now Iudex Gundyr would "judge" those who aspired to become Lords. 

When the First Flame once again began to fade and the previous Lords of Cinder refused to serve as kindling again, the Unkindled buried in the Cemetery of Ash were resurrected. The remains of Gundyr's golem became the judge of these potential champions trying to reach Firelink Shrine. However, after his lifetime of service, the fire that was burned into Gundyr by the Coiled Sword had died down, leaving him open to the pus of man overtaking his body. In his final battle, Iudex Gundyr's raging humanity would erupt in a violent outbreak of dark pus, driven by an untamable fury.

## Theories

It is possible to theorize that Gundyr was an ancient king of Lothric, or was given an armor modeled after that of a king of Lothric and thus served as their champion. This is because the story of his tragic failure to link the Flame became a legend among the local population and the kingdom of Lothric was deeply linked to the tradition of Firelinking, so much so that Gundyr's arena was located right next to the city and close to Firelink Shrine. Gundyr's failure and the resulting stain on the king's reputation would also explain the desperation of future rulers of the kingdom, which culminated in Oceiros's obsession with producing a Lord of Cinder of royal blood.
Considering that those who had deceived Gundyr had given him his halberd and charged him with linking the Flame, they must have had a position superior to the king of Lothric but still close to him. This could point to the involvement of Gwyndolin, king of the gods of Irithyll, who had strong ties to Lothric; or the Primordial Serpents, who were already advisors to the royal family. Using Gundyr as a golem to test the strength of future Champions would ensure that those overseeing the linking of the Flame would be guaranteed to find potential Lords with enough power to prolong the Age of Fire.

## Fight overview

The battle will commence once the player approaches him. Champion Gundyr fights in a similar manner to his present self, although he is far more aggressive, hits harder, and has a larger moveset that covers a wider area, leaving very little openings for the player to attack.

All moves he shares with Iudex have been enhanced in some way. For example, when he slams his halberd into the ground, he does this in an attack that consists of two stages, the first when he slams the weapon and the second when he raises it again, in another attack that has slightly more reach.

Two of the most noticeable attacks added to his repertoire are a round-back kick he performs if he detects the player is next to him after finishing a combo, and a shoulder bash that practically teleports him from any place within the arena to the player's position, knocking them back while dealing medium damage.

In his second phase, he will become even more aggressive and will start using some devastating combos and juggles. All of his attacks are then enhanced in one way or another and he can chain combos almost immediately, harassing the player without taking breaks while performing wide swings and punches. He also resorts to his back-round kicks and shoulder bashes more often and is able to reach the player's current position from all across the arena in just an instant.

However, perhaps his most dangerous enhancement is being able to perform counter-combos. He has a few powerful combos designed to trigger if he finishes one and detects the player is attempting to attack at that moment.

Gundyr can be parried and riposted, greatly trivializing an otherwise difficult fight.

## Summons

Sword Master: If the player has defeated him at the Firelink Shrine, the Sword Master can be summoned for this fight. His summon sign can be found at the entrance to the arena, to the right.
