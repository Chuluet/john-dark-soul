# Champion's Gravetender and Gravetender Greatwolf (DS3)

## Location

The boss fight is found in an optional location of the Painted World of Ariandel. The player must destroy the rickety bridge that leads to the chapel from the side of said chapel, which will then allow them to climb down using the bridge's remains, like in the Catacombs of Carthus. From there, they must descend using the large roots jutting out of the cliffside, fending off Farron Followers and avoiding the exploding arrows that are shot by the two Millwood Knights below. After reaching the bottom, one can find the Depths of the Painting bonfire by proceeding forward from the last root where one of the Millwood Knights stood. From there, the boss fight can be reached by turning right and dropping down into the flower field opposite from the side of the frozen lake with Frost Crabs. The Champion's Gravetender will be found beneath a building resembling Crossbreed Priscilla's old battle arena from Dark Souls.

The Gravetender Greatwolf can actually be found earlier on in two different locations. The first of them is at the end of a forested field full of wolves, found beyond the collapsing snow shelf. The second one is found as the player proceeds uphill past the ruined tower guarded by Millwood Knights.

## Plot

The Champion's Gravetender and Gravetender Greatwolf are an easy to miss optional encounter found within the Ashes of Ariandel DLC.

The fight starts off with just the Champion's Gravetender and three wolves. Once his health has been halved, he will call for the Greatwolf by performing the "By my sword" gesture, making the fight a lot more challenging. Defeating both enemies here allows the player to obtain the Champion's Bones. By burning them at the bonfire in Firelink Shrine, they'll be able to unlock the Undead Match, a type of PvP mode. Beating the boss also results in obtaining Valorheart, which could be the player's only reward if they've somehow found the Champion's Bones already by exploring The Ringed City DLC first.

Unlike the Gravetender, who remains in the arena at all times, the Greatwolf can be encountered earlier on as a powerful miniboss-like encounter, fighting alongside a rather numerous pack of smaller wolves. It has the same abilities as in the boss battle, but unlike in that fight, its health must not be depleated fully, as it will flee after less than half of it has been removed.

If the player defeats the Greatwolf in these two encounters, they will receive three Large Titanite Shard with each victory. This also has the added bonus of lowering its boss fight health by 10% with each time it is beaten.The effect will not reset if the player dies on their first attempt of the boss battle, meaning it is permanently weakened. Beating it with the Gravetender before defeating it in the other areas of Ariandel will make it disappear from those locations, meaning the Large Titanite Shards cannot be obtained any longer.

## Exploit

It is possible to cheese the fight against the boss: from the Depths of the Painting bonfire, approach the ledge around the arena, but do not hop down. Instead, equip the Black Bow of Pharis and the Hawk Ring, plus Feather Arrows. The bow paired with the ring and standard arrows doesn't work for the cheese, as they don't go far enough. Zoom in and there will be a wolf visible to the right, next to a column. Shoot at it to aggro it and the fog wall will appear. Step back at this point and cast poison Mist or Toxic Mist around the area where you just stood. Three wolves at the base of the short cliff will get poisoned and their diminishing health bars will appear visible behind the wall.

The Gravetender will walk up after a short time (his footprints will be visible through the fog). Once he is there, cast Poison or Toxic Mist again, until his health bar begins to shrink. Be aware that he can shoot sorceries through the fog and they can hit you. Once he is dead, in order to spawn the Greatwolf, you have to make your way all the way up to the chapel (warping via a bonfire resets the fight) and down into the basement with the Giant Flies until the howl of the Greatwolf is heard, then run all the way back to the fog wall of the boss fight, and repeat the cheese on the wolf as with the Gravetender. However, since the trip is long and arduous and the flies are particularly dangerous, you can just enter the fog after the man dies (the prompt appears when close to it) and deal with the wolf in normal combat. The wolf will appear near the building, as soon as the fog wall is entered.

## Lore

Long ago, an Undead declared a tournament in which their fellow cursed souls would battle to the repeated death, with their worth being measured in deaths. This was seen as a gift to the Undead, who go hollow without a purpose, and a fight with no end was considered by some to be a glorious use of their immortality.

The Champion of the Undead Match eventually went mad, ending up in the Painted World of Ariandel as a result. Only his page and a large wolf stayed by his side, until the day he finally died. The champion was buried in the lower section of the painting, where a small tombstone is still guarded by the very same page and wolf that had remained with him in his madness. They became guardians of that place, learning to utilize the frost that permeated that land as a way to uphold their task. The Greatwolf quickly established itself as the leader among the other wolves as well, commanding them and likely using them as guards of the tomb as well.

They will only be relieved of their duty by the Ashen One, who finds their way to the bottom of the painting and challenges the duo to partake in the old turnament of the champion. Should they not do that, however, the fate of the page and wolf remains a mystery. All that is known is that the old champion's remains would find their way in the possession of Amnesiac Lapp during the collapse of the world into the Dreg Heap.
