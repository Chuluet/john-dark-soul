# Chancellor Wellager (DS2)

Chancellor Wellager is a character and merchant in Dark Souls II.

He is voiced by Andrew Tiernan.

## Location

He is encountered at the entrance of Drangleic Castle, standing on the stairway to the throne room. He remains here for the entire game.

## Plot

Wellager appears as a ghostly, translucent figure, which makes him impervious to any damage, meaning he cannot be killed and his services will always be available to the player. He acts as a merchant, selling various goods, and will also offer exposition to the player, giving them a good idea of Drangleic's history. Players can also learn the "This one's me" gesture from him upon going through his introduction speech.

After the Looking Glass Knight is defeated, he will begin selling new weapon variants that came included with the pre-ordered Black Armor edition of the game. Once NG++ is reached, Wellager will also sell all Covenant rewards of the Blue Sentinels and Brotherhood of Blood, allowing those who do not wish to grind the PVP-oriented covenants with no offline grinding method a way to still obtain that equipment. Note that increasing the bonfire intensity with Bonfire Ascetics won't work, and a proper NG+ playthrough must be initiated.

If the Giant Lord is defeated in the Memory of Jeigh, speaking to Wellager will have him gift the player with the Royal Dirk and the Espada Ropera, as well as the full Llewellyn Set in the Scholar of the First Sin edition of the game.

## Lore

Chancellor Wellager was one of Vendrick's most important servants, having been present for all of his greatest accomplishments; the founding of the kingdom of Drangleic, the arrival of his queen Nashandra, the construction of the castle and the invasion of the land of Giants, which resulted in the creation of the Golems. This led to a period of peace that the advisor described to be akin to the Dark, a hint to the queen's true nature.

His king had once ordered him to enlist the great blacksmith Llewellyn from the land of Mirrah with a lifetime contract, so that the kingdom of Drangleic could have access of the rare geisteel from the eastern land, which had been secret until then. Wellager would succeed in this, even being gifted the Espada Ropera by the blacksmith. His exploits would strengthen the kingdom greatly.

However, the Giants would return with a vengance, and shortly after the king would vanish too. At some point after that, the chancellor would meet his end and linger on as a ghost, seemingly stuck in time and unable to move on, completely unaware of his current predicament.
