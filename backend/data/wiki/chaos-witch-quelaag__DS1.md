# Chaos Witch Quelaag (DS1)

Chaos Witch Quelaag (混沌の魔女クラーグ) is a boss in Dark Souls.

## Description

Quelaag is a daughter of the Witch of Izalith and, along with most of her family, failed to escape from the chaotic flame and was corrupted by it. Due to exposure to the flame, Quelaag and her sister mutated into spider-like creatures with their upper body being fused to the monsters' backs.

## Lore

She is the Fair Lady's older sister. When their mother, the Witch of Izalith along with their other two sisters became the Bed of Chaos, she and her sister became half demon spiders.

Quelaag and her sister stationed just outside the Demon Ruins, in a fraction of Blighttown that became known as Quelaag's Domain. The two started to breed demon insects to enlarge the demon army, by offering Humanities to the Fair Lady. People in Blighttown venerated the two witches, and gladly accepted to become hosts of the Fair Lady's eggs.

When the people were stricken by a blight, Quelaag's sister chose to absorb their pus to save their lives, which greatly weakened and blinded her while making any further eggs incapable of holding life. Quelaag remained by her side as her guardian and a hunter of undead to gather the humanity necessary to sustain her sister as a Fire Keeper.

When the Chosen Undead entered Quelaag's Domain, the Chaos Witch looked at them with a gleeful smile at the promise of the plentiful  Humanities she would obtain from them.

## Location

Found in Quelaag's Domain in between Blighttown and Demon Ruins.
