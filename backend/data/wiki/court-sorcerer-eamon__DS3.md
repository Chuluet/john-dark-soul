# Court Sorcerer Eamon (DS3)

Court Sorcerer Eamon is an enemy character in Dark Souls III.

## Description

Eamon is the last-remaining Court Sorcerer of the Profaned Capital, a group of spell-casters who once claimed heirship to the legendary Big Hat Logan, even copying the staff he used to emulate him better. Despite these grand claims, however, their legitimacy was dubious at best.

In battle, Eamon is an aggressive spellcaster armed with a great bludgeoning weapon. He constantly switches between casting powerful sorceries and melee combat throughout the fight.

## Location

Found in the Profaned Capital. Awaits on the roof of the ruined chapel in the toxic swamp.
