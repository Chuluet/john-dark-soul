# Crestfallen Merchant (general)

The Crestfallen Merchant is a character in Dark Souls. A pessimistic merchant, he peddles goods at Sen's Fortress. He is voiced by Andy Gathergood, who also voices Laurentius of the Great Swamp, and Felkin the Outcast in Dark Souls II.
__TOC__

## Location

Found in the later half of Sen's Fortress. On the narrow walkway opposite the Giant depositing boulders, there is a gap. The Crestfallen Merchant is located in the structure after jumping over this gap.

## Lore

As a pessimistic and guilt ridden merchant, he will often encourage and praise the players efforts while condemning them as futile. His erratic implications can be taken as signs that he's well into becoming Hollowed himself.
