# Crestfallen Saulden (general)

is a character in Dark Souls II.

He is voiced by Matt Morgan, who also voiced the Crestfallen Warrior in Demon's Souls, the other Crestfallen Warrior and Domhnall of Zena in Dark Souls, as well as Sorcerer Thops in Elden Ring.

## Location

He is encountered in Majula, sitting at the foot of its tall, obelix-like monument found atop a cliff just north of the village's bonfire. He remains there for the entire game.

## Plot

Saulden is a spiritual successor of the Crestfallen Warrior from the previous game and Demon's Souls. While he is still as depressed and hopeless as his predecessors, he seems to have a much friendlier personality, offering helpful advice to the player without belittling them. He is quite helpful for first-time players, as he reveals information about gameplay mechanics such as messages and summon signs, information about Drangleic and directions for where to go. Players can also learn the "Welcome" gesture from him upon going through his introduction speech.

Saulden invites the player to join the Way of Blue covenant, so that the Blue Sentinels can come to their aid whenever they are being targeted by Dark Spirits. Upon following him up on his offer, players will recieve the Blue Seal as well as the "Covenant of the Meek" Achievement. Furthermore, Saulden will reward players who join the covenant when they rank up by vanquishing invaders, be they players or hostile NPCs.

Upon dying one hundred times, Saulden will gift the player with a Ring of Steel Protection to help them on their journey. He will also gift the players with a Soul Vessel should they finish his small questline, which involves rounding up four different characters from around the kingdom. Doing so will give the broken knight some much-needed comfort. This side quest will also award the player with the "Gathering of Exiles" Achievement.

## Lore

Saulden is a cursed Undead, much like the Bearer of the Curse and many who have found their way in Drangleic. Like them, he was expected to go on a perilous journey to find a way to end the Curse, but lost hope along the way, his spirit crushed by the trials of the ruined kingdom. As a result, he sits at the foot of the great monument of Majula, deep in contemplation, and he offers guidance to those who find their way to the seaside hamlet. He thinks it's hopeless to look for a cure for the Curse, since it originates from life and the soul itself.

Despite his pessimistic outlook on life and existence, he still has much faith in the Way of Blue, a humble religion spread among those who seek help. He seems to have been a follower even before his loss of will, since the Emerald Herald describes him as a "blue knight". He will let the Bearer of the Curse join the covenant if they ever feel overwhelmed and in need of assistance.

The Bearer of the Curse can eventually lead more people to Majula, which will lighten Saulden's mood and make him remember better times. Finding new peace in companionship, he'll feel gratitude towards the adventurer.

## Comic Appearance

Saulden appears as a central figure in the comic Dark Souls II: Into the Light.thumb|Saulden in Dark Souls II: Into the Light.A lone warrior bearing a curse meets Saulden when the latter is resting at a bonfire. Saulden decides to invite the warrior to sit around the fire for a while. After a brief conversation, the undead warrior decides to continue his quest, although Saulden tries to convince him to stay for a little longer in order to share some of his knowledge with him. The warrior, however, refuses his offer, deeming that his sword "belongs to a crestfallen man who hides by his fire".

Soon, the same warrior returns, bloodied and dying. Using his blood, he desperately writes a summon sign into the ground before dying, his corpse fading away. Moments later, the warrior is reincarnated near the bonfire. Saulden then asks if the warrior will listen to his advice this time. The warrior replies with "I will."
