# Crystal Sage (DS3)

The Crystal Sage is a boss and later a miniboss in Dark Souls III.

## Locations

Road of Sacrifices
Found in an exterior court located at the end of the ruined keep that connects the marshlands with the cathedral.
Grand Archives (miniboss)
Materializes and starts attacking as soon as the player climbs up the first set of stairs after opening the archives' gates. After sustaining enough damage, the sage will vanish and reappear elsewhere in the building, where they will continue their attack. The locations where they can teleport to are:
At the first end wall as one enters the archives.
On the third floor, at the middle of the corridor that is above the entrance.
On the third floor, at the middle of the wooden bridge that connects to the shortcut lift and the stairs to the fourth floor.

## Description

Both Sages are towering figures that have billowing, long robes that obscure most of their bodies. True to their pedigree, they wear enormous hats that obscure their faces, though they also wear bone masks that look like beaks, with glass-eyed lenses. Instead of sorcery staves, they utilize crystal spheres as catalysts (likely used similarly to crystal balls). The Legion's Sage uses a floral lavender sphere, while the Archives' Sage uses a bluish white sphere. They also wield rapiers for defense.

## Lore

The two scholars are spiritual successors of the legendary grand sage Logan, wearing hats similar to that of the legendary sorcerer as a symbol of their pedigree. They possessed a scroll containing the crystal sorceries of Seath, a branch of sorcery recorded by Logan and carried on by the two sages. Using the research of the Big Hat, they could replicate these sorceries which utilized the full potential of crystals as a medium for souls.

At some point the Crystal Sages reached Lothric and became the spiritual guides of the scholars of the Grand Archives. The Crystal Sages introduced to Lothric not only crystal gems but also the heretical belief in the pale dragon Seath and his mad knowledge. The sages would take Daughter of Crystal Kriemhild as their pupil as well as craft crystal weapons and casting tools, whose special properties were the fruits of their lifetime of research.

Eventually, in a pact of uncertain terms, one of the Sages joined the Undead Legion of Farron to teach them sorceries. As the Legion believed in speed over power, the Crystal Sage created spells that were practical and could be used relatively faster than other sorceries, but required and encouraged the development of specific talent. The daughter of the Legion's lead acolyte, a Xanthous scholar named Yellowfinger Heysel, appears to have had some of her own sorceries tailored in such a way.

## Summons

Eygon of Carim: If the player speaks to Eygon in the Undead Settlement after rescuing Irina of Carim, he can be summoned for this fight. In the pillar chamber leading to the arena, his summon sign can be found behind the third pillar on the left.
