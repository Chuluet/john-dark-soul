# Dark Lord (DS1)

The Dark Lord, also known as the Lord of Men, is a character/position in the Dark Souls trilogy.

## Lore

This section of the lore lacks sources and is in need of further expansions and explanations

The Dark Lords are beings who were meant to become Lords of Cinder, but refused to link the First Flame and extend the Age of Fire, with the specific intent of bringing forth the Age of Dark (also referred to as an "Age of Man"). Dark Lords, while powerful enough to overcome the trials involved in linking the Flame, apparently don't have to inherit a primordial force (such as the First Flame) in order to be deemed worthy of their title. For instance, if the Chosen Undead chooses to become a Dark Lord, abandoning the power of the First Flame, several primordial serpents will nevertheless bow before their new Lord (including both Kaathe and Frampt), even as the Chosen Undead lacks the extraordinary power granted by the Flame.

Contrary to what the title itself might convey, the Dark Lord is not necessarily a malevolent force. Rather, it merely refers to the Dark Soul found within all Humans, an antithesis to the First Flame and the sunlight of Gwyn. According to Primordial Serpent Kaathe, the Dark Lord is prophesied to bring down the reign of the Anor Londo gods and their "unnatural" world order, liberating Humans from the oppression of the Darksign and the curse of the Undead.

The interpretation of the Dark Lord's role in founding a new world order seems to have varied over time. The "Dark Lord" ending of Dark Souls I entails abandoning the First Flame to die out and beginning a new age from naught; the "Usurpation of Fire" ending of Dark Souls III however is somewhat different. Many millennia past the time of the Chosen Undead, the Sable Church of Londor, founded by three sisters who were disciples of Kaathe, are searching for a worthy Lord of Men. If the Ashen One cooperates with Yuria, a sister of the Sable Church, they are asked to "usurp the Flame", to "wrest it from its mantle". In doing so, the Ashen One would inherit what little lifeforce remains of the First Flame, and thus become a worthy Lord of Men—that is, a "Lord of Hollows", as it has come to be referred to. By this time, the notion that Hollows were the "true face of man" had become more accepted, propagated especially by the Pilgrims of Londor, melding the two titles of Dark Lord into one.
