# Dark Sun Gwyndolin (DS1)

Dark Sun Gwyndolin is a character and optional boss in Dark Souls.

Found in Anor Londo behind a statue that reveals a hidden path if the Chosen Undead is wearing the Darkmoon Seance Ring, he can be talked to, unseen, through a fog wall, acting as leader of the Blades of the Darkmoon covenant. Passing through the fog wall will turn him hostile and trigger his boss battle.

## Location

Dark Sun Gwyndolin guards the honorary tomb of Gwyn, Lord of Sunlight in Anor Londo. The tomb can be found by using the spiraling staircase on the rotating bridge while the bridge is in its lowest position and descending to the second accessible bonfire. If the player wears the Darkmoon Seance Ring in this location, Gwyn's statue will vanish and Gwyndolin's fog door will be revealed. The player can then either pledge allegiance to him and his Blades of the Darkmoon or dare to trespass the fog door behind which he dwells, thus commencing the boss battle.

If the player does not yet have the Darkmoon Seance Ring, they can kill Gwynevere instead, which at that moment will be revealed to be just an illusion set up by Gwyndolin himself. The entrance to Gwyndolin's boss room will be accessible at the same aforementioned location. Be advised that killing Gwynevere will cause players to respawn at the first Bonfire in Anor Londo if dying or using Homeward/Dark Sign in Anor Londo regardless of the last Bonfire rested at. This will last until Gwyndolin is dead.

## Description

Gwyndolin appears as a white humanoid figure with the lower half snakes for legs instead of feet, he wears a traditional white robe and wields a gold staff.

## Plot

Gwyndolin initiates players into the Blade of the Darkmoon covenant. He accepts Souvenirs of Reprisal in exchange for increasing the player's rank in the covenant.

Gwyndolin is the lastborn child of Gwyn, Lord of Sunlight and one of the deities who resided in Anor Londo during the Age of Fire. He was worshipped as the "God of the Darkmoon" and held the title of "God of the Dark Sun".

Despite being the son of Gwyn, Gwyndolin was born with a strong moon power, and "weak and repulsive" in appearance, particularly due to the snakes coiled around his lower half. Because of his darkmoon powers and his strong affinity for moon magic, as well as his lineage, Gwyndolin was raised as a female. While still young, Gwyn made him wear women's clothing and gave him a magic ring which would allow Gwyndolin to move and act in a feminine way, leading him to behave "like a dark and fleeting goddess".

Despite his harsh upbringing, and having accepted his identity in the eyes of the other deities as fragile and repulsive, Gwyndolin would grow up with a deep admiration for his father and an aspiration to become like him, wearing a crown with the design of the sun that was imbued with his dark moon magic. He accepted his place and role to the point of supporting his father's will even after his death, and he took part in the workings of his kingdom. Although he was a deity of Anor Londo, Gwyndolin still grew into a powerful sorcerer, using a golden bow, infused with strong magic and employing arrows of moonlight, and even a catalyst for sorcery that channeled not the intelligence but the faith of the user. Gwyndolin also became an eminent figure in Oolacile, where multiple statues of the deity were erected throughout the city. He was depicted with his moonlight robes and the catalyst of the country, implying that he had studied Oolacile sorcery and learned to use their catalysts. At some point, an immense arena was erected in Oolacile in which a ritual combat called the "Battle of Stoicism" took place. The victorious warriors were welcomed to Anor Londo. Curiously, the structure where the fighting took place was covered in those same Gwyndolin statues.

After his father Gwyn linked the First Flame, his throne passed to his firstborn son who, treacherously, chose to "ally" with the ancient dragons, the ancient enemies of the gods, and was therefore dethroned, exiled and his name eradicated from history. Following these events, his sister Gwynevere, Princess of Sunlight abandoned Anor Londo together with nearly all of the other deities, leaving Gwyndolin as the last remaining deity in the city. Despite being the last son of the royal family of the gods, Gwyndolin was psychologically unfit to become the next ruler, still playing the feminine role imposed on him by his father Gwyn, leaving Lloyd to take advantage of the situation who proclaimed himself king of the gods, justified by his being Gwyn's uncle, and become the chief deity of the Way of White.

Gwyndolin created an illusory image of his sister Gwynevere, to govern through her cherished figure and covering for her departure, taking upon himself to lead what remained of his father's kingdom and the Silver Knights through this illusion, though he didn't attempt to claim the throne for himself. Gwyndolin also collaborated with Kingseeker Frampt to maintain the system and religion created by his father to keep the First Flame and the Age of Fire alive. Together they used the illusion of Gwynevere to seek a worthy champion to link the Flame again, creating a prophecy which they hoped to make self-fulfilling by guiding and manipulating those who passed their various challenges and earned the Lordvessel, a component required to access the Kiln of the First Flame, while also maintaining some semblance of divinity and control over the ancient city.

Meanwhile Gwyndolin did not remain passive and founded the Blade of the Darkmoon covenant, welcoming into his service those knights who shouldered the duty of hunting down and punishing those who sinned against the gods, forming a corps that instilled respect for as well as fear of gods among humans. For their mission these knights were granted the Blue Eye Orbs which enabled them to invade the time and place of the sinner and mete out the vengeance of the gods. For this eternal mandate, Gwyndolin collaborated with Velka, Goddess of Sin, who determined the sin and marked the sinners on her Book of the Guilty so that one day they would face the wrath of the Blades of the Darkmoon. Gwyndolin's followers would cut off an ear from the corpse of the sinners they vanquished, bringing them to their god as proof of their task accomplished, receiving Gwyndolin's ring and talisman in return, and even learning of his miracle, creating a sword with the rays of the Darkmoon to manifest the vengeance of their god.

Gwyndolin thus continued to remain hidden in his father's empty mausoleum, administering Anor Londo from the shadows and continuing to carry out his father's will to uphold the Age of Fire, punishing sinners and leading the Undead without revealing himself. At some point, he was visited by an Undead woman. The woman entered his service as a Blade of the Darkmoon and became the sole Fire Keeper in Anor Londo, although whether she was already a Keeper or became one upon entering his service is unclear. He gifted this "Darkmoon Knightess" with a golden armor set to hide her own "hideous form", as well as his Darkmoon special talisman and Darkmoon Blade miracle. The Knightess assisted in Gwyndolin's attempt of fulfilling his father's prophecy, offering Undead who reached Anor Londo her kindled bonfire, and some guidance, first towards the illusion of Gwynevere, or, if they have already obtained the Lordvessel from the illusion, with information on Seath the Scaleless, the nearest of the powerful beings the prophecy would have the Undead slay to feed the Lordvessel. The Knightess helped maintain the facade of Gwynevere, talking about the illusion as if it was the real Goddess even as she knew it was not real. Should an Undead prove a threat to the Gods by attacking the illusion or Gwyndolin, or passing through the later's Fog Door and witness his true form, the Knightess would attempt to slay the heretic Undead.
Two more Blades of the Darkmoon assisted in Gwyndolin's quest, a man wielding a greatsword and another wielding a straight sword, both of whom would try to slay any Undead who would shatter Gwyndolin's illusions.

## Strategies

Gwyndolin's boss battle takes place in a seemingly never-ending corridor.

He is not a very difficult boss if the player has decent dodging skills. Of his three attacks, two can be avoided by hiding behind pillars, while the last one is more powerful and fast (he will tend to use the Sphere Shot after Orb Spread if the player is hiding behind pillars). The main issue in this fight is reaching him, as he will teleport away when the player approaches (equipping the Ring of Fog may increase the time available to attack before he teleports), meaning that the best time to run up to him is when he is just finishing off his Arrow Spread, as he takes a few moments to recover. Gwyndolin can still be damaged while he is in his teleporting animation and for a split second after actually moving, so it is possible to deal considerable damage at that moment.

Both the Orb and Arrow Spread attacks are avoidable by hiding behind one of the pillars on either side of the corridor. The Sphere Shot, on the other hand, will go through walls, inflicting full damage if not blocked or avoided. The Orb Spread and Sphere Shot are Magic-based, though, so a shield that can block 100% physical damage and has good Magic resistance, like the Crest Shield, is recommended for this fight.

While devastating, both the Sphere Shot and Arrow Spread attacks have no tracking whatsoever and are easily avoided simply by running diagonally. If a player does not want to risk it too much, a good strategy to catch up to him is by moving from pillar to pillar until they are about two pillars away from Gwyndolin and then wait until he shoots his Arrow Spread. This attack can consist of two-to-four arrow barrages. Wait until he finishes the second one and go for him, if he shoots any subsequent arrows, dodge them by running diagonally.

Alternatively, once Gwyndolin teleports the first time upon entering the tomb, it is possible to move closer to him to a point just outside his aggro range; then, by equipping the Hawk Ring and using the Dragonslayer Greatbow, the player can easily defeat him from range without him retaliating.

## Arrow Spread

Attack TypeParryableTrackingSpeed266000ThrustNoBothFast

Gwyndolin will slightly raise up and shoot multiple arrows at the Player. Blockable as they do not use much stamina. Alternatively, the Player can stand behind the pillars so that the arrows hit the pillar instead. From New Game+++, if all the arrows hit a non-blocking player, death is certain.

## Orb Spread

Attack TypeParryableTrackingSpeed040500MagicNoBothSlow

Gwyndolin will charge up his catalyst and shoot a cluster of homing blue orbs at the Player. Deals heavy damage if they connect. Recommended action is to hide behind the pillars as the orbs cannot go through them, or roll under it.

## Sphere Shot

Attack TypeParryableTrackingSpeed045900MagicNoBothFast

Gwyndolin will charge up his catalyst and shoot out a huge magic sphere which travels at great speed. Possible to dodge by rolling to the sides or directly under it or blocking with a good magic shield. The sphere can pass through the pillars, so hiding behind them is not recommended.
