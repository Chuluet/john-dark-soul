# Dark Sun Gwyndolin (DS2)

Although one day a Chosen Undead managed to link the First Flame, ensuring that the Age of Fire and the rule of the gods continued, at some point remnants of the late inhabitants of Anor Londo left Lordran and moved to Drangleic, founding the civilization of Heide. In fact, Heide's Knights still possessed the Cracked Blue Eye Orbs, the worn remains of the Blue Eye Orbs that were granted to the Blades of the Darkmoon who served Gwyndolin to mete out the vengeance of the gods at the times of Anor Londo, implying that the original founders of Heide were once servants of Gwyndolin and part of his covenant.
thumb|The Cathedral of Blue, home of the Blue Sentinels, spiritual successors to Gynwolin's Blades. 
In Heide also originated the Way of Blue, which was not a developed religion but a humble prayer that spread naturally among those seeking help, but which eventually grew to the point that a huge Cathedral of Blue was built in the heart of the city. In a similar way to the role played by the Blades of the Darkmoon, the Blue Sentinels were formed, a group of knights tasked with defending the apostles of Blue from evil spirits by travelling through space and time thanks to the Cracked Blue Eye Orbs. Although this order continued to follow the ideals of their ancestors and protect the apostles of the Way of Blue even after the fall of Heide, knowledge of Anor Londo's deities and even their names was lost and their faith evolved towards seeking a higher plane through good deeds.

However, the Blue Sentinels continued to exist past the falls of both Heide and Drangleic, with the dutiful Targray still standing watch at the Cathedral of Blue, and providing those worthy with Cracked Blue Eye Orbs and a lightning miracle similar to those of the Gods of Anor Londo, while in the town of Majula, which acts as an entry point and home base to new Undead in Drangleic, a crestfallen warrior, Saulden, introduced newcomers to the Way of Blue, ensuring that they received the Sentinels' assistance should they wish for it. Fittingly, Targray was guarded by an old Dragonslayer acting as a trial to would-be Sentinels as Dragon Slayer Ornstein once acted as trial for Undead in Anor Londo; this Dragonslayer donned a black version of Ornstein's armor, as well as an identical spear and ring.
