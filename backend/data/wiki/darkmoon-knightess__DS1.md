# Darkmoon Knightess (DS1)

The Darkmoon Knightess, also known as the Darkmoon Knight, the Lady of the Darkling or the Anor Londo Fire Keeper, is a character in Dark Souls.

Found at the first bonfire in Anor Londo, she is a Fire Keeper who will turn hostile if the player shows themselves a threat to the Gods.

She is voiced by Jenny Funnel, who also voiced Quelana of Izalith in the same game, Yuria and Selen Vinland in Demon's Souls, Annalise and Iosefa in Bloodborne, and Karla and the Old Woman of Londor in Dark Souls III.

At the first bonfire in Anor Londo. After arriving from Sen's Fortress, go down the stairs and head left.

## Plot

The Darkmoon Knightess is one of Dark Sun Gwyndolin's loyal followers and the fire keeper of the first bonfire in Anor Londo. As with the other fire keepers, the Darkmoon Knightess can upgrade Estus Flasks using Fire Keeper Souls. Like all other fire keepers, killing the Darkmoon Knightess will cause the fire she watches over to be extinguished, although players can still warp to it after obtaining the Lordvessel.

Should the player kill Gwynevere, the Darkmoon Knightess will appear directly in front of the elevator of Anor Londo to offer punishment for the deed. Should the player attack Dark Sun Gwyndolin, the Darkmoon Knightess will also become hostile and reside in her usual location, the first Anor Londo bonfire. Her armor, the Brass Set can be found in the room with the sunlight blade miracle after defeating Dark Sun Gwyndolin.

She is a member of the Blade of the Darkmoon and will have new dialogue with members of her covenant.

## Lore

The Darkmoon Knightess is possibly from Carim, due to the fact that she uses a Parrying Dagger. After becoming undead, she traveled to the Mausoleum of the Spiral Depths, where she met Dark Sun Gwyndolin and became a Blade of the Darkmoon. The Brass Set was given to her to hide her ghastly form and to hunt the guilty, as she assumes the role of Fire Keeper.
