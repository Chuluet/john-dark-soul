# Darkroot Basin (DS1)

right|400pxDarkroot Basin is a location in Dark Souls.

## Description

The Darkroot Basin is located right after the Darkroot Garden. It can be accessed via the Undead Burg, the Valley of the Drakes, or by the Darkroot Garden. It can also be accessed from the Darkroot Forest ladder.

## Connections
