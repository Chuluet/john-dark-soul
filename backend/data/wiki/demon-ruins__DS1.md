# Demon Ruins (DS1)

right|350px
The Demon Ruins is a location in Dark Souls.

## Location

Found shortly after Quelaag's Domain. The Demon Ruins lead to Lost Izalith; however, Lost Izalith cannot be accessed until the player has retrieved the Lordvessel from Anor Londo and placed it in the Firelink Altar.

On arrival, most of the area is flooded with lava until the Ceaseless Discharge is slain. Once the lava is drained, the lower section of the area may be accessed, and it is here that the route to Lost Izalith lies, initially blocked by the Lordvessel-related orange fog gate. 

The area just after the Demon Firesage has an elevator that will transport the player back up to the Bell of Awakening in Queelag's Domain.

## Bonfires

There are 3 bonfires in this area. None can be warped to using the Lordvessel; that duty falls on the bonfire in Quelaag's Domain, which offers convenient access to two of those below.
The first is just beyond Quelaag's Domain and the shortcut elevator, surrounded by Egg Burdened. Overlooks the entire upper section of the ruins.
The second is below the group of Capra Demons on the ruined wall, next to a non-respawning Burrowing Rockworm. Overlooks the entire lower section, and can be easily accessed by dropping off a ledge, skipping the gaggle of Capra Demons entirely. This bonfire only appears if you have killed Ceaseless Discharge.
The third is after the Demon Firesage boss fight and just before the Centipede Demon boss battle, very close to the shortcut elevator's bottom exit.

## Characters

Kirk, Knight of Thorns will invade the player here as a red phantom.
