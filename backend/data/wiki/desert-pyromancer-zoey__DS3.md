# Desert Pyromancer Zoey (DS3)

Desert Pyromancer Zoey is an enemy character in the Dark Souls III: The Ringed City DLC.

## Location

She is encountered in the Dreg Heap, in a tunnel above the large poison swampland of the Earthen Peak Ruins which can be reached via a large root.

## Plot

Zoey will be encountered by the player as they make their way through the remains of the Earthen Peak. She and a group of Thralls who have sworn fealty to her guard the path forward, so dealing with her is almost obligatory. Defeating her will grant her signature pyromancy Flame Fan.

## Lore

Zoey is a descendant of the Desert Pyromancers, a group that once resided in the Earthen Peak when it still stood. They hailed from the old land of Jugo, though nothing of it remains in the era of the Dreg Heap, much like all the other countries of the world of men. These pyromancers, most of whom were women, appeared alluring at a glance and used beauty to hide their deadlier side.

Their descendant Zoey, the last of them around, inherited only their true beauty, possessing a more earnest disposition and none of their hidden venom. This made her a beloved figure to those who were feeble, like the forsaken slaves of the fallen kingdom of Lothric. Thus they swore themselves to her and elevated her as a queen of sorts, ruling over the mire of her ruined home.

In spite of that good nature, she appears to be mistrustful of outsiders, which puts her at odds with those seeking the Ringed City. The Ashen One was among such adventurers, being forced to engage the queen of the feeble ones in battle and vanquishing even the sole beacon of hope she represented.
