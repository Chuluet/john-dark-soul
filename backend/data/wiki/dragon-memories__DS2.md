# Dragon Memories (DS2)

right|350px
The Dragon Memories is a location in Dark Souls II.

## Location

After obtaining the Ashen Mist Heart from the Ancient Dragon in the Dragon Shrine, go back to the Duke's Dear Freja boss room and you will find a crystal lying right below the dragon hanging from the roof. Interacting with it will cause you to access a dragon memory.

The player will find themselves in a grey, foggy place. Explore a bit and you will find the corpse of what seems to be an Everlasting Dragon, get close to it and you will obtain the Ancient Dragon Soul.
