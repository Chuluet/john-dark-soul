# Dragonslayer Armour (DS3)

The Dragonslayer Armour is a boss in Dark Souls III.

## Location

Found on the highest level of Lothric Castle, along the path that leads to the Grand Archives.

## Description

A suit of armor that belonged to a dragonslayer from times long past. It is controlled by the Pilgrim Butterflies that soar high above Lothric Castle and guard the entrance to the Grand Archives. It wields a gigantic shield and a greataxe infused with lightning.

## Lore

The Dragonslayer Armour is what remains of an ancient warrior of Lothric. During the founding of the kingdom, the soldiers of Lothric had found themselves facing large numbers of wyverns and had to rise to the challenge, ascending common knighthood. And so these armors of Lothric's first knights were crafted: heavy masses of iron capable of withstanding dragon fire, similar in design to the ancient armor of the dragonslayers of Anor Londo, with their shields capable of absorbing the lightning breath of wyverns. 

The old knights of Lothric also turned to the Firstborn of Lord Gwyn for aid, once venerated as a god of war and the sun, who in turn gave them the tools to face the wyverns. From the sun god they would learn how to infuse their weapons with the power of lightning, lethal against dragons, and the ancient techniques for hunting archdragons developed in the war between the gods and the Everlasting Dragons. They also learned of his miracles on manipulating lightning as a weapon. 

The army of Lothric successfully hunted down the wyverns in their region; moreover, by observing how their god commanded such creatures, they even learned how to tame and ride them, and wyverns became a symbol of Lothric alongside its knights. With the wyverns dead or domesticated, the dragonslayers were rendered obsolete, and their armour was retired in favor of lighter, more conventional knight armor. The armour of an ancient Dragonslayer, however, remained in the barracks of the Lothric army, on the bridge between the Altar of Sunlight and the Grand Archives, a monument to the legendary past of Lothric knights. 

In the last days of Lothric, as the First Flame was almost faded, flocks of Pilgrim Butterflies would manifest in the Lothric skies. These gigantic humanoid insects with immense, branch-like wings would infest the skies of the dying kingdom, migrating towards the tip of Lothric Castle. Here they would take control of the statue of a Dragonslayer Armour through dark magic, using it as a puppet to eliminate anyone who attempted to proceed to the top of the castle.

## Theories

It is possible to theorize that the Dragonslayer Armor is the armor of one of Lothric's early kings from the kingdom's dragonslaying past. This is because his immense armor was preserved for centuries despite its model no longer being in use and placed in the center of a small bridge as a monument. Furthermore, the armor is located between the army barracks with the temple of Lord Gwyn's Firstborn and the last section of Lothric Castle where the royals resided, implying cultural value for the history of knights and worship in the heir of the Sun but also for the royals of the kingdom, acting as a literal and metaphorical bridge between the two.
