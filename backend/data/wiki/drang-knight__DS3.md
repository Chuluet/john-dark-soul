# Drang Knight (DS3)

The Drang Knights are a pair of enemy characters in Dark Souls III.

## Location

Irithyll of the Boreal Valley — Both knights await near the staircase at the far end of the big courtyard scattered with dead Giant Slaves located after the Pontiff Sulyvahn bonfire.

## Description

Two skillful knights clad in the Drang Set, one wielding paired hammers, and the other twinspears. They can spot the player from a very long distance and will rush to attack at the same time.

Using paired weapons gives them the ability to unleash many successive hits and stunlock the player with ease, potentially dealing high amounts of damage. They have very good tracking (most notorious with the knight wielding twinspears), which allows them to hit the player even at a considerable distance.

They will generally buff their weapons with Dark before starting combat.
