# Drowsy Forlorn (DS3)

The Drowsy Forlorn is a character in Dark Souls III: Ashes of Ariandel.

## Location

Found at the very beginning of the Painted World of Ariandel, just before the Snowfield bonfire. He lies right behind the player when they first arrive through the painting fragment given by Gael.
