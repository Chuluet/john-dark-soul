# Earthen Peak (DS2)

The Earthen Peak is a location in Dark Souls II.

## Description

Earthen Peak is situated inside a large, decaying fortress covered with windmills. The area features many enemies and environmental traps that cause poison and bleeding.

The huge windmill next to the Central Earthen Peak bonfire can be destroyed by setting it on fire with a lit torch. This will drain the poison pool in the boss arena of Mytha, the Baneful Queen as well as other poison pools throughout the area, including the pool near the first bonfire in Harvest Valley.

## Bonfires

Lower Earthen Peak: Located a very short distance from the Covetous Demon boss room.
Central Earthen Peak: Located about midway through Earthen Peak, behind a fog door guarded by some Manikins.
Upper Earthen Peak: Located behind a hidden wall above the entrance to Mytha's boss arena. It is reached by ascending the stairs next to the boss arena entrance, taking a left, up another set of staris, making another left towards poison-filled urns that are on the ledge, then taking a right towards the end of the ledge and interacting with the wall to reveal a bonfire.

## Characters

Bashful Ray (Shade, SotFS): Summon sign is found right next to a destructible railing that leads to Laddersmith Gilligan. Summoning him will break the railing, indicating to players that it is possible to path through there.
Devotee Scarlett (White Phantom, SotFS): Summon sign is found on the first main floor after the Lower Earthen Peak bonfire, shortly after crossing the bridge to the windmill face of the level.
Jester Thomas (White Phantom): Summon sign is found outside of Mytha's fog door corridor, guarded by two Grave Wardens.
Laddersmith Gilligan (merchant) : Reached by breaking the wooden railing overlooking Harvest Valley and walking along the edge. In addition to acting as a merchant, he can help the player reach a number of items and moves to Majula after his dialogue is exhausted.
