# Elana, the Squalid Queen (DS2)

Elana, the Squalid Queen is a boss in Dark Souls II: Crown of the Sunken King.

## Location

At the bottom floor of Dragon's Sanctum.

## Summoning

Steelheart Ellie and Benhart of Jugo can be summoned for this battle. Ellie's summon sign is just before the fog gate, but Benhart's is further back, behind some large pillars.

## Lore

Elana, the Squalid Queen, like Nashandra, was a fragment of Manus, Father of the Abyss, supposedly a fragment representing the trait of wrath. Regaining her form, she became Elana, and later wife of the Sunken King. It is unknown if she designed to betray the Sunken King or if she truly loved him, but one day Sir Yorgh and his Drakeblood Knights besieged Shulva, defeating the Sunken King and awakening Sinh, the Slumbering Dragon, who then let forth a toxic cloud which annihilated the whole city.

Somehow, Elana survived the siege, and became furious and vengeful for her city's destruction and possibly her husband's death. She took on her true form and now accompanies Sinh, accruing souls for the day of her vengeance.

## Appearance

Elana is a tall female humanoid who appears to be skinless, having red and white exposed flesh and a skirt made of vein-like branches. She wields a similarly flesh-like poleaxe.
