# Elizabeth (DS2)

During the times of Drangleic, Elizabeth's existence persisted, passed down through oral tradition. She is revered as a saint of great beauty and virtue, who devoted her life to healing the ailments of the needy by concocting medicine and potions. Little more is known about her, apart from the fact that her unique medicines were still widely used long after her time.
