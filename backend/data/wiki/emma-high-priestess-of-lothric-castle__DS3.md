# Emma, High Priestess of Lothric Castle (DS3)

Emma, High Priestess of Lothric Castle is a character in Dark Souls III. She is voiced by Joan Blackham.

## Location

Found inside the cathedral at the end of the main courtyard in the High Wall of Lothric, opposite to Vordt's boss arena.

## Description

High Priestess Emma presents the player with the Small Lothric Banner when she is first talked to, an item which grants passage to the Undead Settlement. She also lets the player join the Way of Blue covenant, upon talking to her a second time.

After defeating the first three Lords of Cinder, she will give the player the Basin of Vows, which is used to trigger the appearance of the Dancer of the Boreal Valley. She also drops the Basin if killed at any time, allowing the Dancer to be encountered early.
