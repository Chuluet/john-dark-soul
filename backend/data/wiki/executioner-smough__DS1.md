# Executioner Smough (DS1)

Executioner Smough (処刑者スモウ) is a character and boss in Dark Souls and a mentioned character in Dark Souls III.

Smough was the royal executioner of Anor Londo in service of the Gods, wielding a massive hammer and wearing grotesque armor. He derived great pleasure from his work, to the point of being depicted as an "eerily gleeful" executioner. It is stated that he used to ground the bones of his victims beneath his hammer and consume them with his food; this ghastly habit might be what tempered his hammer's ability to absorb the lifeforce of adversaries. Smough also desired to be a part of the Four Knights of Gwyn, but his monstrous behaviour instead led to him being shunned and feared by the honourable knights.

At some point after Gwyn linked the First Flame and Gwyn's firstborn son was dethroned and exiled, nearly all of the deities abandoned Anor Londo and moved to an unknown location. Smough remained in the forsaken city alongside Dragon Slayer Ornstein to guard the cathedral where Gwynevere resided.

Together, these two powerful warriors would become the last ritual challenge before a Chosen Undead could be rewarded with the Lordvessel. Only after a lethal and lengthy trial, which involved ringing the Bells of Awakening and surviving Sen's Fortress, could the Chosen Undead be granted entry into the city of the gods. Here, guarding the chambers of Gwynevere, Ornstein waited alongside Smough, and oversaw those seeking an audience with the Princess of Sunlight.
