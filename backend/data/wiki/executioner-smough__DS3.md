# Executioner Smough (DS3)

thumb|150px|The massive hammer of Smough.
After centuries of waiting for the Chosen Undead without success, Ornstein grew tired of his duty and left the cathedral in search of the Nameless King. Consequently, the last remaining guard was Smough, who continued to hold his post and protect "Gwynevere". As a reward for his loyalty, Gwyndolin finally granted him the title of "knight," even if he was the last one left in Anor Londo after all others had abandoned it. As the cathedral's sole guardian, Smough met his end when a worthy Undead reached Anor Londo and defeated him, gaining an audience with "Gwynevere" and the Lordvessel with which to begin their search for powerful souls.

Smough's ritual hammer was preserved as a relic by Gwyndolin and protected in his manor when Irithyll of the Boreal Valley rose from the ruins of Anor Londo.
