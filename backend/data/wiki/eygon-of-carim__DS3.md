# Eygon of Carim (DS3)

Eygon of Carim is a character and summonable phantom in Dark Souls III. He is voiced by Grahame Fox, who also voices the Cage Madman.

He is a knight reluctantly tied to Irina of Carim, and the evolution of Irina's storyline will lead Eygon to become either an ally of the Ashen One and be summonable for as many as two boss fights, or render him hostile and make him seek the Ashen One to slay them.

## Locations

Undead Settlement — Found sitting outside the church where Siegward of Catarina and the giant archer are located.
Road of Sacrifices — White summon sign before the Crystal Sage fight, provided the player has spoken to Irina of Carim at Firelink Shrine at least once.
Firelink Shrine — Sitting on a set of stairs near the bonfire, after saving Irina and then defeating the Abyss Watchers.
Lothric Castle — White summon sign in the chapel near the Dragonslayer Armour fight, if Irina has not been corrupted or killed.

## Description

Eygon wears Morne's Set and wields Morne's Great Hammer along with the Moaning Shield.

## Lore

Eygon is a knight from the kingdom of Carim, where he had an sister several years older than him. As all Carim knights, Eygon was gifted a black armor set based on that of legendary Carim knight Morne as well as a great hammer based on Morne's upon reaching knighthood. For the same occasion, he was also gifted the Moaning Shield, which, strangely, adorns the sculpted face of his sister; who gifted it to him, and the significance and origins of this unique shield, are unknown. Knights of Carim are also traditionally gifted Morne's Ring, which boosts the power of miracles, although it is unclear if Eygon wore one.

The Way of White religion is very prominent in Carim, leading to the training of maidens as magicians. Drawing inspiration from Morne's dedication to the goddess Caitha, every knight of Carim dedicated their entire career to a single maiden. As a result, Eygon was assigned to Irina of Carim, a frail, blind prospective Fire Keeper.

Eygon and Irina travelled to the kingdom of Lothric, where Irina was to become a fully-fledged Fire Keeper. However, a "grand treachery" from Irina led the two to consider the quest failed by her fault, and Eygon was seemingly left with little esteem for his assigned maiden, whom he considers "a lost cause" and "beyond repair", although he promised her that he would end her suffering should she lose her mind to the "darkness" that threatened to overcome her. Although he did not technically betray his oath to protect her, Eygon ended up patiently waiting outside a cell containing a confused and highly distressed Irina, unbothered with rescuing her, and perhaps having locked her up himself; Irina thinks herself alone, seemingly believing Eygon to have completely abandonned her.

## Plot

Eygon is first encountered in the Undead Settlement, near the tower leading to the Road of Sacrifices, right outside the cell where Irina is held. He has an initially negative disposition toward the Ashen One, and can even try to dissuade them from rescuing Irina after they have unlocked the cell, but if they accept Irina's services (leading her to relocate to Firelink Shrine), Eygon will ally himself with the Ashen One, making it clear that their alliance is only valid for as long as Irina is kept safe and waring them that he will make them pay if her safety is at risk. After this, he can summoned for the Crystal Sage boss fight. If the player kills Irina after she arrives in Firelink Shrine, Eygon will be waiting for them, hostile, in the area outside of the Shrine past the door next to the Iudex Gundyr bonfire, seeking to avenge her. After the Ashen One has defeated the Abyss Watchers, Eygon will briefly show up at Firelink Shrine to check up on his maiden.

Irina's questline can end in two ways: her "good" questline takes place if the Ashen One provides her with all non-dark miracle tomes and purchases the associated miracles. This will lead Irina to finally achieve her dream of becoming a Fire Keeper, relocating to the bottom of one of the towers outside the Shrine. After this, Eygon can be summoned for the Dragonslayer Armour boss fight; once the boss is slain (even if he was not summoned) and Irina has become a Fire Keeper, Eygon's dead body can be found in the cell Irina was originally found. It is unclear how he died, although the fact that he returned to the place he had abandoned Irina might indicate suicide out of guilt for failing to believe in her.

The second outcome of Irina's questline happens if any dark miracle is purchased from her after giving her a forbidden tome, leading her to succumb to "darkness" and Eygon to kidnap her in a rescue attempt. This will also lead him to appear hostile next to the Iudex Gundyr bonfire, guarding Irina. After he is slain, Irina, when talked to, will return to Firelink Shrine, although she will then be too lost to the "darkness" to provide her services as a miracle merchant. She will not even be able to feel the Ashen One's touch, unless they are wearing Eygon's gauntlets, which lead her to mistake them for Eygon and asking "Eygon" to fulfill his promise to mercy kill her.

Killing Eygon at any point will result in him dropping his weapons, Morne's Great Hammer and the Moaning Shield, and will make his armor available for purchase from the Shrine Handmaid; if he dies on his own as part of Irina's "good" ending, his body can be looted for the same two items and the Handmaid will also sell his set. Outside of his potential rescue of her in Irina's "bad" questine, her questlines remain completely unaffected by Eygon's fate.
