# Felkin the Outcast (DS2)

Felkin the Outcast, also known as Felkin the Hexer, is a character and merchant in Dark Souls II.

He is voiced by Andy Gathergood, who previously voiced the Crestfallen Merchant and Laurentius of the Great Swamp in Dark Souls.

## Location

Felkin can be found right before the first bonfire in Huntsman's Copse, coming from Majula after using Licia or the Rotunda Lockstone to open the path left.

## Description

Felkin the Outcast dons the Hexer's Set. Unless made hostile, he is sitting in a wooden chair, facing a wall, and seemingly constantly lost in thought.

Felkin is a sorcerer who has devoted himself to the Dark and the powers of hexes. He will offer his wares, including hexes, if the player has both Intelligence and Faith levels of 8 or above; having the required level in only one of the two stats is not enough. If the player does not meet the requirements, he will dismiss them and request to be left alone.

If the player speaks to Felkin with Intelligence and Faith levels at 20, Felkin will gift the Hexer's Set and the Sunset Staff. If the player previously talking to Felkin meeting his requirements but no longer meets them due to re-specs via a Soul Vessel, Felkin will still offer his wares, his dialogue unchanged.

Strangely, if made hostile, he will cast only pyromancies with his Sunset Staff, even though when wielded by the player, the Sunset Staff only allows for the casting of hexes or sorceries. He'll also use the Chaos Rapier and Chaos Shield, which he only offers as wares in Scholar of the First Sin.

If killed, he will drop the Sunset Staff, and the Hexer's Set will be available for sale from Merchant Hag Melentia for a total of 18,600 souls. Being gifted his staff and set, or killing him for them, are the only two ways of acquiring these items.

## Lore

Once an aspiring magician, Felkin studied both sorceries and pyromancies, including at "a great school in the South"; this school was likely located in Melfia, a kingdom directly south of Drangleic known for its sorceries and pyromancies, and even founded by sorcerers. His wares imply that he also studied miracles, as he sells the Magic Barrier miracle as well as items from Lindelt, a land known for its miracles, such as the Archdrake Chime used as a catalyst for miracles. Lindelt notably considers all forms of sorcery anathema.

In spite of his magical abilities, Felkin struggled with his studies due to motivation issues; no magic appealed to him, to the point where he considers that he has learned "nothing at all" during those years. However, while at that school in the South, he "happened upon the dark". It is unclear what exactly he means by this, but the result, or perhaps the event itself, was his discovery of hexes, a form of magic different from all others he had studied before.

Hexes were a unique form of magic, separate from miracles, pyromancies and sorceries, although derived from the latter, and originating from the lost land of Oolacile. While the Xanthous dedicate themselves to the golden sorceries that were traditional to Oolacile and manipulated light, Hexes are derived from sorceries born from Oolacile's eventual corruption by the Abyss. In hexes, Felkin finally found a magic towards which he felt a true kinship, characterizing the Dark as having a "warmth" and a "nostalgic" feeling, likely subconscious results of humanity's primal connection to the Dark. He even develloped original hexes: Resonant Flesh, which healed the caster in exchange for souls, Dark Weapon, which imbued a weapon with Dark, and Resonant Weapon, which imbued a weapon with even more dark at the added cost of souls..

Eventually, Felkin's research into hexes and the Dark brought him to the fallen kingdom of Drangleic, "where Dark runs deeper than anywhere else", likely due to the Throne of Want holding the power of the fading First Flame and making the kingdom the epicenter of the Undead curse and a potential coming of an Age of Dark.

It is of note that, having come to Drangleic to research the Dark and not in search of a cure for Undeath, Felkin explicitly states that he is not undead, making him one of the very few uncursed humans in the kingdom. Felkin has a stutter of sorts, although it is unclear if it is an actual speech disorder or merely his tendency to get lost in his own thoughts of the Dark.

If attacked, Felkin will state that the Bearer of the Curse has "let Dark take control", indicating that he deems the Dark dangerous; and, if successfully defending himself, will claim: "One day...I will share your fate...", suggesting his consideration of even his own downfall to the Dark inevitable.
