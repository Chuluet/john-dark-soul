# Fire Keeper (Dark Souls III) (DS3)

The Fire Keeper is a character in Dark Souls III.

She is voiced by Olivia Mace.

## Location

The Fire Keeper can always be found tending the bonfire or sitting on the stairs in Firelink Shrine.

## Description

The Fire Keeper tends to Firelink Shrine and serves the Ashen One throughout their long and arduous journey. She is a blind maiden of unknown origin; she was, at the time, the current Fire Keeper that tended to the flame when the Ashen One arrived at the Firelink Shrine to fulfill their duty. She dons a white crown that covers her eyes, and a black robe, as well as black gloves. Like all fire keepers, she can transform souls into raw power.

She allows the player to upgrade their stats individually in exchange for a determined amount of souls per level.

She can also cure dark sigils the player has accumulated either by leveling up with Yoel or by taking part on the ritual in Anor Londo. Additionally, she will also heal their hollowing level, thus restoring their physical appearance, when provided with a Fire Keeper Soul. The cost for healing each dark sigil is directly related to the player's soul level.

If given the eyes of the first Fire Keeper, she sees a world without fire, and is frightened by the mere thought of it. Even so, she is willing to end the cycle permanently if the Ashen One requests it. If the Ashen One chooses to realize her dream, she will be summoned to the Kiln of the First Flame. At that time, she will hold the First Flame in her hands and let it die out, thus unlocking the End of Fire ending. If the Ashen One chooses to succumb to their desire, and kill her during the ritual; the power of the First Flame would fall to their hands.

The Fire Keeper can also be talked to, and her dialogue changes throughout the course of the game.

She will psychically respond to most gestures performed by the player.

If killed, she will always respawn at Firelink Shrine, stating that as a Fire Keeper, she cannot die.
