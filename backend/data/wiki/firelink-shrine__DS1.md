# Firelink Shrine (DS1)

Firelink Shrine is a location in Dark Souls.

The first area visited after the player escapes the Undead Asylum, it serves as the entry point into Lordran for newcomers and a crucial central hub for explorers.

## Description

The Firelink Shrine serves as a base for the Chosen Undead and is a hub connecting to many other locations. Once a character is rescued, or upon reaching a turning point of their storyline, they will typically relocate to Firelink Shrine and offer some sort of service. Anastacia of Astora is the bonfire's Fire Keeper.

If any key item (such as a Lord Soul or the Covenant of Artorias) is dropped during gameplay, it will respawn in the chest behind Kingseeker Frampt's location.

## Characters

This is a list of characters who start at or relocate to Firelink Shrine during the course of the game:
Crestfallen Warrior: Seated by the bonfire, he will disappear after the player speaks to him once Kingseeker Frampt arrives.
Anastacia of Astora: The local Fire Keeper, located in a cage at the bottom of the stairs by the Crestfallen Warrior.
Petrus of Thorolund: Teacher of miracles, found near the stairs to the elevator.
Reah of Thorolund : Teacher of advanced miracles, found near Petrus. Note that she will not offer any spells while at Firelink.
Vince of Thorolund: Reah's friend and bodyguard who will disappear along with her.
Nico of Thorolund: Reah's friend and bodyguard who will disappear along with her.
Knight Lautrec of Carim: Arrives after escaping from the church in Undead Parish. Found near the Fire Keeper's cage, he will disappear after Anastacia's death.
Griggs of Vinheim: Teacher of sorceries who will be located near the stairs to Undead Burg after his rescue from there.
Laurentius of the Great Swamp: Teacher of pyromancies who arrives at Firelink after being rescued in the Depths. Located between the bonfire and Kingseeker Frampt's area, he will give the player the Pyromancy Flame.
Big Hat Logan: A teacher of advanced sorceries who arrives at Firelink after being rescued in Sen's Fortress. He can be found near Griggs.
Kingseeker Frampt: Primordial Serpent who arrives at Firelink after both Bells of Awakening have been rung. He will appear in the pool that was previously filled with water.
Domhnall of Zena: Merchant who arrives at Firelink after both Bells of Awakening are rung, he will be found under the aqueduct leading to Undead Burg.
Ingward: One of the three Sealers of New Londo, who arrives at Firelink once the player speaks to him following the defeat of the Four Kings.
Patches: A former thief who renounces his life of crime and becomes a merchant when he arrives at Firelink after Gravelord Nito is defeated. He will be found in an alcove by Kingseeker Frampt.
Siegmeyer of Catarina: Knight of Catarina who arrives at Firelink after being saved in Anor Londo.
Sieglinde of Catarina: Siegmeyer's daughter who arrives at Firelink after being rescued from the Golden Crystal Golem in The Duke's Archives.
Giant Crow: A constant presence that acts as a means of transport between Firelink Shrine and the Undead Asylum.
