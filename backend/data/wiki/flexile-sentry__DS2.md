# Flexile Sentry (DS2)

The Flexile Sentry is an optional boss in Dark Souls II. They are found aboard the ship in No-man's Wharf.

Additionally, the game features a respawning Flexile Sentry (either in the Shaded Woods in the base game, or in Sinners' Rise in Scholar of the First Sin), plus two non-respawning ones in Frozen Eleum Loyce (in Crown of the Ivory King).

## Location

The Flexile Sentry can be found in the brig of the ship that arrives to No-man's Wharf. The ship is called into dock by ringing the bell at the top of the cliffside village.

A Flexile Sentry also appears in the Shaded Woods, near the Shrine of Winter on the path to Drangleic Castle. Two non-respawning Sentries are also found behind the torch-locked gate in Frozen Eleum Loyce.

## Scholar of the First Sin

In Scholar of the First Sin, a respawning Flexile Sentry spawns in Sinners' Rise at the beginning of the flooded prison area, replacing the Enhanced Undead normally present here; from NG+ onward, both will spawn in this area. The Flexile Sentry normally found in the Shaded Woods has been replaced with a Forest Guardian.

## Description

Appears as two conjoined reptilian torsos facing away from each other and attached at the waist with one set of legs. Both sides each dual wield their own respective weaponry of either spiked clubs or scimitars.

## Attacks

Overhead Slam: Leaps and slams both clubs on the ground. 
Club combo: Swings both clubs at the same time, twice in a row. If in melee range, he will follow up by thrusting both clubs.
Sword Flurry: Slashes both blades in a flurry then finishes with a leaping double-sword strike.
Sword Combo: Slashes twice and ends with a thrust attack.
Grab: The sword half will skewer the player. If the player is fighting the club half then the club half will bend over, allowing the sword half to lean upwards and stab the ground over him. If caught by either attack, the player will be sent above the sentry and in quick succession, stabbed and mauled by the creature with great speed.
Conjoined combo: Uses both halves of the creature to attack the player with several outlandish attacks.
