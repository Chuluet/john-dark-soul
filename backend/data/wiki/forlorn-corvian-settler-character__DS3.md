# Forlorn Corvian Settler (character) (DS3)

The Forlorn Corvian Settler is a character in Dark Souls III: Ashes of Ariandel. He is a non-hostile Forlorn Corvian who is encountered near the entrance of the Corvian Settlement.

He is voiced by Tim Bentinck, who also voiced Steady Hand McDuff in Dark Souls II.

## Location

Found in the Painted World of Ariandel, in the corner of a house as one enters the Corvian Settlement.

Relocates outside the house once Sister Friede has been defeated.

## Description

A non-hostile Forlorn Corvian who welcomes the Ashen One to their settlement in the painted world and begs them to put an end to the rot that now afflicts their world since Friede deceived Father Ariandel.
