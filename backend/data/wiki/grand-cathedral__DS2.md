# Grand Cathedral (DS2)

The Grand Cathedral is a location in Crown of the Ivory King.

## Lore

The grand cathedral of Eleum Loyce was built by the Ivory King in an attempt to appease the raging Chaos that brewed deep below the land. Within, Alsanna the oracle prayed in silent vigilance, but their effort was in vain. After the Ivory King departed Eleum Loyce, he left everything to Alsanna.

## Overview

Upon entering the main hall, Alsanna the Silent Oracle can be found. After speaking to her and accepting her offer, she will melt various frozen sections of Eleum Loyce. Most notably, the large pit leading to The Old Chaos will be opened.
