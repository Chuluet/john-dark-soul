# Grave of Saints (DS2)

thumb|400px
The Grave of Saints is a location in Dark Souls II.

## Description

The Grave of Saints is a tomb located halfway down the deep pit in Majula. At one point, it may have served as a resting place for Drangleic's holymen, but has since been overrun by rats. The Grave is one of the Rat King's domains and, as such, trespassers will be summoned to the worlds of his subjects to be dealt with.

## Bonfires

Harval's Resting Place: Upon arriving, via the landing in the deep pit, this bonfire will be a short distance into the cave.
Grave Entrance: This bonfire is on the second level, just before the fog gate to the Royal Rat Vanguard.
