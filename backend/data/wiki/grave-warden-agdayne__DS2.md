# Grave Warden Agdayne (DS2)

Grave Warden Agdayne is a character and merchant in Dark Souls II.

He is voiced by Patrick O'Kane.

## Location

Found a short distance into the Undead Crypt. Upon entering his room, he will warn the player against producing light.
Can be summoned for the Velstadt, the Royal Aegis boss fight. His summon sign is found on the left side of the large room before Velstadt's chamber.

## Plot

When approached, Agdayne bids the player not produce light. Wielding a lit torch or using Cast Light within this room will cause him and his fellow Grave Wardens to attack the player.

After conversing with him, Agdayne will give the player access to his wares, and he will become available as a summon against Velstadt, the Royal Aegis.

## Lore

As a Fenito, Agdayne watches over the Undead Crypt and its deceased to make sure they are properly kept in their rest and not disturbed. He was entrusted this task by "the one who gave us the first death". He mentions a "king of light" from an age long ago who was afraid that humans would bring forth an Age of Dark. The king he mentions is likely Gwyn, Lord of Cinder.
