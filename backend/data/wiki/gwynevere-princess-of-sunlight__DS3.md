# Gwynevere, Princess of Sunlight (DS3)

At some point in time, after her younger brother Dark Sun Gwyndolin assumed the title of "Chief Deity" and built Irithyll of the Boreal Valley on the ruins of Anor Londo to house the descendants of the gods, she returned to her ancient home. Here the goddess was venerated with multiple statues displayed throughout the city and became the progenitor of several children with royal blood such as Rosaria, Mother of Rebirth and the Dancer of the Boreal Valley. The goddess would return to her quarters in the old cathedral of Anor Londo, protected by the remaining Silver Knights who had once protected her illusion and whose shields she would once again bless. Eventually she also left Irithyll, leaving her Sun Princess Ring behind.

Sometime later, Gwynevere reached Lothric and became the queen of the kingdom, marrying into the bloodline of its monarchs. The goddess resumed her role as a divinity, producing holy water with healing properties and being worshipped by humans as a symbol of fertility and bounty. Her "angelic daughter" Gertrude also became her holy maiden and her healing miracles were taught to her personal knights. During the long tradition of supporting the linking of the Flame by the kingdom of Lothric, the goddess alone cared for the Cemetery of Ash, the resting place for the Unkindled, beings who had failed to link the flame and whose graves saw no visitors.

She eventually became wife to King Oceiros, the last monarch of Lothric, and supported the kingdom's desire to produce an heir worthy of linking the First Flame, giving birth first to Elder Prince Lorian, raised to become a knight, and Younger Prince Lothric, expected to become a "champion" and sacrifice himself to the Flame. However, when Prince Lothric and his brother refused to link the fading Flame and they chose to wait out the end of the Age of Fire, King Oceiros went mad in his attempt to produce an heir worthy of their sacrificial destiny. The king discovered the heretical faith of the paledrake Seath among the scholars of the Grand Archives and became convinced that a crossbreed heir would have the strength to link the Flame. Oceiros eventually became a deformed humanoid dragon and with him the queen had her youngest child, Ocelotte. After producing the potential champion, Gwynevere quietly disappeared from the kingdom of Lothric. Realizing that the Flame would soon go out and it would be necessary to bring back even the Unkindled to search for a new champion, the queen enshrined her ring in the Cemetery of Untended Graves, so that one day an Unkindled might profit from its use.

In the final days of Lothric, the citizens of the kingdom forgot even the miracles of the goddess of sunlight, and ended up recounting childish but earnest stories to try to recall her blessings and the back of her figure, as the last image of their queen before she left.
