# Harvest Valley (DS2)

The Harvest Valley is a location in Dark Souls II.

## Description

Harvest Valley lies beyond Huntsman's Copse. It is an open, charred valley scattered with poisonous pits and mines, in the shadow of a huge decaying windmill.

## Adjacent locations

Earthen Peak
Huntsman's Copse

## Bonfires

Poison Pool: Near the entrance from Huntsman's Copse, this bonfire is located in a cave on the far side of a poisonous pool.
The Mines: After the quarry with Undead Laborers and two Mounted Overseers, this bonfire is located in a small cavern at the end of a nearby tunnel.

## Covenants

Heirs of the Sun: To the left of the entrance to Earthen Peak is a road which leads to a cave containing a broken statue bathed in sunlight, similar in appearance to the Sunlight Altar from Dark Souls. Kneeling in front of the altar will prompt the player to join.

## Characters

Stone Trader Chloanne (merchant): Located close to the entrance from Huntsman's Copse, Chloanne is a Titanite merchant who will move to Majula outside her father's workshop after her dialogue is exhausted.
Lonesome Gavlan (merchant): Found in a cave reached through the large basin filled with poisonous mist which lying between the two area bonfires, as long as the player has previously spoken to him in No-man's Wharf.
