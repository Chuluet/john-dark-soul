# Havel the Rock (Dark Souls III) (DS3)

Havel the Rock is an enemy character in Dark Souls III.

## Description

Havel returns once more, this time as an enemy summoned by wizards in Archdragon Peak, and also in a proper encounter at the top of one of the buildings of this sacred place. The fight, however, will not be as difficult as it was in Lordran, since the player will most likely be at a high level and wielding advanced weaponry at that point in the game. In addition, the combat area will be much more spacious, allowing for better evading maneuvers.

New to his repertoire of attacks is the ability to cast Stone Flesh on himself and cover his body with a rock layer, which will render him extremely resilient to attacks, but also reduce his mobility.

## Locations

Havel has a small chance of being summoned by the two Man Serpent Summoners that inhabit Archdragon Peak, one of which is found in the Dragon-Kin Mausoleum, and the other just before reaching the Great Belfry.

Havel's fixed location lies at the top of the building on the right after coming from the Great Belfry, standing in front of a dead wyvern and guarding a Titanite Slab; it does not matter if he was previously defeated as a summon in any of the other two areas. As with other enemies of this kind, he will not respawn once he has been defeated in this location.
