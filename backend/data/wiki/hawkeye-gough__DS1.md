# Hawkeye Gough (DS1)

Hawkeye Gough was once a Giant who lived in the wilderness. He wielded a bow larger than any other, that only he had the strength to fire. His ammunition of choice were large arrows that he crafted on his own out of stone and tree bark. His skill was so vast that he could even knock a flying dragon out of the sky with ease.

His ability was likely the reason why he was made to join the Four Knights of Gwyn, making him the only member to not be part of the gods' clan. In spite of that, he was given the same honors as the other knights, supplied with a specialized armor made out of the same material as that of the Silver Knights, and a ring that would soon be permeated with the Giant's vast prowess with the bow. Lastly, he was made to lead a group of Silver Knights equipped with massive Dragonslayer Greatbows, which used special arrows that could puncture flesh easily. All of this made him an incredibly important figure during the war against the dragons, and his contributions to the conflict were so great that Lord Gwyn himself would bequeathe to him a medallion as proof of his distinguished honor, which paired nicely with the dragon bone pauldrons that he had also earned in the battlefield.

Outside of his involvment in the ancient war, Gough also had his fair share of interpersonal relationships. He was a friend of his fellow knight Artorias, as well as the Giant Blacksmith of Anor Londo, to whom he would gift his ring. Additionally, the heroic archer Pharis would be the only other person to rank alongside him in skill with the bow, in spite of being just a human.

At some point in time, Gough would retire from his position as knight, as there was no more need for a dragon hunter like him anymore. He would end up in Oolacile, where he was eventually imprisoned within a tower by its treacherous citizens, locked away with a rather powerful spell. They also sealed his eye holes with tree resin, dismissing the old hero as nothing but a vulgar, brutish Giant. Trapped and without other options, Gough began carving unusual faces from the bark of Archtrees, imparting real emotion to each and every one of them. Doing so allowed him to achieve some form of personal enlightenment.

He witnessed the fall of Oolacile caused by their arrogance and greed, as well as Artorias' failure to quell the Abyss and his demise at the hands of the Chosen Undead. Should the time traveling hero choose to engage the legendary Kalameet, a dragon so fierce not even Anor Londo dared to cross him, Gough could even be allowed one last hunt, firing an impressive shot in spite of his now limited vision, speaking to the true might of the legendary archer. Having done that, though, he'll give away his precious bow, officially leaving that life behind him.
