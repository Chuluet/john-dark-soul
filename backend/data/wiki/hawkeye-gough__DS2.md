# Hawkeye Gough (DS2)

The skilled Giant's name has been lost in the era of Drangleic, but some of his artifacts remain; namely the peculiar carved heads he had made during his imprisonment, which are well-regarded for  the fine craftsmanship still shining through after ages, despite their unknown artist. They are sold by Magerold of Lanafir, a treasure hunter and collector of curious items.

Another is the Hawk Ring, though it came to be associated with Blue-eyed Durgo, a rather successful archer from the land of Lanafir who used the ring's boon to earn many valiant victories.
