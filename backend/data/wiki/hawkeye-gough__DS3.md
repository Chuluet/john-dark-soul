# Hawkeye Gough (DS3)

Gough's name is still spoken of in the era of Lothric, remembered for his impressive deed of striking down a calamitous dragon in spite of having been blinded prior. Curiously, a Giant wielding a massive bow is the current owner of the Hawk Ring, using his skill with the weapon to protect peculiar birch trees that trace their origins back to Oolacile and Princess Dusk. In a sense, the great archer is akin to a spiritual successor of the ancient Giant knight.

Sadly, his carvings remain uncredited even in this age.
