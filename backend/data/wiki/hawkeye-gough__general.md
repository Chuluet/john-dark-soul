# Hawkeye Gough (general)

is a character and merchant in the Dark Souls: Artorias of the Abyss DLC. He is also a mentioned character in Dark Souls III.

He is voiced by Michael Carter, who also voices Biorr of the Twin Fangs, Blacksmith Ed and Blacksmith Boldwin in Demon's Souls.

## Location

He is encountered atop the coliseum on the way to Oolacile Township, after Knight Artorias is defeated. One must go up a set of stairs opposite the Battle of Stoicism Gazebo and open up a locked door with a key obtained in the adjacent town. Behind that door is a short ladder that leads directly to him. He will remain there for the entire game.

## Plot

Gough is found isolated on a locked tower above the coliseum of Oolacile. To reach him, the player must first locate the Crest Key, found in the deeper parts of Oolacile Township, hidden within a Mimic that rests next to a lit fireplace.

Upon unlocking the door, Gough will act as a merchant. He won't be too chatty, and his wares aren't anything special for the most part, but he will sell unique items of his making, such as two of his carvings and his great arrows.

His true relevance is revealed once the player discovers Black Dragon Kalameet's whereabouts. After meeting the beast at the gorge, who flys out of reach and breathes fire from up above, returning to Gough will have him ask about the encounter and if the dragon is causing the player trouble. Answering yes to his question, and then to the question after, will make the old knight assist with the hunt, taking up his bow to shoot the dragon out of the sky and forcing him to engage with the player on the ground for a proper battle. Despite this, Gough's involvment is not necessary to kill Kalameet, but beating him differently will result in a slower, more tedious process than just fighting him head on like every other boss beforehand. 

Regardless of how Kalameet is defeated, Gough will reward the player with his personal greatbow, though he needs to be talked to for that to happen. The only way to obtain his set, however, is by slaying him, which can be done at any point. He'll drop the bow too if he hasn't gifted it already.
