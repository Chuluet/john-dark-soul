# High Lord Wolnir (DS3)

High Lord Wolnir is a character and a boss in Dark Souls III.

## Location

At the end of the Catacombs of Carthus, the player will come upon a grim chalice. Interacting with it will send the player to the Abyss to face Wolnir.

## Description

High Lord Wolnir is a colossal skeleton adorned with jewelry and a few shreds of clothing. Only his upper body remains, so he will be crawling throughout the fight.

## Lore

Wolnir is a giant humanoid who once ruled the realm of Carthus of the Sands. Carthus was a desert kingdom where pyromancers developed their knowledge in isolation from other forms, thus evolving differently, and their warriors developed incredible sword techniques, said to move as weightlessly as a grain of sand. They greatly prized victory, and didn't shun the use of twisted pyromancies for degrading their enemies' weapons and improving their own.

The warriors of Carthus swore their lives to Wolnir, growing ever ruthless and bloody in his name. Under Wolnir's rule, the kingdom of Carthus grew aggressive and expansionist, to the point where it was likened to uncontrollable fire, its beacons being used as war signals. His conquests were spearheaded by his warriors with superior combat skills and combat pyromancies. Wolnir soon subjugated the kingdoms around Carthus, melding the various "crowns" that ruled different lands into a single one, thus ascending to "High Lord". At some point Wolnir stripped three armlets and a holy sword from the corpses of some clerics, demonstrating how religion and gods counted for nothing in his kingdom.

Consumed by power, Wolnir wished to outlive every other being in the world and sentenced his entire empire to death, slaughtering civilians and soldiers alike. The Catacombs of Carthus, an immense underground necropolis, were built to inter the bones of countless people and soldiers. High Lord Wolnir would then use necromancy to reanimate himself and control a skeleton army and empire, wishing to remain the last being. Following this, the Abyss would break out in the Catacombs of Carthus and Wolnir would fall into it. Experiencing the terror of true darkness, Wolnir would plead to the gods for the first time, and his holy sword along with his stolen sacred bracelets would come to comfort him. They prevented him from being wholly swallowed by the Abyss, which was barely contained in a magical artifact.

After falling into the Abyss, Wolnir discovered a form of pyromancy that used the darkness of humanity; these pyromancies seemed to seek a will of their own, a result of interacting with humanity. The pyromancers that had become the guardians of the catacombs would discover Wolnir's dark flame, born from the Abyss and shadowless, and employed it for their own usage.
