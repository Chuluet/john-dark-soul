# Ingward (DS1)

Ingward (イングウァード) is a character in Dark Souls. He is voiced by Sean Barrett, who also voiced Andre and Petrus, Grandahl in Dark Souls II, as well as Andre and Hodrick in Dark Souls III.

## Location

On a platform atop the ladder located in the fireplace-like nook in the New Londo Ruins.

He is later found tucked in a corner in Firelink Shrine near Griggs if he is spoken to after defeating the Four Kings.

## Plot

Ingward awaits the one that has obtained the Lordvessel, granting them the Key to the Seal which is required to drain water from the lower ruins in order to enter the Abyss.

## Lore

Ingward is mentioned to be one of the three sealers who entered the New Londo Ruins in order to flood the area and stop the Four Kings and the Darkwraiths, together with Yulva and another whose name remains unknown. The other two tired of their duty and left the area, making Ingward the only remaining sealer. Not much more is known about the sealers other than the fact that they were also known for their healing powers, hence their crimson robes.

## Wares

Ingward can also remove Curse at the cost of one Humanity.
