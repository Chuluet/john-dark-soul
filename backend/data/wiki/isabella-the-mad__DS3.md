# Isabella the Mad (DS3)

is an enemy character in Dark Souls III.

## Description

Isabella was an inhabitant of the Undead Settlement who, at some point, started consuming human flesh, an action that she took with great glee. Now she wanders the nearby forest, lying in wait and murdering anyone unlucky enough to cross her.

## Location

Found in a cave below a hill in the Road of Sacrifices. From the Road of Sacrifices bonfire, follow the path toward a stone bridge and from there head to the left and down a narrow path that leads into the cave.
