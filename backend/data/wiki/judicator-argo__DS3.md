# Judicator Argo (DS3)

Judicator Argo is a character and special enemy in the Dark Souls III: The Ringed City DLC.

He is voiced by Pete Gallagher.

## Location

He is encountered inside the Church of Filianore, found at the end of the Ringed City after encountering Darkeater Midir on the bridge.

## Plot

The Giant will speak to the player as they approach the doors of the Church of Filianore, warning them not to enter. Upon doing so, he will admonish them for their intrusion and summon a Church Guardian to attack them. After a few seconds, he will either summon Halflight, Spear of the Church or a player who is a member of the Spears of the Church covenant before dying and vanishing.

If the player has defeated Darkeater Midir, then they can choose to join the Spears of the Church covenant and be summoned by Argo into the boss fight. However, members of this covenant are still required to make him hostile and defeat his champion in order to progress with the rest of the DLC. Entering the Spear of the Church fog door as part of the covenant will have Argo say unique dialogue, and the fight will proceed as normal.

If the player has defeated Argo and his champion, then they can resurrect him for a number of souls at the Purging Monument. This will allow them to rejoin the covenant and continue to level up its ranks, although reentering the church will make him hostile again.

## Lore

Argo is a Judicator Giant and the master of the Spears of the Church, capable of summoning its members to protect Princess Filianore. He was tasked by Lord Gwyn to ensure her slumber would not be disturbed. His loyalty to the Gods is absolute and he will not tolerate trespassers. This includes the Ashen One, in search of the Dark Soul. Even in death, he calls for the Church Guardians to make haste.
