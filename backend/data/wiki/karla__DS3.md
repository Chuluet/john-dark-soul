# Karla (DS3)

Karla is a character and merchant in Dark Souls III. She relocates to Firelink Shrine after she has been freed from her cell in Irithyll Dungeon.

Karla specializes in dark, forbidden magic. In addition to selling two sorceries by default, she will, if provided the associated tomes, also sell pyromanciesthat Cornyx is unable to teach, and miracles that Irina is unwilling to.

She is voiced by Jenny Funnel, who also voiced the Old Woman of Londor in the same game, Yuria and Selen Vinland in Demon's Souls, the Darkmoon Knightess and Quelana in Dark Souls, and Annalise and Iosefa in Bloodborne.

## Location

Karla is located in a locked cell in the lower level of the Irithyll Dungeon, in the area with numerous Jailers. In order to unlock it, the player must have the Jailer's Key Ring, found in the Profaned Capital, and then go back to the Irithyll Dungeon once more to free her.

After exhausting her dialogue and agreeing to rescue her, Karla will relocate to Firelink Shrine and become a merchant there. She can be found under the stairs to the left of Andre's smithy. Unlike most Firelink Shrine characters, she has no questline, nor does she relocate after all of her wares have been purchased, and will remain in Firelink Shrine regardless of the player's actions unless killed.

## Description

Karla is a child of the Abyss, clad in grey, tattered robes and wearing a hat not unlike the one worn by Witch Beatrice. She is ashamed of her origins, but will agree to teach the Ashen One numerous dark spells, as well as certain standard pyromancies.

## General information

Pyromancies, sorceries and miracles trainer. Accepts the following tomes:
Deep Braille Divine Tome
Grave Warden Pyromancy Tome
Londor Braille Divine Tome
Quelana Pyromancy Tome

In order to get Karla to accept the Dark Miracle tomes, the player must attempt to give each tome to her twice, as she refuses when initially presented with one but relents on the player's insistence.
