# Kiln of the First Flame (DS1)

The Kiln of the First Flame is a location in Dark Souls. It is the last area in the game.

## Description

This area is accessed by offering to the Lordvessel the souls of Bed of Chaos, the Four Kings, Gravelord Nito, and Seath the Scaleless in the Firelink Altar.

## Lore

The Kiln of the First Flame houses the First Flame, where the Lords first found their Lord Souls. At some point, the structure of the Kiln was constructed around the cave where the flame was first discovered, during the Age of Ancients.
