# Laurentius of the Great Swamp (DS1)

Laurentius of the Great Swamp (大沼のラレンティウス) is the basic Pyromancy trainer in Dark Souls. He is voiced by Andy Gathergood, who also voiced the Crestfallen Merchant, and Felkin the Outcast in Dark Souls II.

## Location

In a room after the second Butcher in the Depths. He is trapped in a barrel.
At Firelink Shrine near the path to Kingseeker Frampt.
Hollowed in Blighttown near where Maneater Mildred invades (if answered "Yes" to his question regarding Quelana's pyromancy).

## Plot

Laurentius is first encountered trapped in a barrel, presumably captured and stored there by a Butcher. After his release, he travels to Firelink Shrine.

If the player trains their flame with Quelana or acquires any chaos pyromancies, Laurentius will ask the player where they obtained such power. If the player answers "Yes" to his question, he will travel to the Blighttown swamp where he will become hollow. If the player answers "No", he will remain in Firelink Shrine and continue as a merchant and pyromancy trainer.

## Lore

Laurentius was a student of pyromancy in his home, the Great Swamp. He considered turning Undead to be beneficial, as it prompted him to travel to Lordran where pyromancers earn a certain respect.

## Wares

Laurentius can upgrade Pyromancy Flames in exchange for souls. He can enhance a pyromancy flame to up to +15, but cannot ascend it.
