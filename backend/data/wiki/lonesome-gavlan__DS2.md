# Lonesome Gavlan (DS2)

Lonesome Gavlan is a character and merchant in Dark Souls II. He is voiced by Stephane Cornicard, who also voiced Greirat in Dark Souls III.

## Location

Lonesome Gavlan is initially found in No-man's Wharf, on the second floor of the large two floor building occupied by several Dark Stalkers. From the first bonfire, proceed forward until encountering the second set of stone stairs to find the building, with a large hole in its side on both floors.
After meeting him in the wharf, he will relocate to one of the first caves in Harvest Valley. To find this location, start from the Poison Pool bonfire and enter the cave in the first poison gas pool. He is in a small, secondary cave to the left.
Finally, after talking to him in Harvest Valley, he'll relocate permanently to the Doors of Pharros, up the stairs outside of the Gyrm's Respite bonfire, on the way to Brightstone Cove Tseldora.

## Scholar of the First Sin

His location in No-man's Wharf is moved slightly. After ringing the bell and dropping down from a series of platforms, enter the leftmost building and proceed to the third floor. He will be standing beside the pulling ring that opens the gate connecting the two main wharf areas.

## Plot

Gavlan is a member of the stocky Gyrm race. He is the only merchant to whom the player can sell items, similar to Kingseeker Frampt from Dark Souls. Upon interacting with him after purchasing 16,000 souls worth of merchandise, he will give the player the Gyrm Greataxe.
