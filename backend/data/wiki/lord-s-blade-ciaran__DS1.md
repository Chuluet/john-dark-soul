# Lord's Blade Ciaran (DS1)

Ciaran was part of the gods' clan and the only female member of the Four Knights of Gwyn, fighting her king's enemies in the shadows as all Lord's Blades did and filling their targets with terror. As a knight, she earned the honor of a personalized Porcelain Mask that differentiated her from the other assassins of Gwyn, decorated with her own locks of ivory hair. In addition, she recieved the Hornet Ring, used while wielding her daggers to lay waste to her Lord's foes. Her fighting style was akin to a graceful dance, mesmerizing her victims with the Gold Tracer before poisoning them with the Dark Silver Tracer.

She appears to value Artorias a fair bit, stating that he was a dear friend of hers. She looked for him during the fall of Oolacile only to find him dead, put out of his misery by the Chosen Undead. She erected a small grave for him, offering a black lock of hair, likely the one featured on the knight's helm, and mourned him. Upon meeting the champion who had laid him to rest, she asked them to recieve his afflicted soul so she could give him a proper sendoff.

Years later, when Artorias' deeds faded to legend and the township was subsumed by the surrounding forest, her ring could be found clutched by a female corpse behind the hero's gravestone, meaning that the knight tended to his grave until her very end.
