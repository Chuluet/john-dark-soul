# Lord's Blade Ciaran (DS1)

Lord's Blade Ciaran is a character in the Dark Souls: Artorias of the Abyss DLC. She is also a mentioned character in Dark Souls III.

She is voiced by Hannah John-Kamen, who also voices Sweet Shalquoir in Dark Souls II.

## Location

She is encountered in the coliseum on the way to Oolacile Township, after Knight Artorias is defeated. She remains there briefly, before disappearing for the rest of the game.

## Plot

Ciaran appears after Artorias is defeated, asking the player for the soul they received after the fight. If they decide to hand it over to her, she rewards the player with the Gold Tracer and the Dark Silver Tracer. Refusing to give it to her will prompt her to stay until the area is reloaded, giving the player a chance to change their mind. Once she leaves though, there won't be any way to go back on her offer.

She will not spawn if the player consumes the Soul of Artorias before returning to the arena. The grave will still be there, though no one will be attending it. It's slightly different in the PC Version, as Ciaran can still be found kneeling by the small grave after the soul was used to craft the Abyss Greatsword. This gives the player a chance to still interact with her, or even challenge her for the unique loot such as her set, which cannot be obtained in any other way.

Ciaran was part of the gods' clan and the only female member of the Four Knights of Gwyn, fighting her king's enemies in the shadows as all Lord's Blades did and filling their targets with terror. As a knight, she earned the honor of a personalized Porcelain Mask that differentiated her from the other assassins of Gwyn, decorated with her own locks of ivory hair. In addition, she recieved the Hornet Ring, used while wielding her daggers to lay waste to her Lord's foes. Her fighting style was akin to a graceful dance, mesmerizing her victims with the Gold Tracer before poisoning them with the Dark Silver Tracer.

She appears to value Artorias a fair bit, stating that he was a dear friend of hers. She looked for him during the fall of Oolacile only to find him dead, put out of his misery by the Chosen Undead. She erected a small grave for him, offering a black lock of hair, likely the one featured on the knight's helm, and mourned him. Upon meeting the champion who had laid him to rest, she asked them to recieve his afflicted soul so she could give him a proper sendoff.

Years later, when Artorias' deeds faded to legend and the township was subsumed by the surrounding forest, her ring could be found clutched by a female corpse behind the hero's gravestone, meaning that the knight tended to his grave until her very end.

## Cut Content

According to her cut dialogue, she would have reacted to the player owning a ring that belonged to Artorias, which was stolen by "a man in a long coat", likely the shady Marvellous Chester. She would then ask them if they'd be willing to hand it over to her. This sequence was replaced by her request to to be given his soul.

A potentially ominous interaction is revealed in a cut dialogue from Hawkeye Gough. He was meant to warn the slayer of Knight Artorias about Ciaran, citing her affection for the knight. This seems to imply that the assassin may have been intended to fight players for what they did to the Abysswalker, motivated by her feelings for him. In the final game, her love for Artorias is much less pronounced, and could even be seen as simple admiration.

Her weapons were set to appear in Dark Souls III, as graphics of their item icon exist within the game files, which is what is used on this very page. They weren't meant to function as they did in Dark Souls, as they are displayed together as paired weapons. The reason why they weren't implemented is a mystery.
