# Lord's Blade Ciaran (DS3)

Legends of Ciaran survive to the time of Lothric, remembered as the only woman serving as one of Gwyn's Four Knights, and for her deadly abilities, which helped her dispatch countless enemies to the throne of Anor Londo. The Hornet Ring, her heirloom, can only be found in the Untended Graves, next to the same gravestone that Hawkwood the Deserter prays to in Firelink Shrine. It will only appear when the Abyss Watchers are defeated. They are admirers of Artorias and upholders of his legacy, which connects her to the tragic swordsman yet again.
