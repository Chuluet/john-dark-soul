# Lud, the King's Pet & Zallen, the King's Pet (general)

Lud, the King's Pet & Zallen, The King's Pet are dual bosses in .

They are optional bosses found at the end Frigid Outskirts, with the exact same appearance and moveset as Aava, the King's Pet. Lud starts the battle alone, with Zalllen joining later on.

## Lore

Lud and Zallen served the Ivory King, along with five other beasts. Each was conferred a specific duty. Lud and Zallen's was the mercy killing of exiles.

## Location

Found in a large arena located at the end of the Frigid Outskirts.

## Summons

All summons are found near the statues.
Abbess Feeva
Head of Vengarl
Scholar of the First Sin: Manhunter O'Harrah

## Attacks

Double Swipe Attack: Jumps forward and swipes its claws twice one after the other.
Jumping Bite: Grabs the player in his mouth and bites down, dealing heavy damage. After the bite, the beast throws the player to the floor.
Homing Ice Soulmass: Roars and casts a version of Homing Crystal Soulmass around itself. Fires them after a short delay.
180° Claw Attack: Spins 180-degrees and swipes its claws at anything in its path, knocking them to the floor.
Ice Blast: Roars, causing an area-of-effect blast that knocks the player to the floor.
Ice Pillars Attack: Summons damaging pillars of ice to emerge from the floor.
Regenerative Roar: Zallen will do a roar that both increases his damage and continuously heals him (about 1% per second) if he remains below half health for too long. If Zallen is downed before Lud, then the remaining beast will perform the roar at low health. The roar causes him to glow red and the effect lasts for about a minute or two, after which he does not appear to use it again. The animation appears similar to the ice blast move, but he throws his head further back while roaring.

## Defenses (Lud and Zallen)

(Stats are from Param Studio and are not estimated)Strike/Slash/Thrust defense are applied on top of Physical Defense. -10% thrust means taking 110% damage.
