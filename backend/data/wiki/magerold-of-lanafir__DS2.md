# Magerold of Lanafir (DS2)

Magerold of Lanafir is a character and merchant in Dark Souls II.

He is voiced by Joe Sims, who also voiced Creighton the Wanderer.

## Location

He is encountered at the end of a side corridor right of the Iron Keep's entrance, guarded by a few Alonne Knights. He is found sitting against the wall, surrounded by his belongings and wares, and will remain here for the entire game.

## Plot

Magerold sells a variety of items to the player, including several spells, armor, as well as nicher things, like the Carvings. He is also the only character able to lower a player's Faith or Intelligence requirement for sorceries and miracles in return for souls and a Skeptic's or Simpleton's Spice. It must be kept in mind that it will reduce the effectiveness of a given spell though, as it's still dependant on the associated Attack Bonus, and using a spell below its base requirements won't give the same results.

He has an additional purpose, as his dialogue will forshadow the Dragon Shrine, and will make it apparent that he wishes to visit it to find something of value. Once players reach the area, they can look for the Shrine's heirloom, the Petrified Egg, and bring it back to Magerold. Only then they'll be able to join the Dragon Remnants covenant and be awarded with the Ancient Dragon Seal, the Dragon Eye and the "Covenant of Ancients" Achievement.

After exhausting his dialogue and spending 10,000 souls in his shop, he will gift the player with the Covetous Gold Serpent Ring +1.

## Lore

Magerold hails from the land of Lanafir, an isolationist land to the south. Like many other traveling merchants from that country, he is quite the odd fellow, fascinated with things only if they grab his attention, not necessarily due to their worth. He claims to only be a merchant on the side, and that he is in truth a treasure hunter.

It might be the reason why he is found in the Iron Keep, the fortress of a once wealthy lord who perished by way of his own hedonism and greed. Many have come to the sinking fortress, pillaging it for its precious iron and reusing it for their own means, seeking its many treasures and weapons, as more and more of it was lost to the magma. As someone with an interest in gaining rare and peculiar items, it would make sense for him to wish explore its ruins.

Magerold also displays a fascination with dragons, secretly being an adherent of the Dragon Remnants and seeking the location of the Dragon Shrine so he may find great treasures there. The Bearer of the Curse can bring him one such artifact, a mysterious stone egg, likely that of a dragon. Upon being given the fine specimen, he will allow the adventurer into his covenant.
