# Manus, Father of the Abyss (DS1)

Manus, Father of the Abyss is a character in Dark Souls and a boss in Dark Souls: Artorias of the Abyss.

## Description

Manus is the final boss of the Artorias of the Abyss DLC. He is encountered in the Chasm of the Abyss.

It is possible to summon the past version of Great Gray Wolf Sif to aid in the battle. Sif's summon sign is found in the arena where the player fights Manus, inside the fog door.

## Appearance

Manus appears to be a massive beast-like humanoid with a long spiked tail and multiple chaotic horns sprouting from his head and neck, covered with red-glowing eyes. He wields a staff in his right hand to attack and to cast dark sorceries, while his larger, furry left arm, capable of extending to reach enemies, is complete with a hand laden with teeth inside the palm.

## Plot

Once the Chosen Undead has the Broken Pendant in their possession, Manus pulls them through a magical rift from Darkroot Basin into the past land of Oolacile.

He kidnapped Princess Dusk and is eventually confronted in the Chasm of the Abyss.

Manus was a primordial human who possessed an extraordinary soul consisting of a viscous, lukewarm lump of gentle humanity, an immense Dark Soul. He was also a sorcerer who used a rough and old wooden catalyst, very crude but large enough to be used as a weapon. His sorceries could grant a fleeting will to the Dark of humanity, induced to relentlessly chase enemies through channeling innate human affinities. Manus also possessed one half of an ancient stone pendant, a precious magical artifact that emanated a distinct, wrenching nostalgia as well as reverence.

Manus was a sorcerer of Oolacile, and after he died, his body was buried in the caverns beneath the city. 200 to 300 years prior to the journey of the Chosen Undead, the Primordial Serpent Kaathe arrived in Oolacile, in search of a potential Dark Lord following the flooding of New Londo. He manipulated the country to upturn the grave of the primordial man, Manus, buried beneath the dungeons of their city and resurrect him. Manus awakened to find his pendant missing, and was eventually driven into a beastly frenzy as his Dark Soul went wild, transforming him into the "Father of the Abyss".

His immense mass of humanity out of control led to the manifestation of an Abyss that spread, threatening to devour the entire land. The Dark emanating from Manus generated countless Humanity Phantoms and seeped among the residents of Oolacile, making their humanity go wild and mutating their bodies, their bloated heads lined with innumerable tiny red eyeballs and brain parts protruding from cracks. Corrupted Oolacile sorcerers discovered abyssal sorceries, more weighty and dealing physical damage instead of magical, since the Dark produced sorceries with a more tangible, "human" presence.

As the Abyss spread across the land of Oolacile, Knight Artorias arrived to halt its advance and confront Manus, as the Knight of Gwyn already had experience with fighting the Abyss and its creatures after the events of New Londo and his hunt for the Darkwraiths, and possessed a Silver Pendant that protected him from the Dark. However, Artorias's pendant was stolen by Oolacile's inhabitants and hidden behind an illusory wall, depriving Artorias of protection against Manus's dark sorceries. Together with his trusted companion Sif, Artorias faced Manus but his strength proved insufficient to defeat him, and he succumbed to the Abyss. Injured in the battle, Artorias used his Cleansing Greatshield to form a magic barrier that protected Sif from the Dark. Now corrupted to his soul by the Abyss and with his armor corroded, Artorias was left to fight alone and hold back the treacherous citizens of Oolacile.

Manus at one point kidnapped Princess Dusk of Oolacile—possibly after her rescue from a Golden Crystal Golem—and imprisoned her deep within the Chasm of the Abyss. In the meantime, his prized pendant came under the possession of Seath the Scaleless and then a Chosen Undead in the future; Manus, driven by his unrelenting search for his pendant, snatched the Undead from the future and brought them into the old Oolacile. There, the Chosen Undead put down the corrupted Knight Artorias and journeyed within the Chasm of the Abyss, where they finally defeated Manus and put him to rest. While Dusk was rescued in the process, she was driven by Elizabeth into thinking that she was saved by Artorias, preserving the legacy of the Wolf Knight. Nevertheless, Dusk came to recognize the Chosen Undead's similarity to the fabled knight, and developed doubts of her own as to her true savior.

## Strategies

It is possible for the player to kill Manus outside his fog gate:
Kill all the surrounding Humanity Phantoms.
Unequip all armor, then equip a fully upgraded longbow, Feather Arrows, the Symbol of Avarice, the Red Tearstone Ring, and the Hawk Ring.
Unequip the Symbol of Avarice after the Red Tearstone Ring activates.
Stand on the right side of the bridge that leads to the fog gate. There are a set of ridges, and on the third from the bridge, Manus's red eyes will be visible.
Shoot towards the eyes until you successfully hit Manus. Keep shooting until his health is low. Now you have two options:
Enter the fog door and finish him off.
Kill him from the ledge. The fog gate will disappear and you'll gain his reward souls. When you approach the platform that Manus usually grabs you off of, you can roll off to land without damage.

If the player wishes to face Manus conventionally, it is advisable to equip stamina-boosting items that such as the Cloranthy Ring, as well as a high-Stability shield that blocks physical damage. Only attack Manus after he finishes his jumping smash or frenzy combo, lest you be caught in the middle of his strikes.

Manus will start using his magic attacks at around 50% health. Use the Silver Pendant to deflect them, as they are difficult to dodge.

## Berserk Combo

Attack TypeParryableTrackingSpeed758000StrikeNoBothVery Fast
Manus lets out a quick scream which makes him mad, then performs four swipes and two downward smashes, potentially hitting the player six times. This deals high damage, and drains a lot of stamina if blocked.

## Dark Orb Barrage

Attack TypeParryableTrackingSpeed058500MagicNoBothAverage
Manus uses his Catalyst to cast a barrage of Dark Orbs, similar to the sorcery Dark Bead. The Dark Orbs spread out in a fan shape, making it easier to evade by strafing/rolling at close range. The Dark Orbs also drop to the ground after a moment, limiting the range of this spell.

Manus telegraphs this attack by holding his Catalyst out to his left, charging it for a moment, then swinging it towards the player to release the Dark Orbs. This attack can break the guards of most shields, so try blocking it with the Silver Pendant.

## Abyss Rain

Attack TypeParryableTrackingSpeed046700MagicNoBothAverage
Manus casts a dark sorcery where multiple Dark Orbs appear in the air above and rain down around him in a sizable area of effect. Each Dark Orb can home in on the player within a certain range. The player can use their invulnerability frames from fast rolling (12 invulnerability frames, 14 with the Dark Wood Grain Ring). Alternatively, use the Silver Pendant — it does not stop the Dark Orbs, but holds them in place above the player if you remain still, letting you roll away before the Silver Pendant wears off.

## Abyss Cage

Attack TypeParryableTrackingSpeed000MagicNoBothAverage
Dark Orbs propagate from above Manus, surrounding the entire arena before homing back in on Manus. This attack covers the entire arena, except for the area right next to Manus, like an inverted AoE. Dodge it by getting as close as possible to Manus once he starts casting. The Silver Pendant can also block this attack.
