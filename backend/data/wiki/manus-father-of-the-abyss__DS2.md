# Manus, Father of the Abyss (DS2)

Although he was killed, the Dark Soul of Manus was so large and powerful that he was reborn in death, and his soul split into miniscule fragments that retained some will. These pieces began to coalesce once again by absorbing other humanities, becoming the incarnations of different emotions of Manus and then human in shape, with their own self-awareness: Elana was the embodiment of Manus' wrath, Alsanna of his fear, Nadalia of his solitude and Nashandra, the tiniest fragment of Manus, the embodiment of his want. These new entities had some recollection of their father and his dark powers and when the Abyss that they called home was dissipated they scattered across the land. To sustain themselves and seek power, they reached Drangleic where in one way or another they came into contact with the holders of magnificent souls, drawn to their awesome strength.
