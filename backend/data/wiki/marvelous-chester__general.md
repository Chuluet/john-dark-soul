# Marvelous Chester (general)

is a character, merchant and invader in the Dark Souls: Artorias of the Abyss DLC.

He's voiced by William Houston, who also voices King Vendrick in Dark Souls II, Retired Hunter Djura in Bloodborne, and Oceiros, the Consumed King in Dark Souls III.

## Location

He is encountered in the Royal Wood, to the right of the coliseum where the fight with Knight Artorias takes place, past a short bridge. He leans against the cliff face, highlighted by a lit candelabra to his right. He remains there for the rest of the game.

He will invade as a red phantom early into Oolacile Township, on the descending walkway after the first Bloathead Sorcerer. This only happens if the player is human.

## Plot

Marvellous Chester is found very close to Artorias' arena and sells a variety of useful items such as Green Blossoms, various moss clumps, some offensive consumables, and other utility items, albeit for a higher price than normal. This makes him a very convenient stop for stocking up on items for the nearby boss battle or for the Battle of Stoicism unlocked right after.

Unfortunately, he will invade the player whenever they get to Oolacile Township, provided they are human. Unprepared players may be taken by surprise, especially since he has a completely different moveset compared to the usual red phantom NPC. Vanquishing him once won't actually stop him from invading another time, as he will always return every time the area is reloaded.

Despite doing this, Chester will still sell items to the player, either mocking them for coming to him after the invasion if he killed them, or speaking in a rather disgruntled tone as if being mocked by the player coming back if he is the one who was defeated. To stop all invasions permanently, the player will need to kill him there, which is also the only way to receive his unique set. Of course, killing him before he ever even invades will also prevent him from doing so in the first place.

## Lore

Marvellous Chester is a mysterious man who, much like the Chosen Undead from the time of Lordran, was snatched by Manus and stranded in the past. He met with Oolacile Sanctuary's keeper, Elizabeth, making quite the negative impression before departing. He is later met by the time traveling hero, offering his wares should they need them.

His attire is of an exquisite quality, betraying an aristocratic origin, as well as lending itself extremely well to stealth. That fact, coupled with his ownership of a Sniper Crossbow, suggests that the man may be one of Carim's snipers from far into the future, though nothing is truly certain with someone as shrouded in mystery as he is.

Despite appearing relatively friendly, he will take the first chance he can to backstab the Chosen Undead, ambushing them in the town of Oolacile for an unclear reason. Should the hero find the betrayal unacceptable, they can challenge the real version of Chester and slay him for his transgression. However, inheriting his equipment, more specifically his peculiar grinning mask, will cause them to make the same expression underneath it. This is believed to be the result of his vengeful spirit itself; the curse of Chester.

## Cut Content

There's an alternative model of Marvellous Chester without his coat and iconic top hat. This variant wields a peculiar black and gold Zweihander, which has an unused weapon model that would have allowed players to wield it. What this version of Chester was going to be used for is unknown.

According to cut dialogue from Lord's Blade Ciaran, a ring that belonged to Artorias would have been stolen by someone she describes as "a man in a long coat". The only character in the DLC that matches that description is Chester, implying he'd be more involved in the sidequests initially. The player would have to obtain the stolen ring from him, possibly by force.

He was also seemingly more involved with Elizabeth at some point, as all his cut dialogue makes reference to her. He was supposed to have a fascination with her peculiar sorceries, as well as chastising the player for siding with her against him. This would all explain why the mushroom lady seems to consider him odious in the final game.

His unscrupulous nature appears to have been toned down significantly from his inception, even though he continues to be shady and tracherous all the same in the current version of the story.
