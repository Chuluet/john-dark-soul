# Maughlin the Armorer (DS2)

is a character and merchant in Dark Souls II.

He is voiced by Richard Standing.

## Location

He is encountered in Majula, inside a small house left of the large pit. He remains there for the entire game.

## Plot

Maughlin is a merchant specialized in armor, with three basic shields as alternative options. He will at first only sell a few basic sets of armor; however, his variety will gradually increase as the player spends more souls on his wares, which also has an effect on his demeanor and personality.

After spending a total of 1,000 souls at his shop or higher, Maughlin will begin selling the Royal Soldier Set and the Elite Knight Set, as well as boss armors to the player; however, the respective boss must be killed first. He will also start growing a bit more confident, but keeping up his polite attitude.

After spending a total of 15,000 souls at his shop, Maughlin will gloat about how rich he has become and begin selling the unique Alva Set, an incredibly lightweight medium armor set with high defense. Late-game boss armor will be added to his stock as well, provided the player has got to them and killed them. He will also have a unique interaction if the player visits his shop without a single soul in their soul counter, as he will feel charitable enough to gift them the transparent variant of the Aurous' Set free of charge.

Maughlin will also be affected by NG+, or alternatively by increasing the bonfire intensity of Majula using a Bonfire Ascetic. In NG+ or at bonfire intensity 2 in Majula, he will sell the Moon Butterfly Set excluding the hat. In NG++ or at bonfire intensity 3 in Majula, he will begin selling the Moon Butterfly Hat.

## Lore

A merchant hailing from the distant land of Volgen, found west of Drangleic. Due to his progressively failing business, he has drifted into depression, and forced to travel from land to land selling his wares, with little luck. His wandering ventually led him to the desolate ruins of the once great kingdom.

Maughlin was an unsuccessful merchant back in Volgen, because it was impossible for him to get off the ground. A group of worshippers of the Way of Blue has become gatekeepers of trading in Volgen, hindering upcoming merchants under religious pretenses. He is outraged at the Apostles of Blue, condemning their claims to be serving the greater good as "absolute hogwash". His distrust isn't just a product of the unfairness he experienced, however, as the clergy of Volgen is well-known for being more power-hungry than it wishes to appear.

He'll get his start as a merchant with the help of the Bearer of the Curse, and later that of many unseen patrons, some of which never return to buy more. He has even met Benhart of Jugo, having an interest in his peculiar weapon and wishing to buy it. However, should he actually be presented with the blade, he'll easily recognize it as a fake.

At some point he will even forget his first customer and their patronage, and his original meek and humble personality will be replaced with that of a greedy, self-important man. However, that is a result of the Undead Curse, which he isn't even aware of being afflicted by. More profits lead him to forget of his homeland entirely, focusing only on the riches he gained.
