# Memory of the Old Iron King (DS2)

The Memory of the Old Iron King is a location in Dark Souls II: Crown of the Old Iron King.
thumb|300px|The throne of Iron King

## Description

The Memory of the Old Iron King is located at the top of Brume Tower. It is located within the armor and weapon once used by Sir Alonne. The Ashen Mist Heart and the Crown of the Old Iron King  are needed to enter the memory. 

The player can stay in the Memory of the Old Iron King only for a limited amount of time. After 18 minutes have passed, the memory will end the player will automatically be returned to Brume Tower.
