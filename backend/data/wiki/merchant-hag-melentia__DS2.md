# Merchant Hag Melentia (DS2)

is a character and merchant in Dark Souls II.

She is voiced by Marcia Warren.

## Location

She is encountered in the Forest of Fallen Giants, next to the Cardinal Tower bonfire.

After exhausting all of her dialogue and defeating The Last Giant, she moves to Majula, and settles on the left of the bonfire, next to the wall.

## Plot

Melentia is a merchant who sells various wares to the player including pieces of armor, weapons, and sorceries. If the player speaks to her, she provides some information about Drangleic's backstory. She also sells Lenigrast's Key, which opens Blacksmith Lenigrast's workshop in Majula and allows the player to use his smithing services, as well as a Fragrant Branch of Yore in the Scholar of the First Sin edition, which, although quite expensive, is needed to unlock certain paths around the world of Drangleic.

When she moves to Majula, her stock will change, and she will sell unlimited Lifegems, as well as starting to sell a limited supply of Radiant Lifegems. Her Brightbug stocks will also refill during the course of the adventure. Additionally, she will sell the armor sets and weapons of certain characters if the player has killed them.

When the player spends 10,000 souls in her shop and exausts her dialogue, Melentia will gift them with the Covetous Silver Serpent Ring +1.

## Lore

The traveling merchant Melentia is one of the few survivors of the fall of Drangleic, having witnessed the Giants crossing the sea and the resulting war that lasted for generations. She is always seen carrying her wares on her back due to her experience living in a war zone as one of the poorer commoners. She goes around the long-abandoned battlegrounds in search of things to sell, even stealing things from people in Majula, like Lenigrast. She also seemingly pillages the corpses of the recently deceased, selling their belongings in her shop later.

She speaks of the forest that currently envelops the large, seaside fortress, citing that many believe it to be a result of the dead giants transforming into them upon their death. Though she says not to simply trust the words of an old hag like her, what she says is actually completely true.

Beneath her crazed facade, the old merchant hides a dark secret. Her hands are permeated by a dark aura which will curse anyone that she touches, making them Hollow almost instantly. It is possible, then, that Melentia is some sort of witch or hexer.
