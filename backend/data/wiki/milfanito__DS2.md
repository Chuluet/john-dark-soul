# Milfanito (DS2)

thumb|right|300px

The Milfanito are a race in Dark Souls II.

There are four of them found in-game as non-playable characters, all unnamed; three are located in the Shrine of Amana, while a fourth is held captive inside a cage in Drangleic Castle; two of them vanish upon being talked to, while the others have dialogue. Each of them has a different design.

## Locations

Drangleic Castle
A Milfanito is held captive inside a cage in a small room at the top of the castle, sealed by The Embedded. The Key to the Embedded is required in order to free her.
Shrine of Amana
One is found inside the first building on the lake after the Tower of Prayer bonfire.
A second one is found right before entering the Demon of Song boss room. Although the other three Milfanito have similar designs, this one looks drastically different, with long arms and a particularly weathered appearance. She will vanish when attempted to be talked to.
A third Milfanito can be found praying at an altar after the Rise of the Dead bonfire. The player must be hollowed in order to open the door that gives access to this area.

## Plot

The Milfanito are secluded maidens who reside within the Shrine of Amana and sing to comfort those that carry death and dark within themselves. They seem to have very little individuality and more so refer to themselves as a single entity. When they sing, "the little ones dance", referring to the Brightbugs scattered around the area. They constantly say that they know nothing about the outside world but have no desire to leave. However, they possess Divine Blessings, although they don't know what they are. There are three Milfanito in the Shrine of Amana and one in Drangleic Castle.

The Key to the Embedded is obtained after the player defeats the Demon of Song; this key will grant access to the Milfanito imprisoned at the top of Drangleic Castle. When freed, she will sigh and disappear, dropping the Ring of the Dead. A brief singing can be heard while on the elevator leading back to the lower floor. Talking to each of the other two Milfanito afterward will reward the player with one Divine Blessing. After defeating the Demon of Song, the Milfanito will express their gratification for silencing "that cursed singing" and reward the player with an item.

In , they will reward the player with a Petrified Something after killing the Demon of Song. Talking to the Milfanito before said boss, will reward the player with a Fire Seed.

## Lore

Milfanito, together with the Fenito, were created by Gravelord Nito to deal with the emergence of Undead connected to the fading First Flame. These women had little concept of self, referring to themselves as a single entity. Nito taught to them a special song, with which the Milfanito would comfort the Undead; they lived only to sing, and continued until they no longer could.

Millennia past Nito's demise, the Milfanito and the Fenito persistently carried out the will of the "Great Dead One". They eventually arrived at Drangleic where they positioned themselves to manage the Undead Crypt, a place of rest for the "living" where those afflicted by the curse of Undeath would pray to one day gain entrance. The Milfanito sang their enchanting song to appease the spirits of the dead buried in the crypt.

In the realm plagued by the Curse, the Milfanito and the Fenito seemingly assumed responsibility for easing the suffering of Undead. A Milfanito is found praying in the Shrine of the Living, home to an artifact capable of reversing Hollowing. Another Milfanito imprisoned in Drangleic Castle possesses a Ring of the Dead that can make its wearer appear human even while Hollow, suggesting that the Milfanito managed or developed artifacts that dealt with the condition of Hollows.

## The Shrine of the Living

The altar that the fourth Milfanito prays to can be used to reverse hollowing. The player must not have any Human Effigies in their inventory or item box for this to work. Dropped effigies on the ground will also prevent the shrine from working. Praying at the Shrine while still having an effigy will result in the message "Alas, nothing happened".

The altar is located near the Rise of the dead bonfire, in Shrine of Amana.
