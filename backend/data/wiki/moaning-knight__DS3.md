# Moaning Knight (DS3)

The  is a dark spirit in the Dark Souls III: The Ringed City DLC.

## Location

He is found in the Ringed City, and invades inside the Shared Grave, an area situated right after Darkeater Midir's ambush in the gorge. After dropping down to the lower section of the tower, the Moaning Knight will appear in one of the burial chambers near the connecting staircase.

## Plot

The Moaning Knight is an enemy that players will most likely meet as they make their way through the Ringed City, as his invasion spot cannot be skipped. He will also invade regardless of if the player is a Host of Embers or not, a trait shared with other dark spirits in The Ringed City DLC. Upon his defeat he'll drop the Blindfold Mask, an alternative headpiece for the Black Witch Set.

## Lore

The Moaning Knight is one of the few Undead to have made it to the legendary Ringed City, a rather impressive feat. From his attire and weapon of choice, it's easy to deduce he was once a knight of Carim, men famous for following in the footsteps of the legendary Apostle Morne.

Most peculiar among his equipment is the Moaning Shield, which acts as the knight's namesake. This item was conferred to Eygon of Carim upon being knighted and it's meant to resemble his older sister. As such, it is most likely a unique keepsake of his, which would seemingly imply that the Moaning Knight is what became of him in the far future.

He is also in possession of the Blindfold Mask, a piece of equipment that once belonged to Zullie the Witch. This headpiece is said to closely resemble the crown of a Fire Keeper, which may explain why the knight is in its possession. If he is truly Eygon, then he may be holding onto it as a way to remember Irina of Carim, the blind maiden he was sworn to and who aimed to become a Fire Keeper in her own right.

The Ashen One will encounter him on their way to the Church of Filianore, among the skeletons of the Shared Graves. If he's truly Eygon, then this might mean he has completely forgotten about the Unkindled hero in all the years spent reaching the Ringed City, as he will attack regardless of how amicable their relationship used to be. Regardless of his identity, the Ashen One is forced to put down the Moaning Knight for good.
