# Moonlight Butterfly (DS1)

The Moonlight Butterfly (月光蝶) is an enemy in Dark Souls encountered as a boss in Darkroot Garden and as a regular enemy in the Crystal Cave.

## Description

A very large butterfly that has no visible head or legs and shows a wheel-like device between its upper wings. Moonlight Butterflies are capable of firing various magic attacks, but they don't have any melee capabilities.

The Butterfly in the Darkroot Forest is a boss and attacks as soon as its room is entered, but those found in the Crystal Cave are passive and will not attack the player unless provoked.

## Lore

The Moonlight Butterfly is a mystical creature whose soul was created by Seath the Scaleless. The result is a being naturally infused with a pure and powerful crystal power and moonlight magic, Seath's own characteristics, which are capable of emitting crystal sorceries and moonlight energy. Seath created multiple specimens of these butterflies that remained in his Crystal Cave but one left the place to move to inhabit the Darkroot Forest, defending its territory from intruders.

Despite the death of their creator, multiple moon butterflies survived over the centuries, migrating to Drangleic, evolving into Great Moths and developing poisoning abilities. They became rare and mysterious creatures that would appear only on full-moon nights in the winter. Their entire ecology was not known as their larvae were never once spotted, leading to rumors about the fact that creatures had been created with the magic their bodies were tinged in.

## Theories

It is possible to theorize that Seath used a human as the basis to create the Moonlight Butterfly. Seath has a long history of experimenting on humans to create new creatures like the Pisacas, the Serpent Soldiers, the Undead Crystal Soldier and Menscorpion.
