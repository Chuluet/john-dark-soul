# New Londo Ruins (DS2)

Although the Four Kings had been defeated, the seal that contained the ruins of New Londo and the water that had submerged it had been removed and as a result the remaining Darkwraiths were free to spread across the rest of the world, continuing to wreak havoc. Although these knights subsumed by dark never reached Drangleic in strength, knowledge of their existence has come to the region through foreign legends as poor souls who chased the art of Lifedrain and were able to freely manipulate the Dark, implying that they attacked the countries around Drangleic.

Despite this, the residuals sins of these knights who had succumbed to the temptations of dark reached Drangleic in the form of Cracked Red Eye Orbs. These artifacts were actively employed by the covenant of the Brotherhood of Blood to invade other locations and times and collect the blood of other Undead. Although this cult followed the same combat and invasion philosophy as the original covenant of the Darkwraiths, it was also centered around the mysterious figure of the god Nahr Alma, God of Blood and encouraged its followers to reject the world and follow a path of constant struggle and bloodshed, indulging in their hedonistic desires for violence to collect blood and offer it as a sacrifice for their god instead of seeking to understand the Dark powers of the humans.

The knowledge of the ancient healing sorceries of New Londo somehow survived and reached Drangleic where they were employed this time through miracles like Caressing Prayer, a text developed only around the kingdom of Vendrick and whose authenticity as a sacred text was even questioned, that was able to purify the body and remove all poison of the caster and those close to them.
