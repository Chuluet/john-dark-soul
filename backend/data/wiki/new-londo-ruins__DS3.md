# New Londo Ruins (DS3)

Although countless centuries had passed since the fall of New Londo to the dark, remnants of the Darkwraiths continued to exist as dark invaders, wearing armor now worn to the point of looking like they may crumble to dust at any moment, and they reached the region of Farron which was converging toward that of Lothric.

By this point the name of New Londo itself had been lost but the Red Eye Orbs rooted in the small country continued to be used. In fact, the followers of Rosaria, Mother of Rebirth, as members of the covenant of Rosaria's Fingers, would employ themto invade other locations and times and collect the Pale Tongues from other Undead and offer them to their speechless goddess.

The ancient miracle of Drangleic times which conveyed the knowledge of the healing sorceries of New Londo, in the times of Lothric is associated with Caitha, Goddess of Tears in the form of the miracle Caressing Tears, the tale of the many deaths surrounding the goddess that was able to cure from bleeding, frostbite and poisoning.
