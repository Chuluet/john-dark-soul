# Nico of Thorolund (DS1)

Nico of Thorolund (ニコ) is a minor character in Dark Souls.

## Description

Nico wears the Cleric Set and wields the Crescent Axe.

## Plot

Nico, and his brother-in-arms Vince, are bodyguards of Reah of Thorolund. Unlike his comrade, Nico only grunts when spoken to. Nico and his group will appear in the Firelink Shrine after the player defeats the Capra Demon and stay there until the player either exhausts all of their dialogue with both Vince and Reah, or until the player defeats Pinwheel at the Catacombs.

The player next encounters Nico as a Hollow in the Tomb of the Giants. He, along with Vince, must be defeated in order to rescue Reah from her predicament.
