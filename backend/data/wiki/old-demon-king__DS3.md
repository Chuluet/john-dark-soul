# Old Demon King (DS3)

The Old Demon King is an optional boss in Dark Souls III.

## Location

Found in the Smouldering Lake, in a chamber past the Carthus Sand Worm.

## Description

One of the many demons born from the Chaos Flame, the Old Demon King is now among the last of the dying race still existing in the world. It resides in a large chamber surrounded by the corpses of Capra Demons , Stray Demons and a Centipede Demon.

## Lore

The Old Demon King was one of the many demons born from the Chaos Flame, and the last remaining demon to have been alive before the Chaos of Izalith was extinguished, making it one of the oldest living beings in existence by the time of Dark Souls III. After the destruction of the Bed of Chaos, the line of demons began to decline with no means of maintaining it. In spite of this mass extinction, the Demon King persisted until the events of Dark Souls III, along with the Demon in Pain and Demon from Below (one of which would be reborn as the Demon Prince before ultimately dying). With the Chaos Flame extinguished, the Demon King has slowly withered away. When the King's health falls low enough, its inner fire will extinguish and it will kneel over helplessly, gasping and exhausted, perhaps hinting at its truly ancient age.

## Summons

Great Swamp Cuculus: Her summon sign can be found near the staircase leading to the boss arena.
Knight Slayer Tsorig: If the player defeated Tsorig as a Dark Spirit in the Catacombs of Carthus, he can be summoned to help with this boss fight. His summon sign can be found near the Demon Ruins Bonfire (Defeating him again in the Smouldering Lake does not negate this).
