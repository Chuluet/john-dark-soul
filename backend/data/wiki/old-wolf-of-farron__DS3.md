# Old Wolf of Farron (DS3)

The Old Wolf of Farron is a character in Dark Souls III.

## Location

Deep inside the swamp of the Farron Keep, atop a tower protected by a group of Rotten Slugs at the base. There is a ladder that will lead straight up to the Old Wolf's room.

## Description

The Old Wolf of Farron is the leader of the Watchdogs of Farron and inducts the player into their ranks. He bestows their medallion the first time he is interacted upon. Once within the covenant, the player can rank up and receive rewards by offering Wolf's Blood Swordgrasses to him.

Although the wolf appears to be dead, he is actually asleep. He bleeds when struck and is said to be emaciated.

The wolf cannot be fought or killed.
