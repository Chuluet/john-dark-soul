# Old Woman of Londor (DS3)

The Old Woman of Londor is a character in Dark Souls III.

She is voiced by Jenny Funnel, who also voiced the Karla in the same game, Yuria and Selen Vinland in Demon's Souls, the Darkmoon Knightess and Quelana in Dark Souls, and Annalise and Iosefa in Bloodborne.

## Location

Irithyll of the Boreal Valley
Found concealed as a statue in one of the corners of the Church of Yorshka, only if the player is following Anri's/Yuria's questline.
Awaits at the entrance of the Darkmoon Tomb if following Anri's/Yuria's questline and yields the player the Sword of Avowal to consummate the ritual. She is found dead on the way back, holding the Chameleon sorcery.

## Questline

If the player is following either Anri or Yuria's questline, the Old Woman will first appear in the Church of Yorshka in Irithyll of the Boreal Valley. There, she will be disguised as a statue via use of the Chameleon spell, where she can be interacted with, ignored, or killed. Killing her or leaving her alive each yield their own consequences.
Should the player leave the Old Woman alive, Anri's questline will end and the Old Woman will next appear outside the Darkmoon Tomb, under Anor Londo. She will present the player with the Sword of Avowal and urge them inside the tomb. Upon using the sword and exiting the tomb, her corpse will lay where she stood, and on it will be the Chameleon sorcery.
