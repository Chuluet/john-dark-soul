# Oscar, Knight of Astora (DS1)

Oscar, Knight of Astora (アストラのオスカル) is a character in Dark Souls. He is voiced by Oliver Le Sueur.

An undead knight from Astora, Oscar appears in the opening scene, freeing the player character from their cell in the Undead Asylum and therefore kickstarting their quest. He can be found mortally wounded shortly after, as the first character in the game that can be talked to, wishing to impart the character with his dying words and provide them with the all-important Estus Flask.

## Appearance

Oscar is equipped with the Elite Knight Set, a Crest Shield, and an Astora's Straight Sword.

## Plot

Oscar appears in the Undead Asylum, where he rescues the player character from imprisonment by dropping a corpse with the Dungeon Cell Key into the cell. He is later found dying, and asks the player to listen to his tale before he expires. If this request is granted, he will tell the Chosen Undead of a prophecy stating that an undead will one day travel to Lordran from the Undead Asylum and ring the Bells of Awakening (which he refers to as a singular bell, unaware that there are two). At the conclusion of his tale, he will give the player an Estus Flask and the Undead Asylum F2 East Key (as well as the Big Pilgrim's Key if the player has defeated the Asylum Demon during their first encounter with the boss), after which he states that he can die with hope in his heart. If the player talks to him again after this, Oscar will request for them to leave him to die alone, afraid that he might hurt the player character after turning hollow after death.

If the player answers "No" to his question (refusing to hear his tale and request), the only way to obtain the Estus Flask and the keys is to kill Oscar or rest at a bonfire. Despite being hurt by the player's refusal, Oscar still gracefully requests them to leave so that he does not hurt them after turning hollow.

Oscar is encountered again in the same area upon revisiting the Asylum, now a hollow enemy.

## Lore

Oscar was likely a high-ranking knight from Astora, who was determined to seek out Lordran once he turned undead. Exactly how he ended up in the Undead Asylum and why he rescues the player is unknown, though it is implied that he was intentionally releasing undead from the Asylum in hopes that at least one would ring the Bells of Awakening.

There is a hole in the ceiling at the location he is first encountered, and since the Asylum Demon first appears descending upon the Chosen Undead to guard the exit from any undead hoping to escape, it can be assumed that Oscar had fought the Asylum Demon on the roof of the Asylum, but wound up crashing down into the squalid dungeon the player encounters him in.

It is implied that the ensuing despair he felt at being unable to even overcome his first obstacle as an undead is what turned him Hollow.
