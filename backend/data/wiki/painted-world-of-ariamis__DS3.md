# Painted World of Ariamis (DS3)

The Painted World of Ariamis likely rotted and was set on fire in order to be restored. This is a usual cycle within painted worlds. The history of Painted World is no longer known in Lothric. It is taught only in the mythology of heretics, passed down just among them, and in ancient tales of hunchbacked heretics. Those heretics are Corvian Storytellers, spreading tales of Painted World among forlorn ones. They are wretched beings with no place to go, just like their followers, Corvians, the unwanted ones possessed by fear. Some of them wield Great Corvian Scythes, visually resembling the one used by the mistress of the Painted World and causing bleeding. It is a reference to Priscilla's scythe rather than Friede's, mistress of the Painted World of Ariandel.

Company Captain Yorshka is speculated to be the daughter of Crossbreed Priscilla.
