# Pygmy Lord (DS3)

The  is a character in the Dark Souls III: The Ringed City DLC.

He is voiced by Ramon Tikaram, who also voiced the Ringed City Pygmy in Dark Souls III, as well as Godrick the Grafted and the Mad Pumpkin Heads in Elden Ring and Elden Ring Nightreign.

## Location

He is encountered in the Ringed City after awakening Princess Filianore, in Filianore's Rest.

## Plot

The Pygmy Lord is found dragging his broken body up a dune in the ashen desert accessed after interacting with Filianore. He exists largely to point towards the location where Slave Knight Gael's fight can be initiated, adding some anticipation and flavor to the ensuing encounter. He drops nothing upon death, and will die on his own once he makes it near the doors to Filianore's ruined chamber.

## Lore

This Pygmy Lord is a member of the royalty within the Ringed City, who directly inherited the Dark Soul and protected it within themselves, being related to the Furtive Pygmy in some way.

Lord Gwyn gifted them the city as a sort of peace offering, as well as his daughter Filianore, who would eventually slumber, preserving the city and keeping the Dark at bay. They ruled the city in a rather adequate manner, with the exception of a madman who would rise among the royalty. He would be dispatched by Shira, Knight of Filianore, who failed to kill him for good, and thus secluded herself inside a darkroom where she and the cross spear that had impaled the undying king would be held close until the end of time, preventing him from causing havoc.

As primordial beings predating most modern humans, they were able to better resist the effects of aging thanks to the Dark Soul that kept them alive to the point that their very blood dried out. The wizened Slave Knight Gael, who sought the Dark Soul with the goal of taking it to his Lady, devoured them and their viscous blood rich of the Dark Soul, even though he was quite aware of the negative effects it would inflict, for he was not a champion fit to receive its full power. 

As the last survivor of Gael's massacre, he attempts to make it to Filianore's remains even with his throat slashed by Gael, and his legs bleeding and broken. The Ashen One would pass by the pitiful soul, who would never get to see the princess' final state, succumbing to his wounds as he called out her name in vain.
