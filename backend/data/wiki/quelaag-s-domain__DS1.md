# Quelaag's Domain (DS1)

right|300px
Quelaag's Domain is a short area in Dark Souls located between the base of Blighttown and the Demon Ruins.

## Description

Numerous eggs of humanity are housed in Quelaag's Domain, the web-filled tunnels leading in and out of the area. Inside is the arena where Quelaag is fought and where her sister nests by her bonfire. The second Bell of Awakening is also found immediately after the fight with Quelaag.

## Adjacent locations

Blighttown
Demon Ruins
Lost Izalith (only available after activating the elevator shortcut from Lost Izalith)

## Covenant

Chaos Servant - The covenant is located down the flight of stairs next to the Bell of Awakening, behind an illusory wall. Beyond this wall is Quelaag's Sister, the leader of the Chaos Servant covenant.

## Characters

Eingyi (merchant) - Behind an illusory wall, guarding Quelaag's Sister.
Quelaag's Sister - With Eingyi behind the illusory wall. Can initiate players into the Chaos Servant covenant.
