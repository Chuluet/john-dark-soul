# Rapacious Andrei (general)

thumb|290x290px
Rapacious Andrei is a phantom in . He is summonable for the Cerah, Varg, and Afflicted Graverobber boss fight.

## Overview

He can apply Gold Pine Resin to his weapon.
He's armed with Witching Urns.
Can heal himself via Estus.

## Location

Cave of the Dead
His summon sign can be found in the first room of the area, near a tombstone.
