# Rapier Champion (DS3)

The Rapier Champion is an enemy character in Dark Souls III.

## Locations

Summoned infinitely by the second Man Serpent Summoner in Archdragon Peak, in a room located after the big open area with the second Ancient Wyvern and before reaching the Great Belfry.

## Description

An enemy deprived of most clothing, although he makes up for in brawniness what he lacks in apparel. He can only be summoned by the second Man Serpent Summoner in Archdragon Peak, shortly before reaching the Great Belfry.

## Equipment

Ricard's Rapier
Wolnir's Crown
Cornyx's Wrap
Loincloth
