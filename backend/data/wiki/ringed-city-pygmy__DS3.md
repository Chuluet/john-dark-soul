# Ringed City Pygmy (DS3)

The Ringed City Pygmy, or Ringed City Hollow, is a character in the Dark Souls III: The Ringed City DLC.

He is voiced by Ramon Tikaram, who also voiced the Pygmy Lord in Dark Souls III, as well as Godrick the Grafted and the Mad Pumpkin Heads in Elden Ring and Elden Ring Nightreign.

## Location

He is encountered at the entrance of the Ringed City, just before the Mausoleum Lookout bonfire.

## Plot

The Ringed City Pygmy is the first character the player will meet when making their way past the Demon Prince. He doesn't have much of a purpose outside of pointing the player towards a new objective once they've finally reached the fabled city, which is to disturb Princess Filianore's slumber. He mainly serves an expository role.

He will disappear completely once the player does what he suggested and Filianore is awakened.

## Lore

Not much is known about this enigmatic Pygmy. His appearance resembles that of the Pygmy Lords, as he wears a tatterd version of their clothes and has their same long white hair. All he is missing is their signature crown, something that was seemingly gifted to them by Gwyn and the Gods, which he very openly despises.

He believes that the Ashen One found their way to the city in an attempt to dispell Filianore's illusion and get their hands on the Dark Soul, no matter what they say about their own intentions. He directs them to the ends of the city, claiming to be their friend and ally and vanishing once the deed is done.

## Theories

Some people speculate that the Ringed City Pygmy is actually the fabled Furtive Pygmy. This isn't based on much other than his mysterious demeanor, fairly unassuming presence, and subsequent disappearance from the game. His similarities to the city's rulers, as well as his surprisingly beefy health bar only serve to fuel this specific conclusion.

This is all just speculation though, to be taken with a grain of salt.
