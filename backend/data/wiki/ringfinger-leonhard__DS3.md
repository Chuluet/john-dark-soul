# Ringfinger Leonhard (DS3)

Ringfinger Leonhard is a character and enemy in Dark Souls III. He is voiced by Edward Bennett.

## Locations

Firelink Shrine — Found leaning against the central (Lothric's) throne

First appearance after exploring the High Wall of Lothric

Second appearance after coming into possession of a Pale Tongue

Cathedral of the Deep (after Red Eye Orb has been obtained)

Leaning on the wall just inside the entrance to Rosaria's Bed Chamber, after offering 1 Pale Tongue to Rosaria (no area reload required)

## Description

Ringfinger Leonhard is a loyal knight and servant of Rosaria, Mother of Rebirth. He is a member of the covenant Rosaria's Fingers, along with Heysel, Creighton and Kirk (as well as the Ashen One should they choose to follow his questline). He introduces the player to PvP and the invasion mechanic by gifting them several Cracked Red Eye Orbs.

## Lore

Born into royalty, where he learned the arts of sorcery and swordsmanship, Leonhard received horrid burns across his entire body through unmentioned circumstances. His face in particular was scalded terribly, and he wore a Silver Mask to conceal this.

Some time later, seeking a way to heal his scarred body through rebirth, he arrived at the Cathedral of the Deep. When he finally met Rosaria, Mother of Rebirth, he abstained from healing his burns for unknown reasons, instead taking vows to serve Rosaria and inheriting a crescent sword imbued with the power of the moon.

## Questline

Leonhard first appears in Firelink Shrine after either lighting the Tower on the Wall bonfire in the High Wall of Lothric or defeating Vordt of the Boreal Valley. He will give the player five Cracked Red Eye Orbs and encourage them to invade other players to collect Embers. He will then leave Firelink Shrine the next time it is reloaded.

After defeating the Curse-rotted Greatwood or lighting the Cliff Underside bonfire in the Undead Settlement, Leonhard will return to his place next to Lothric's throne should the player be in possession of a Pale Tongue. When spoken to, he will give the player the Lift Chamber Key, encouraging them to kill the Darkwraith locked up in Lothric's dungeon.

The key is used to open a locked door in the High Wall of Lothric underneath the Tower on the Wall bonfire. Killing the Darkwraith at the bottom of the elevator will reward the player with a full Red Eye Orb. When spoken to afterward, Leonhard will congratulate the player and teach them the "Applause" gesture, before he encourages them to join Rosaria's Fingers.

Once the player deepens devotion to Rosaria's Fingers by offering a Pale Tongue to Rosaria, Mother of Rebirth (as opposed to using the attribute reallocation or appearance change services), Leonhard will appear near the entrance to her chamber, where he briefly greets the player. Note that offering a Pale Tongue to Rosaria will cause the questline of Sirris of the Sunless Realms to fail; in order to reap the rewards of both quests, Sirris' questline must be completed first, followed by Leonhard's.

When the player reaches the Profaned Capital past Irithyll Dungeon, Leonhard becomes paranoid that they will try and steal Rosaria's soul, and murders her before fleeing to Anor Londo. The player can find a Black Eye Orb on Rosaria's corpse, which can be used to invade Leonhard's world in the chamber that once contained the illusion of Gwynevere, Princess of Sunlight.

Upon defeat, Leonhard will drop the Soul of Rosaria, his Silver Mask and the Crescent Moon Sword; the remainder of his set then becomes available to purchase from the Shrine Handmaid. The questline ends at this point, as the player can choose to either return Rosaria's Soul to her corpse, consume it for souls, or transpose it with Ludleth of Courland. Since Rosaria's rebirth and covenant services are unaffected by her status, restoring her soul to the body will have no mechanical benefit or yield any rewards for doing so.
