# Rosabeth of Melfia (DS2)

is a character and merchant in Dark Souls II.

She is voiced by Carina Reeves, who also voices one of the Milfanito.

## Location

She is encountered as a petrified statue near Majula, blocking the door to the Shaded Woods. To find her, backtrack towards Things Betwixt from the village's bonfire, and then enter the first cave on the right.

After being unpetrified and given spare clothing, she will move in front of the tall monument in Majula, remaining there for the rest of the game.

## Plot

Rosabeth initially appears as an obstacle, keeping the player from entering the Shaded Woods until they unpetrify her. The player must use a Fragrant Branch of Yore to do so, a rare item found across Drangleic. After being saved, she will thank the player and give them a Prism Stone as a gift. 

With that done, the way forward will be revealed, but it's not fully advised to open the door immediately after freeing her. Doing that will trap both the player and Rosabeth in the enclosed room while many Goblins are released into it, which could lead to her accidental death. For that reason it is best to either move her to Majula right away, or simply leave the area and come back, which will cause her to stand outside, next to Benhart of Jugo. In the Scholar of the First Sin edition, it's easier to save her, as there are some barrels filled with gunpowder nearby. Making them explode will make all the Goblins come out from their cells without closing the entrance of the structure, allowing the player to deal with them before freeing Rosabeth from her petrification.

Saving Rosabeth will allow her to start selling items and upgrading Pyromancy Flames. Unlike in Dark Souls, where Pyromancy Flames could be upgraded through souls only, the Dark Souls II version requires both Fire Seeds and souls. Thankfully, some can be bought from Rosabeth herself, but they can also be found throughout Drangleic. Freeing Rosabeth gives access to many options regarding pyromancies, with access to a variety of useful early game spells. However, she won't give the player a Pyromancy Flame, unlike with Laurentius in the first game.

To have her go to Majula, the player must first fulfill her request to be to given any spare clothing, as her current dress is in tatters. This allows the player to give her any piece of armor they have, and if they do so she will move to Majula, next to the staircase of the village's monument, wearing whatever was given to her. This will also reward the player with the "Change of Clothes" Achievement. Once moved to the hamlet, she will offer all of her previous services, but she will also still accept new clothing should the player decide to give them to her. However, it must be noted that any given piece of armor can't be taken back, so one must be mindful of what they decide to give her.

## Lore

Rosabeth is a gifted pyromancy student from the southern land of Melfia, once enrolled in its prestigious Magic Academy and under the tutelage of Pyromancer Glocken. Despite her calling for pyromancy, however, she had a great fascination with sorcery, which led her to seek the tutelage of the famed sorcerer Carhillion of the Fold, another teacher of the academy.

When Carhillion tired of his colleagues of the Melfian Magic Academy, he set off on a journey to Drangleic, believing it to be a place where the forces of magic and souls laid dormant. And so, Rosabeth went after him, and accompanied him for a portion of the journey. The two got separated pretty early on though, and the pupil made much of the way on her own while trying to track her own teacher down, leading to many dangerous situations and near-death encounters. However, her luck would run out when she was petrified, presumably by one of the Basilisks lurking in the Shaded Woods.

Thankfully for her, the Bearer of the Curse would find and release her from her horrible state with a Fragrant Branch of Yore. She will be extremely grateful for their help, though greatly embarrassed due to the state of her attire. After a change of clothing provided by her benefactor, she'll move to Majula, in hopes of meeting up with her teacher there. She will begin to wonder if Carhillion hadn't simply ditched her after she first lost track of him, though in truth the old master hoped she was safe and wondered where she disappeared to.

Even should they reunite in Majula, Rosabeth will decide to practice on her own, wishing to see what she can achieve by herself.

## Equipment

Note that the armor will change depending on the equipment you have given her. However, this will not increase her defense, as it is merely a cosmetic change.  There are a few exceptions to this, like the Moon Butterfly Set, which will have her inflict poison on anyone who comes near her, and the Symbol of Avarice, which will slowly kill her.

## Bugs

There is a bug on the Xbox 360, PS3 and PC where Rosabeth may vanish from the player's current game forever and for no reason. After she is un-petrified and gear is given to her, she might not appear in Majula. A Bonfire Ascetic won't even bring her back. This isn't to be confused with accidentally killing an NPC, where their grave will appear for re-summon. After un-petrifying her, rest at the Majula bonfire and check if she has appeared. If not, quit to main menu and try loading. There is another pyromancy trainer, Carhillion of the Fold, but he will only upgrade your flame, he won't sell Flash Sweat, etc.
