# Rosaria, Mother of Rebirth (DS3)

For the questline associated with the character, see Ringfinger Leonhard's.

Rosaria, Mother of Rebirth is a special character in Dark Souls III.

Found in an isolated part of the Cathedral of the Deep, she is the leader of the Rosaria's Fingers covenant. In addition, she can allow the player to re-allocate their attributes or alter their physical appearance, in exchange for a Pale Tongue.

## Location

Found in the Cathedral of the Deep, in front of the Rosaria's Bed Chamber bonfire.

## Description

When encountered, she will silently allow access into her covenant and will receive Pale Tongues as offerings to raise ranks while being a member.
She also allows the player to reallocate attributes and change their appearance up to five times in a single playthrough.

## Lore

Rosaria is a descendant of Gwynevere, Princess of Sunlight and is an Irithyllian, hybrids between the race of the gods of Anor Londo and humans who inhabit Irithyll of the Boreal Valley, the city built for them on the ruins of Anor Londo. She once possessed a Crescent Moon Sword infused with the power of the moon worshipped by the nobles of Irithyll as Darkmoon Gwyndolin had become the king of the gods and ruler of their new city.

Rosaria is the mysterious mute leader of the Rosaria's Fingers covenant, whose numerous followers consider her a goddess. Archdeacon Klimt in particular defected from the Aldrich Faithful, instead choosing to serve Rosaria. Rosaria's followers collect tongues to either be "reborn" by the goddess in exchange, or simply to bring her comfort. It is said that Rosaria, the mother of rebirth, was robbed of her tongue by her firstborn, and has been waiting for their return ever since.
