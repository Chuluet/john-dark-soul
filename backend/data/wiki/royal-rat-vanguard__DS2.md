# Royal Rat Vanguard (DS2)

The Royal Rat Vanguard is a boss in Dark Souls II. Found in the Grave of Saints, he is optional, and one of two bosses with the Royal Rat Authority whose slaying gives access to the Rat King covenant.

His atypical boss battle features many regular Corpse Rats swarming the player; after some time, the Vanguard, who is another Rat of similar size but slightly different appearance, will join the fight, with his health bar becoming visible on-screen. He must be singled out and hunted, as only killing him will win the fight and the other Rats will otherwise spawn endlessly.

## Location

The Royal Rat Vanguard is fought in the Grave of Saints, past the second bonfire.

This location is also very useful to farm souls before killing the boss, as the smaller rats continue to spawn and grant a fair amount of souls each. It should be noted that killing the boss will stop rats from spawning in the area as when the boss was alive.

## Description

The Royal Rat Vanguard has a very similar appearance and size to the rest of the rats fought in the room. However, it has a prominent crest of hair on its back.

## Plot

The Royal Rat Vanguard is one of the many loyal subjects of the Rat King, and serves as a test to prove the Bearer of the Curse's worthiness to him.

## Attacks

The boss itself and the normal rats share the same attacks.
Rearing Bite: The rat rears back and bites. Inflicts poison buildup.
Lunging Bite: The rat leaps forward and bites. Inflicts poison buildup.

## Defenses

All Rats in the game have the below stats except that physical defense changes slightly, and the Mongrel Rat has only 50% poison resistance.

All rats except the boss have 30% curse resist. The boss has 100% curse resistance. (Stats are from Param Studio and are not estimated)
