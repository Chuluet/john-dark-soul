# Seath the Scaleless (DS1)

Seath the Scaleless, also known as Duke Seath, is a boss in Dark Souls. He is also a mentioned character in Dark Souls II, albeit only as the "Paledrake", and a mentioned character in Dark Souls III.

One of four great beings who must be slain in Dark Souls so that their special souls may satiate the Lordvessel, he uniquely has two distinct boss battles, each with a different location and moveset. In another unique occurence, the first one atop The Duke's Archives must result in the player's death for the game to progress, while he can actually be defeated in his true boss battle at the end of the Crystal Cave.

## Location

Encountered at the top of The Duke's Archives, and later at the end of the Crystal Cave.

## Description

Seath is a large white dragon that lacks scales and has his lower body covered in crystals. He has a smooth torso that is quite humanoid and in place of legs he has several tentacle-like tendrils. He has six blue-violet wings and has long appendices extending from his head.

Seath is one of the primordial archdragons who inhabited the world when, before the appearance of the First Flame, the world was composed only of grey rocks.

Seath was born with unique characteristics compared to the other archdragons, lacking the rock scales that made his brethren immortal. In addition, Seath's pale and deformed body was infused with the magical power of moonlight, through the study of which he created weapons that catalyzed it.

At some point, the Four Lords who had found immense Lord Souls and built their civilizations underground emerged to the surface with the intention of waging war against the Dragons and conquering the world for themselves. Seath decided to betray his race to ally with Gwyn, Lord of Sunlight, and help the Lords in the war. He was instrumental in the gods' eventual victory, and the triumphant Lords would exterminate much of the Ancient Dragons, cementing their rule. Seath would also pillage the Ancient Dragons' sacred treasure, the Primordial Crystal, the centerpiece of his efforts to achieve immortality.

Through their successful alliance against the Everlasting Dragons, Seath became a close friend to the Lord of Sunlight and was amply rewarded for his indispensable contributions to the war. He was wed into Gwyn's family as his son-in-law, inducting him into the nobility of the Gods, and was bestowed the honorific of "Duke". Gwyn further gifted Seath a fragment of his powerful Lord Soul and the Royal Archives, an enormous library towering above Anor Londo, which contained all the knowledge available to the city of the gods, leaving the dragon the freedom to do his own research; in time, the archives became so synonymous with Seath that they became known as "The Duke's Archives" instead.

Seath eventually sired a humanoid hybrid daughter, Shira. Although the mother is never stated explicitly, Shira is both "one whose veins coarse with royal blood", and a "descendant of Gods", making her likely mother Seath's spouse who was a relative of Gwyn.
Shira became the sworn knight and personal handmaiden to Princess Filianore, Gwyn's last daughter, and the two, together with the dragon Darkeater Midir, would be forever sent away to the Ringed City, the hidden city Gwyn gifted to the humans who helped him end the Age of Ancients, so that the three would keep the Dark within at bay. Shira would remain in the Ringed City for the rest of her life, dutifully guarding Filianore over countless ages, although she would always take pride in being Seath's daughter.

Another possible daughter of Seath was Crossbreed Priscilla, a half-dragon hybrid whose dragon attributes are reminiscent of him; as she is outright described as a bastard, she would have been conceived out of wedlock. Priscilla was feared by the other gods and treated as an abomination due to her Lifehunt powers and unnatural origin. Eventually she was drawn away into the cold and lonely Painted World of Ariamis, trapped within a great canvas.

Seath seemingly had little interest in politics or pleasure, choosing to immerse himself in research of the scales of immortality that the ancient dragons possessed but he lacked. His Archives became a magnificent pool of tomes and knowledge, the fruits of Seath's superior wisdom and unsound fixation over achieving the immortality that was denied to him at birth.

Seath's research focused on souls, seeking ways to manipulate them and draw out the power within, mainly through sorcery. Seath, in possession of the Primordial Crystal, noticed the peculiar connection between crystals and souls, and the way in which this element could be used to best catalyze their power, as crystals represented an ideal medium to create a bond between souls and objects. Seath would thus pioneer the Crystal Sorceries, a powerful branch of sorcery. The revelation was the beginning of Seath's obsession with crystals, which became the focus of much of his experiments. He managed to create a Crystal Ember capable of infusing weapons with the power of the crystals and passed this knowledge on to the Giant Blacksmith of the gods.

The pale dragon's experiments were successful in animating Crystal Golems with souls and infusing Undead Soldiers with crystals. To adequately supply his research, the cave that housed his Primordial Crystal was gradually crowded with crystals, nearly overtaking the Archive's inner gardens. Seath also created the soul of the Moonlight Butterflies, mystical creatures that emitted crystallic power and Seath's moonlight as sorceries. Seath also managed to crystallize his own magical power of moonlight, creating a Greatsword that catalyzed it.

As a nobleman on par with the gods, Seath also had personal servants. His Channelers, trained sorcerers that became his eyes, hands, and feet, would reach and see what the dragon could not, and helped him in his research. The Channelers wore outfits representing Seath, with the six eyes on their helms meant to "compensate for Seath's lack of sight"; the nature of his lack of sight, and when he became impaired, are unknown.

At the culmination of his quest, Seath was able to achieve what he had always sought: immortality through the Primordial Crystal. By connecting his body and soul to the powerful crystal, the pale dragon gained insulation from death, so that no mortal blow could affect him and his wounds would close promptly, as long as the crystal was intact. His very breath became a powerful crystal breath that emitted a lethal curse.

Although the white dragon achieved his prized immortality, the arduous process had consumed him. The loneliness within him, the bane of his existence since his conception as a malformed dragon, was only fueled by the countless hours wiled away in solitude seeking his "cure"; the void within him was tragically left unfulfilled. The power and status he had since attained remained hollow, against the envy and resentment he harbored first towards his brethren, and then the world at large. His experiments grew more and more twisted as the inescapable reality of his condition drove him insane. The Archives were soon reduced to a prison housing the abominations of Seath such as the Serpent-men, unwitting jailers of volumes of scientific works, and the Pisaca, serpentine women who were once holy maidens of Gwynevere, before Seath's experiments transformed them. Although Gwyndolin, as the last remaining deity in Anor Londo, did not punish him for this heinous act, Seath was confined to his Archives, declared a forbidden area and demarcated by a divine magical barrier, so that no one could approach the Archives. Meanwhile his Channelers, steadfast in their service of the Duke, continued their travels, kidnapping subjects for his experiments.

At some point after Gwyn linked the First Flame and Gwyn's firstborn son was dethroned and exiled, nearly all of the other deities abandoned Anor Londo and moved to an unknown location. Seath remained in his Archives, now completely estranged from the world and obsessed with his research.

When a Chosen Undead was able to ring the Bells of Awakening, overcome the challenges of Sen's Fortress and reach Anor Londo thus being rewarded with the Lordvessel, they would be guided by Kingseeker Frampt to find immense souls to fill the receptacle, and link the fading First Flame to succeed Gwyn in his sacrifice. The Chosen Undead would reach the Duke's Archives, where they were initially defeated and imprisoned by the immortal dragon. Managing to escape, the Chosen Undead would cross the dangerous Archives and reach the Crystal Cave where the Primordial Crystal was kept, source of the pale dragon's invulnerability. Seath would soon arrive at the cave to defend his crystal, but following the crystal's destruction would be slain by the Chosen Undead, who gained possession of Seath's fragment of the Lord Soul.

## Strategies

When first encountered in The Duke's Archives, he regenerates health extremely quickly due to the power of Primordial Crystal, effectively making him invincible.. He inevitably kills the player. Loss of souls and humanity can be avoided by wearing a Ring of Sacrifice, but if he kills the player through Curse, then a Rare Ring of Sacrifice is needed to avoid penalties. In addition, it is possible to travel back through the fog door to avoid being killed by Seath, however can still go through the fog wall after exiting. Regardless, the player must be killed by Seath in order to progress.

Upon encountering him again within the Crystal Cave, he will be fought within an arena that contains the Primordial Crystal. In order to defeat Seath, it is necessary to destroy the Primordial Crystal so that any damage dealt to him is not immediately healed. The crystal will respawn if the player dies or leaves the fight, making it necessary to destroy it in each attempt to defeat Seath.

Staying at his side makes it easier to stay out of the area-of-effect of his breath attacks, and he won't be able to hit with his claws or tail as easily. Video

It is also possible to use only a bow against Seath with great success. Get a good distance away from him and it is possible to get 4-5 shots in before he gets too close. The only real danger in this strategy is when running past Seath to gain some distance.

His only long range specific attack is the far curse beam. That means if players stay at a distance, one can lure him to fire the beam and charge his defenseless body.

Crystal curse explosion triggers when the player cannot be reached by any of Seath's other attacks; usually when situated behind Seath with the tails just out of reach. One strategy is to begin sprinting a good distance away from him once he arches forward to charge a ball of magic. A second approach would be to develop a high enough curse resistance and block some of the erupting crystals. Immediately heal the damage received before he executes his tail swipes/slams.

Being the grandfather of Sorcery, Seath is extremely resistant to Magic damage, so it would be wise to try to use a different element when combating him.

When trying to obtain the Moonlight Greatsword it is best to attack the tip of the tail, as the area around midway through the tail is considered his body. Thus dealing damage to this area will not help in acquiring the Moonlight Greatsword.

A good strategy for acquiring it is when Seath himself breaks the Primordial Crystal. Wait behind the crystal until Seath starts to rear up his head for any of the Curse Beam attacks. Just before he fires it, start sprinting left or right around the sides and towards his tail. Seath will break the crystal and stun himself, giving the player ample opportunity to attack his tail three to four times. Make sure Seath's tail isn't clipping in the wall before trying.

It should be noted that Seath takes extra damage if hit in his belly. As an example, when hit with standard 1H fast attack Man-serpent Greatsword +15 in of his tentacles at STR 40, Seath takes 226 damage. And if hit in his belly by the same attack, he will take about 450 damage. If the character is tanky enough and has enough Curse resistance, he can stay right at Seath's belly and hit him without pausing until Seath dies, using his Estus Flask only one or two times.

## Moonlight Greatsword

To cut off a tail it's necessary to deal a certain amount of damage against it. The HP of the tail increases with higher NG cycles and is based on the HP of the respective enemy. Seath's tail has 825 health on Ng and 1,295 on NG+.
Equipment load under 25% is highly recommended to cut his tail.
Lure Seath as close as possible to the crystal. It is ideal to kite Seath close to one of the walls, so that when he attacks with his breath, you have plenty of room to move around his other side. Once Seath is close enough to you he will pause and begin his breath attack; immediately run behind Seath. Run to his left side (your right), as he breathes his crystal breath from his right to his left. Seath will destroy the crystal with his own breath attack and be stunned temporarily. Without locking on to him, target the tip of his back (middle) tail and attack it while two-handing your highest damage weapon or using fast strong spells.
Use weapons that have vertical slash when two-handed: katanas, straightswords, greatswords, ultra greatswords, etc. Slash the tip once or twice, then run away. Provoke him to attack with his crystal breath, run to his tail, slash once or twice again, then repeat.
You can also use fast spells to cut his tail. Unlike using weapons, it is recommended to retreat from the boss battle (Homeward, Homeward Bone, or quit game) if you fail to cut it on the first try after breaking the crystal.

The first of Seath's Curse Beams: Seath will fire a beam from his mouth in a circular arc close to his body, and crystals will erupt from the ground around where the beam touched.

## Breath Crystal

Attack TypeParryableTrackingSpeed72000StrikeCurse: 2NoBothMedium
The crystals that erupts form the ground after each Curse Beam attack, they are unblockable and deal continuous damage, as well as curse build up.

## Crystal Curse Explosion

Attack TypeParryableTrackingSpeed14448000StrikeNoNeitherMedium
Seath's most dangerous attack: he will begin charging briefly, and will then erupt crystals from his body in all directions. This crystal eruption is even more damaging than the others and, of course, much harder to evade. The only way to avoid it is to get quite far away from Seath's body, out of range of the eruption.

## Tail Thrash

Attack TypeParryableTrackingSpeed612000StrikeNoNeitherMedium
Seath will attack with his tails if the player stays near one of his three tails for too long. There are two variations of this attack, one where Seath repeatedly slams his tails onto the ground, and the other where he sweeps them. These can hit multiple times, stunlocking the player. This attack has considerable range, but it can be reduced by cutting off Seath's tail.

## Far Cursebeam

Attack TypeParryableTrackingSpeed057000StrikeNoNeitherMedium
The same effect as the Close Curse Beam, except this is fired in a circular arc further away from Seath's body.

## Straight Cursebeam

Attack TypeParryableTrackingSpeed057500StrikeNoNeitherMedium
The last of the different Curse Beam types, Seath fires a beam in a straight line from the base of his body to the end of the room, in the direction the player is standing.

## Left or Right Hand Swipe

Attack TypeParryableTrackingSpeed624000StrikeNoRightMedium
Seath will swipe his left or right hand at the player. This is incredibly damaging and can only be blocked by high stability shields, but fairly easy to dodge since his hand hitbox is small.
