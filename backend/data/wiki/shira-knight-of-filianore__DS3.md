# Shira, Knight of Filianore (DS3)

Shira, Knight of Filianore is a character and summonable phantom in the Dark Souls III: The Ringed City DLC. At a certain stage of the DLC, she will permanently turn hostile.

She is voiced by Poppy Drayton.

## Locations

She is first encountered behind a large door, which is found at the top of the stairs above the Ringed City Streets swamp, patrolled by several Harald Legion Knights.

Shira's summon sign is located inside the small shrine from which Darkeater Midir's arena can be accessed.

After the player has interacted with the slumbering Filianore, her previous locations will all become unavailable, as she will now be found exclusively within a small ruin in the ash-covered landscape accessed from the Filianore's Rest bonfire.

## Plot

Shira is one of the few friendly characters in the Ringed City. She stands behind a locked door and can be interacted from the other side. Upon telling her the name of Lord Gwyn she will warm up to the player's presence, and request of them the great favor of mercy-killing the afflicted Midir, a dragon that still guards the city and the princess from intruders. If the player accepts to aid her, she will give them the Sacred Chime of Filianore.

This interaction will also allow Shira to be summoned for the boss fight against said dragon, providing ranged support with her Lightning Arrow miracle. She tends to have a hard time keeping up with the beast's aggressive attacks however. Summoning her for the encounter or not has no bearing on the completion of her request, as she'll thank the player for granting Midir his deserved rest regardless. As a reward, she'll give out a Titanite Slab.

She will have some extra dialogue if the player has joined the Spears of the Church covenant, commending them on their choice to serve Princess Filianore. However, Shira will become permanently hostile if her slumber is disturbed, disappearing from all her previous locations, halting her involvement in the Midir sideplot. She will ambush the player in a delapitated ruin found in the ashen desert accessed from the Filianore's Rest bonfire. While battling the player, the Slave Knight Gael fight won't begin. Conversely, if his boss fight has already started, she will not show up at her spot. She'll still attack the player even after his defeat.

Slaying her here will net her Crucifix of the Mad King as well as any item she could have gifted but hadn't yet. The door from which you could speak to her before will also be open, revealing a corpse holding the entirety of her set.

## Lore

Shira is a descendant of the Gods, a child of royalty, and the daughter of a certain Duke, who goes unnamed. Her crown denotes her noble pedigree, made out of silver and adorned with a pearl retrieved from a dangerous Man Eater Shell. She appears to have been from the early age of the gods, as she makes use of a miracle of that era, issued to female knights. She was also the sworn knight and personal handmaiden of Princess Filianore, the youngest daughter of Gwyn.

The Lord of Sunlight would end up gifting the Pygmies with a magnificent city at the edge of the world, sealing the deal by relinquishing his beloved daughter too, who would remain there until the day he would return for her. Shira and her trusted friend, the dragon Midir, who was raised by the Gods and given the duty of battling the Dark, would follow their liege and guard the Ringed City, keeping those who would rouse her awake at bay. By the king's decree, the Judicator Giants and the Spears of the Church would follow suit, ensuring peace. Shira seemes to admire this order, especially those of them who would reach the rank of Divine Spear.

Shira would eventually have to quell a corruption that had been growing within the very city, caused by a mad king that had emerged among the other Pygmy Lords. When she tried to put him to rest, she realized that his body could not die, and he simply remained impaled to her cross spear, which began to be permeated with the king's dark power. As a result, Shira would bring her weapon to a darkroom, sealing the door shut and remaining by its side to ensure the king would remain put. Even in this prolonged captivity, the descendant of the Gods remained dignified, wearing her noble attire even though no one would see it.

Meanwhile, her friend Midir had been corroded by the powers of the Dark he had been battling. Although his strong sense of duty wouldn't let him cease his watch, the knight reluctantly concluded that the afflicted dragon had to die, for the Dark would eventually destroy everything that made him who he was, eroding him into nothing but a mad beast. She wished to let him pass on with his honor and sense of self still intact.

Shira and Midir continued to uphold their duties, even as the state of the city deteriorated over the countless years of the unnaturally prolonged Age of Fire. Even as the world began collapsing onto itself, the two stood dutiful, until the Ashen One follows the path of the world-ending spiral to the hidden city. The Unkindled, speaking the name of the ancient king Gwyn, manages to win the knight's trust, who takes the opportunity to enlist them to finally put Midir to rest, even offering her aid to accomplish that feat, leaving the darkroom she had secluded herself into just for the sake of releasing her friend from his duties.

The great weight lifted from Midir's shoulders is celebrated, but the respite is brief. In truth, the Ashen One was seeking Princess Filianore, following the trail that had been left by Gael. This was the one thing Shira had warned them not to do. Betrayed and filled with righteous fury, she attacks the Unkindled in the name of her heritage, her princess and her companion Midir. The champion of Ash would triumph, likely extinguishing the line of the Gods.

## Theories

It is incredibly likely that the Duke mentioned by Shira is none other than Seath the Scaleless himself. For one, he is the only prominent duke who is ever mentioned as part of the age of the gods, which would explain why Shira speaks of her parentage to a duke as if it were self-explanatory. Another clue may come from the pearl lodged in her crown, which was harvested from the Man Eater Clams. These creatures were found nesting in the Crystal Cave, part of Seath's domain, as well as the primordial Ash Lake, a place tied to the dragons.

Shira's descent from royalty and the Gods would also make sense, due to the original Dark Souls establishing that Seath had become part of Gwyn's family by becoming the consort of one of his female relatives. This would mean that she would be born of that marriage, thus retaining most of her divine heritage. Her friendship with Midir could also take on a new light, as if she had draconic descent, she'd be able to relate and even grow incredibly close to the great being with relative ease due to their partially shared roots.

Even the name has some thematic and etymological connection to Seath, as Shira resembles the Japanese word for "white", written as "白" and pronounced "shiro", a kanji that makes up part of the Duke's title of "White Dragon", written as "白竜". It doesn't stop there, though, as the name also shares an impressive connection with Priscilla's, as Shira's Japanese name "シラ" is part of the Crossbreed's Japanese name "プリシラ". Priscilla's connections to Seath come from her stark white coloration and her nature as a half-dragon, as crossbreeds were something the old Paledrake was highly interested in. This would make the two half-sisters, but not full ones, as Priscilla is stated to have been born of adultery, meaning they'd have two distinct mothers.

This is all just speculation, however, to be taken with a grain of salt.
