# Shrine Handmaid (DS3)

The Shrine Handmaid is a character and merchant in Dark Souls III.

She is voiced by Carolyn Pickles.

## Location

Found in Firelink Shrine, in the corridor past the Fire Keeper.

## Description

The handmaid sells diverse items to the player. Her stock can be upgraded in exchange for Umbral Ashes that can be found throughout the course of the game.

She claims that she's Undead and affected by shrine's curse, so there's no point in killing her (as can be understood from her dialogue). She respawns when killed, and doing such only affects prices, from this point higher (also earnings for the player will be lowered). Her prices will return to normal after the player absolves sin at the shrine to Velka.

However, her counterpart in Untended Graves can be killed. She drops the Priestess Ring. It is not clearly stated if its description can be connected to the Handmaid only. It can be bought, but its only drop is available after killing her, suggesting that the Priestess Ring is her personal belonging.
