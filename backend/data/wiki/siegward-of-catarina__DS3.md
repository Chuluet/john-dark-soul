# Siegward of Catarina (DS3)

Siegward of Catarina is a character in Dark Souls III. He is voiced by Miles Richardson, who also voiced Siegmeyer of Catarina and the Undead Male Merchant in Dark Souls.

## Locations

Undead Settlement (first encounter)
Cathedral of the Deep (second encounter)
Irithyll of the Boreal Valley (third encounter)
Profaned Capital (final encounter)

## Description

Siegward wears the Catarina Set and wields a Storm Ruler or a Zweihander along with the Pierce Shield.

## The Undead Settlement tower

Siegward can first be encountered taking a two-stage elevator up from the basement of the church in the Undead Settlement. He tells the player that he is trying to figure out how to get to the top of the tower, in order to speak with whoever is launching the giant arrows, but the elevator only goes down to the basement. Once the player takes the elevator in either direction, Siegward will relocate, having solved the mystery of the elevator with the player's assistance.

When the player is traveling between the ground level and the top of the tower, Siegward can be heard pondering during the descent, indicating that he has changed locations. There is a hidden platform that can be accessed by jumping off the elevator as it travels between floors, which leads to an upper level of the Undead Settlement.

Siegward can be encountered here, sitting on a ledge observing the Demon off in the distance. If the player goes down and engages it, Siegward will berate them but offer support in defeating it. When it is defeated, he will reward the player with a Siegbräu and the "Toast" gesture. If the player speaks to him again, they will learn the "Sleep" gesture as well.

## Trapped in the Cathedral of the Deep

After encountering Unbreakable Patches in the Cathedral of the Deep, the player can encounter Siegward stuck in a well in the Cathedral of the Deep, right outside of the Cleansing Chapel bonfire. As Patches has stolen his armor, the player must retrieve it in order to rescue Siegward. The armor can be obtained by purchasing it from Patches, or by killing him and looting it. If the player missed Patches's encounter in the Cathedral, they must trigger his appearance at Firelink Shrine. The triggering flag for Patches's encounter event is opening the large double doors to the rooftop mausoleum, so opening these doors will cause Patches to sell the armor at Firelink Shrine.

When the player returns the armor to Siegward, he will teach them the "Rejoice" gesture.

## Before Irithyll Dungeon

Next, Siegward will be found in Irithyll of the Boreal Valley, near the Distant Manor bonfire in the kitchen past the Sewer Centipedes. Upon talking to him, he will thank the player for their assistance at the Cathedral of the Deep, and gift them Emit Force, another Siegbräu and heal the player upon completion of the initial dialogue. He will also grant the "Toast" and "Sleep" gestures here if the player has not previously obtained them.

While in this location, Siegward will rescue Greirat of the Undead Settlement if the latter has been sent to pillage Irithyll. However, once the player sets foot in Irithyll Dungeon, Siegward will immediately relocate and thus be unable to rescue Greirat anymore, which will result in his death unless Patches is capable of rescuing him.

## Jailed in Irithyll Dungeon

The last place Siegward can be found is in the Irithyll Dungeon, locked in a cell. In order to free him, the player must obtain the Old Cell Key and find the hidden entrance located in Profaned Capital to his cell to unlock it; there is an opening near the Court Sorcerer that can be reached by jumping from the rooftop. Within the cell is the Covetous Gold Serpent Ring. Once Siegward has been freed, he will thank the player and grant them a Titanite Slab as a gift.

## Fulfilling his duty

Siegward's last appearance will be at Yhorm the Giant's boss fight, where he will join the player with a Storm Ruler should he have been freed prior to entering Yhorm's boss arena for the first time. If he survives the encounter, he will give the player one final Siegbräu. Upon reloading the arena, his armor set, shield and Storm Ruler can be found within the arena. If the player has already defeated Yhorm before freeing Siegward from the cell in the Profaned Capital, the Storm Ruler will appear as a drop in the boss area, but the player will not receive his armor set nor shield or Siegbräu.
