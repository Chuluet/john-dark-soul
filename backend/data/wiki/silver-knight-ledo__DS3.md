# Silver Knight Ledo (DS3)

Silver Knight Ledo is a dark spirit in the Dark Souls III: The Ringed City DLC.

## Location

He is found in the Ringed City, and invades in a small room at the end of the Ringed City Streets. Once at the bottom of the stairs of the street, stay to the left to find a ladder which allows access to the last buildings. From there, drop onto a stone path that leads toward a destroyed tower. As you enter it, Ledo will begin invading, appearing on one of its balconies.

A golden manifestation of him can also be summoned by the Judicator Giants as one of their attacks.

## Plot

Ledo is an optional encounter that can be battled if the player is traversing his invasion area. He will appear regardless of if the player is a Host of Embers or not, a trait shared with other dark spirits in The Ringed City DLC. He must be killed before defeating Halflight, Spear of the Church, as that will prevent him from invading any longer. In case that already happened, one must reinstate the King's decree at the Purging Monument. This will allow him to return to his usual invasion spot. When defeated he drops his Great Hammer.

## Lore

Ledo is one of the Silver Knights who served Lord Gwyn and his royal family during the Age of Fire. He was known to be an eccentric, as he favored a massive Great Hammer, the heaviest tool wielded by a knight of Anor Londo, as opposed to the usual slender and elegant weapons of his peers.

He would eventually travel across the outskirts, where he became close friends with the Giants, as well as the legendary Havel the Rock. The mighty being may have even blessed his hammer, as the skill Call to Stone resembles that of Havel's Greatshields: Stone Flesh. His final destination seems to have been the Ringed City, where he has remained stationed. There, the Judicator Giants are able to summon a manifestation of him whenever they need protection, a great showcase of Ledo's bond with their kind.

By the time of the Dreg Heap, he seems to have become a full spirit. Despite his undying manifestation still standing guard and protecting the Judicators, he will personally fight anyone who enters an abandoned tower overlooking the large pool of stagnant darkness, which has swallowed entire districts of the city. The Ashen One may challenge and vanquish the hardened knight here.

## Cut Content

A few unused items seemingly tied to Ledo were discovered within the files of The Ringed City DLC. The undeniable one is the "Proof of a Pact", a spell that would allow the player to summon Ledo the same way the Judicator Giants do. This spell also contains a description, which speaks of Ledo's friendship with the tall beings, as well as stating clearly that he is the exception to these pacts, as everyone else that they summon is forced into servitude by them.

Four icons relating to an armor set were also found in the files. Their respective models and textures do not exist in the files, but the icons themselves allow us to take a nice look at it. The armor appears to be heavily inspired by the Silver and Black Knight Sets, sharing some design similarities with the Millwood Knight Set as well. The color of the armor is much very reminiscent to that of Ledo's Great Hammer, sacrificing the silvery look for a black-looking metal plating. This has led many to believe that this set was meant to be his unique armor before getting cut.
