# Sir Vilhelm (DS3)

, also known as , is a character in the Dark Souls III: Ashes of Ariandel DLC. At a certain stage of the DLC, he will permanently turn hostile.

He is voiced by Steven Hartley.

## Location

He is first encountered outside the chapel where Sister Friede resides, standing to the right of its massive door. He will tell the Ashen One that, if they are lost, they may speak with Friede for guidance.

After the player has reached the building that houses the Painter he'll permanently move away from his previous location, remaining inside the building to prevent players from meeting the captive girl.

## Plot

Vilhelm is the second friendly character that players are likely to come across, inviting the player to chat with Friede so she may help get them out of the painting. His cold, dismissive and almost threatening demeanor after speaking to her makes it clear that there's something wrong with him, and by extension, with his lady, though that becomes apparent not too long after. Trying to attack either he or Friede in this location will have both dissipate as if they were some kind of illusion, their real selves located somewhere else.

Once the player makes it to a seemingly abandoned library at the end of the Corvian Settlement, the knight will intercept them, becoming hostile and challenging them to a rather arduous duel that they must win if they want to proceed with the DLC. Vanquishing him nets players with his Onyx Blade and the Contraption Key, needed to free the painter girl.

Though he doesn't drop his set himself, it can later be retrieved by searching within the rotting basement of Ariandel Chapel, a place teeming with Giant Flies.

## Lore

thumb|Vilhelm's Helm, a piece of the knight's black armor.
Vilhelm is a hollow knight of Londor who served the Sable Church and its founding sisters, but held a much deeper loyalty and admiration for the eldest of them: Elfriede. He was certainly a skilled swordsman, as all members of the Sable Church are, yet he was considered to be particularly cold-blooded in his ways, acting akin to a brutal hangman.

Eventually, his liege would leave Londor and attempt to link the First Flame, burning to ash as a result. Her motivation for doing so is unknown, but the members of her church, including her very sister, appear to view that action as abandoning their cause and a source of great shame. Despite this, Vilhelm remained loyal to her, and would join her side again once the Unkindled were called back from their graves to slay the Lords of Cinder, who refused their duties.

The revived Elfriede would renounce everything after her resurrection, which included her knight's allegiance towards her. As a parting gift, she handed him a greatsword permeated with her blackflame. Accepting it would symbolize the end of his service. Yet in spite of accepting the blade, the knight followed his former mistress through the entirety of her miserable pilgrimage, until they found the Painted World of Ariandel, a place where the forlorn and forsaken would call their home. Here, Elfriede would find a new purpose, the knight still standing by her side of his own volition.

His mistress, now known simply as Friede, would gain quite the following among the Corvians who inhabited the painting, including the loyalty of the Corvian Knights and the favor of Father Ariandel himself. But as the rot spread and consumed the painting, she refused to let go of the world she had now called home. To ensure his lady's wishes would be granted, Vilhelm would take it upon himself to carry the Painter away, locking her in a library's attic to ensure she would not envision flame as she was meant to, and guarding the key to her hiding place fervently.

At some point, the Ashen One is beckoned to the Painted World of Ariandel by Slave Knight Gael, who believed they are the Ash meant to defeat the other Ash currently ruling over the painted landscape. The first meeting appears to be cordial, with Vilhelm pointing them to Friede, and then reinforcing her sentiment about them leaving the world behind and getting back to their journey. He also warns them to suppress their curiosity, as the depths of the painting would easily destroy them in their endeavors to sate it.
thumb|The Onyx Blade, meant to be a farewell gift.
When encountered in the building housing the captive painter girl, he will deride the Ashen One's inquisitiveness, claiming he has seen their type often and that they would never have what it takes to actually face the danger they have put themselves in by sticking their nose where it didn't belong. After a duel to the death, the Unkindled champion is victorious, slaying Vilhelm, who laments his failure to uphold his oath to Friede.

A corpse holding his entire suit of armor can later be found in the dark basement of Ariandel Chapel, overflowing with rot and insectoid abominations. Since this set of armor seemed to belong to him alone, it's rather likely that this was where Vilhelm truly died.
