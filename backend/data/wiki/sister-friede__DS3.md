# Sister Friede (DS3)

Sister Friede, born Elfriede, is a character and boss in Dark Souls III. She is voiced by Amara Karan.

Exclusive to the Ashes of Ariandel DLC, she is located in Ariandel Chapel in the Painted World of Ariandel. She can first be talked to as a regular character, and later acts as the final boss of the DLC; her boss battle has three phases, the second of which sees Father Ariandel fight alongside her.

## Location

Sister Friede is found sitting on a chair in Ariandel Chapel in the Painted World of Ariandel.

## Description

Friede wears the Ordained Set, and is armed with a scythe. She has burn marks on the right side of her face.

## Lore

thumb|Friede, along with Yuria and Liliane, founded the Sable Church of Londor.
Elfriede is the eldest sister of Yuria and Liliane. The three sisters at one point entered into the service of the Primordial Serpent Kaathe, becoming his maidens. The Primordial Serpent taught them his art of the Dark Hand, which could sap the essence of its victims. The sisters pursued Kaathe's goal of ending the Age of Fire founded by the gods and ushering the Age of Dark, and searched for a Dark Lord who could usurp the First Flame and use its powers to lead the humans into a new age. To this end, the three sisters used their battle prowess to establish the Sable Church, an organization that claimed to offer salvation to Hollows. Elfriede, the eldest sister, became its leader. The Church became a haven for those with unsavory or blurry pasts, along with other rejects of the Age of Fire. An increasing number of Undead and Hollows flocked to the Church, forming a society of wretchedly aged, deceitful, deeply detested Hollows. Their realm would be known as Londor, where Hollows could exist beyond the oppression of the Darksign.

With Elfriede at the helm, the Sable Church developed dark miracles, forged specialized weaponry, and trained knights unflinchingly loyal to their cause. The influence of the Sable Church would extend beyond Londor and into other realms via Londor Pale Shades, assassins and knights who served the interests of the Church abroad. At some point, Sir Vilhelm would come under the employ of the three sisters of the Church. He was especially loyal to Elfriede, becoming her steadfast follower and cold-blooded hangman.

Elfriede eventually departed Londor to attempt to link the First Flame. It is uncertain whether she intended to usurp the Flame, realizing Kaathe's vision of an Age of Dark, or wholly abandoned Londor to link the Flame for another purpose. Regardless, she was unable to withstand the Flame's power, and was burned to Ash.

Following Prince Lothric's refusal to link the First Flame, the Unkindled were exhumed from their graves, tasked with hunting down the Lords of Cinder that had refused to serve once again as kindling. Among the resurrected Unkindled was Elfriede, who instead chose to renounce everything, leaving behind her role as leader of the Sable Church, Londor, and even her name, becoming known simply as "Friede". Left without a home, she was eventually drawn into the Painted World of Ariandel, where the forlorn and the outcast were welcomed and found refuge. Friede became the first Ashen One to reside in it, coming to accept the cold of the Painted World and considering it her new home. She discovered that she wanted to "protect" the inhabitants of the Painted World, and thus assumed the precise form that they yearned for: that of their former mistress Priscilla. She went so far as to wield a great scythe, which was symbolic of a long-lost home for the inhabitants of the painting and similar to Priscilla's scythe. Her knight, Sir Vilhelm, eventually found her in the painted world, returning to her service despite having been released from duty.
thumb|The Scythe embodied nostalgia for the Painting inhabitants.
Over time Friede earned the trust of some painting inhabitants, to the point that the Corvian Knights of their village became infatuated with her. Above all, she managed to get close to Father Ariandel, a great Corvian who guarded the Flame inside the Painted World. His task was to unleash the Flame when the painting stagnated and began to rot, a custom intended for making way for the next painted world. However, when the painting began to be corrupted and its inhabitants made up their minds to burn it away, Friede opposed their wish, preferring a slow death in the comforting rot to the renewing change of the Flame. She convinced Ariandel to bury the Flame of the painting and to quench it with his blood, while her Corvian Knights swore to "protect" the painting from fire, even killing their brethren who opposed Friede. The Painter was imprisoned in the library of the corvian village and guarded by Sir Vilhelm, to prevent her from restoring the painting. His Lady's imprisonment prompted Slave Knight Gael to escape from the painting, taking with him a rotten scrap of it, and search for another Unkindled that could fulfill the prophecy of the painting, which stated that when the Ashes inside the painting became two, the Flame would return and burn the rot away.

Friede remained in the chapel of the Painted World, guarding the Flame and Father Ariandel against trespassers, while the inhabitants of the painting rotted and filthy insects spread. When an Ashen One arrived in the painting, she would kindly but firmly invite them to leave and return to the outside world, leaving the inhabitants of the painting in peace and forgetting about them. When they ignored her advice and descended the chapel to confront Father Ariandel, she would be forced to wield her scythe and confront them, not before asking Father Ariandel to turn his gaze away from the imminent carnage. When she is defeated the first time, an enraged Ariandel sparks the Flame he bled so much to subdue, in a desperate attempt to revive her. Now rekindled, Friede would come back from death to fight by his side, their souls linked. After falling a second time, Friede would rise yet again thanks to her inner Dark Flame, in a final struggle to defeat the Ashen One and prevent the Painted World from being burned away.

## Theories

It is possible that Friede inherited her Blackflame from the First Flame, following an only partially successful attempt to usurp it from its kiln.
 The Ashen Estus Flask description relates the item's nature to the Unkindled in that they "turn a bonfire's heat cold"; in addition, the Ember description highlights that similar nature of an Ashen One, as they lack the "embers that burn within a champion's bosom". This could point to a lack of inherited Flame within Unkindled Ones, making it unlikely as a source from which Friede's blackflame is derived.
 The weapon skill of the Onyx Blade, "Elfriede's Blackflame", states that the blackflame was "born of the similarly-hued flame that smolders within her". Following from the speculation that Unkindled Ones lack the original Flame, this similarly-hued flame possibly hails from a different source, possibly the First Flame itself within the Kiln, the very fire which ended up burning Elfriede.
 Given the Sable Church's affinity with dark miracles, it seems likely that the First Flame would attempt to manifest within its would-be usurper as a derivation from these arts that are powered by the Darkness of humanity.

## Summons

Slave Knight Gael: His sign is to the left of the boss arena entrance, opposite where Friede sits. Can be summoned even while not Embered. After activating his sign, he will be summoned not immediately, but as the second phase begins, and directly inside the arena.
