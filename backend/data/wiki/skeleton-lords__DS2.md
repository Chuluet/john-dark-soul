# Skeleton Lords (DS2)

The Skeleton Lords (スケルトンの王 Skeleton King) are a collective boss in Dark Souls II.

## Location

Located at the far end of the main path in Huntsman's Copse, near a waterfall.

## Description

During this battle, the player will fight not one but multiple foes at once. At the beginning of the fight, the player will have to face the three Skeleton Lords: one on the left, one in the middle and one on the right. The Lord on the left carries a Great Scythe, the Lord on the right wields the Roaring Halberd and the Lord in the middle carries a large and unique bone staff.

Killing the halberd-wielding Lord (right) will cause skeleton swordsmen to spawn, killing the scythe-wielding Lord (left) will spawn armored skeletons and killing the staff-wielding Lord (middle) will spawn bonewheel skeletons. Since all the skeletons share the same health bar, the player is required kill all of them in order to defeat the boss.

## Lore

The Skeleton Lords were originally servants of the Old Iron King and were tasked with hunting and imprisoning the undead in the Huntsman's Copse, aided by Necromancers, Torturers, and other soldiers. Eventually overcome by the Curse of Undeath, they lost their memories and abandoned their duties, choosing to create their own kingdom out of the bones of their victims and fashioning themselves "Kings".

## Summoning

There are no characters that can be summoned for this fight in the original version of Dark Souls II.

## Scholar of the First Sin

In Scholar of the First Sin, the player can summon up to two phantoms for this fight, Bashful Ray and Creighton the Wanderer.

Bashful Ray's summoning sign can be found inside one of the jail huts found between the two Artificial Undead spawn points. Creighton's summoning sign can be found in the cave inhabited by skeletons and necromancers after exhausting his dialogue.
