# Spear of the Church (DS3)

The is a boss battle in the Dark Souls III: The Ringed City DLC.

It is composed by Judicator Argo, a for each phase of the boss fight, as well as one Spear of the Church, acting as the actual boss. The Spear will either be an invading player member of the Spears of the Church covenant, or a special character, , who will appear if the player is offline.

## Location

The boss fight is found inside the Church of Filianore, situated at the end of the Ringed City after encountering Darkeater Midir on the bridge.

## Summons

Unbreakable Patches: Available if the player has completed Amnesiac Lapp's questline. His summon sign is located behind the right door of the cathedral, next to a spear-wielding statue.

## Plot

Peculiar in several ways, this boss fight is the penultimate mandatory battle in The Ringed City DLC, as well as within the whole game. As such it takes place within the church near the end of the titular city.

At first, the only enemy present is Judicator Argo, although his only goal is to summon three special enemies, which he will do regardless of the player's actions: a Spear of the Church and two Church Guardians, one for each phase of the boss fight. They are a support enemy unique to this battle, resembling Painting Guardians from Dark Souls, but using a regular player model instead of a unique character model.

The Spear of the Church, meanwhile, is the real boss of the encounter, getting a dedicated health bar. The mechanics of the fight are reminiscent of those of the Looking Glass Knight in Dark Souls II, removing the "unique boss" part of the equation in favor of a purely PvP experience. This means that the fight is never really the same, even among several different attempts, though that largely depends on the amount of players online at the moment. Even if you aren't a Host of Embers, an online player will be summoned to fight, so one must set the game in offline mode in order to make sure they will fight Halflight instead.

Halflight, Spear of the Church, is the replacement for anyone who doesn't have the chance of connecting to the internet or simply prefers a more hand-crafted, less chaotic experience when playing through the game. Since he is an NPC enemy, his behavior has patterns that the player can more easily memorize. He is no pushover despite that, though, posing a decent challenge even at the end of the game. Defeating either him or the online Spear of the Church will net you Filianore's Spear Ornament as well as a Titanite Slab.

Starting the boss battle counts as a betrayal of the Spears of the Church covenant, if one is part of it when they first engage it. However, in a unique occurence compared to all others in the entire game, this boss battle can be "reset" by "Reinstating the King's decree" via the Purging Monument in the Ringed City or Velka's statue in the Undead Settlement. The cost for doing so scales with the player's Soul Level, meaning it will be higher the higher level a player is. Choosing to reinstate the decree will allow players to resume serving the covenant or go through the battle again should they choose to. The only reward for doing that is another Spear Ornament, as the Titanite Slab drop can only happen once per playthrough, and will only be obtainable again when the player begins NG+.

## Stat Increases

The stats of a summoned Spear of the Church are increased for the whole fight. How much they are raised depends on the amount of phantoms, if any, the host has summoned.
When the host is alone, the Spear of the Church gains 10% its FP, 10% its Stamina, base poise increase of 10.00, Damage Absorption increase of 10.000(50.500 when Ritual Spear Fragment effect is active), and 20% increase in all Resistances.
When the host has summoned one phantom, FP is increased by 25%, 15% in Stamina, 20.00 in poise, 45.000(69.750) in Damage Absorption, and 60% in Resistances.
When the host has two phantoms, FP is increased by 35%, 20% in Stamina, 33.00 in poise, 65.000(80.750) in Damage Absorption, and 110% in Resistances.
When the host has three phantoms, FP is increased by 45%, 30% in Stamina, 50.00 in poise, 75.000(86.250) in Damage Absorption, and 160% in Resistances.
Note that all data was found while wearing no armor and rings, such that the stats' increase, especially Damage Absorption, would be affected negatively.

## Lore

The Spears of the Church are a group dedicated to the protection of Filianore's rest. They are led by Judicator Argo, who summons them in times of need, and they fervently uphold the King's decree. These loyal followers of the Gods receive the tips of spears from which they get their name from, items that were once part of the ritual spears of Princess Filianore's knights, which allow to call upon their holy weapons. They receive Spear Ornaments as proof of their successful tasks, and the most dutiful among them receive higher honors, like crystalized dew meant to celebrate and protect them, as well as the tips of the Divine Spears of gold for Spears of the Church of an especially high rank, who are themselves called Divine Spears for their efforts.

Halflight is the last recorded Spear of the Church. He was a young missionary, from the land of Oolacile, who remained behind in the city and faithfully protected the church from any intruder who dared approach. His elegant robe was never intended for fighting, and yet his attire served well in many battles where he emerged victorious. He may have gained the respect of Midir, as he wields a blade that can normally only be made with his soul, and like the knight Shira he was bound by duty to the princess.

Filianore is also protected by the Church Guardians, or Guardians of the Church, who work alongside the Spears of the Church to ensure her peaceful rest, although only few of them remain now. The Guardians wear the an identical outfit to that of the defunct guardians of the Painted World of Ariamis, though it's unclear which group technically came first, and wield throwing shivs. They also answer to Judicator Argo.

These holy warriors are all that stands between the Ashen One and the sleeping princess, who presumably held the key to obtaining the Dark Soul. Even the last of the Spears falls to them in time.
