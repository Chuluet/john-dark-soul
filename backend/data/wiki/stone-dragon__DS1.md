# Stone Dragon (DS1)

The Stone Dragon, also known as the Everlasting Dragon, is a character in Dark Souls.

## Location

The Stone Dragon is located at the very end of the large linear walkway in Ash Lake.

## Lore

The Stone Dragon is one of the few dragons left in the world, descendant of the Everlasting Dragons. It resides itself in the incredibly secluded and mysterious location of Ash Lake.

## Plot

The Stone Dragon initiates the player into the Path of the Dragon covenant.
