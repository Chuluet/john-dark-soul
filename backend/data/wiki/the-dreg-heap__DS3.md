# The Dreg Heap (DS3)

The Dreg Heap (吹き溜まり Fukidamari, The Drift) is a location in Dark Souls III: The Ringed City.

Accessed through either of two special bonfires respectively located at the Kiln of the First Flame or at the end of the Ashes of Ariandel DLC, it serves as the first area of the Ringed City DLC, followed by the titular city as the second half.

## Description

Found at the world's end, the Dreg Heap is the result of the close of the Age of Fire: with the world collapsing in on itself, ancient lands and ancient civilizations all find themselves converging into a single location. Among the remnants of old cilizations that converged onto the Dreg Heap are the windmill from Drangleic's Earthen Peak.

At the start of the area, an unnamed pilgrim of Londor watches over the merging of the kingdoms, musing over the end of the known world. The early areas of the Heap are populated by many Murkmen spawning from the ground, and flying over the area are powerful Angels manifested by the much-weaker, hiding Pilgrim Pupas.

A poisonous swamp is found deeper down, atop which Desert Pyromancer Zoey, a descendant of the Desert Sorceresses of Drangleic, acts as queen to the Thralls found within.

At the very bottom and center of the convergence, within the remains of Lordran's old Firelink Shrine, are the two last demons left in the world, the Demon in Pain and the Demon from Below. Beyond them is the Small Envoy Banner, which allows transportation to The Ringed City hidden beyond the end of the world.

## Accessing the Dreg Heap

There are two ways of accessing the Dreg Heap:

Should the player defeat the Twin Princes and place all four Cinders of a Lord upon their thrones at Firelink Shrine, the player will be able to access the Kiln of the First Flame. After proceeding to the Kiln from the Flameless Shrine, there will be a bonfire adjacent to the Kiln bonfire, with a tattered strip of Slave Knight Gael's cape affixed to the hilt. Accessing this bonfire will warp the player to the Dreg Heap.

Alternatively, if the player owns Ashes of Ariandel, they may defeat Sister Friede. Adjacent to the bonfire which appears after her defeat, a similar bonfire to the above will spawn at the back of the arena. This bonfire will also warp the player to the Dreg Heap.
