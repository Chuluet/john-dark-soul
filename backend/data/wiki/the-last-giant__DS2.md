# The Last Giant (DS2)

The Last Giant is a boss in Dark Souls II.

## Location

Within the Forest of Fallen Giants, the Last Giant is found past the second bonfire, at the bottom of a pressure plate-activated elevator.

## Description

Originally featured in the "Cursed" trailer, the Last Giant is believed to be the last surviving Giant. It has a stone pillar impaled through its torso, with many swords lodged in its back. Like the other Giants of Drangleic, the Last Giant lacks a face, possessing a hole where it would be.

## Lore

The Last Giant is the Giant Lord who laid waste to Drangleic before his defeat by an unknown hero long ago. Beaten and broken, he was imprisoned beneath the stronghold where he remains to this present day. Once his arena is entered, he recognizes the player's character and charges for one last stand.

## Summoning

Mild-Mannered Pate and Sellsword Luet may be summoned to assist in this battle. Their signs are located immediately in front of the fog gate. If Pate is summoned and survives the fight, when spoken to  in Earthen Peak, he will give the player his armor set, spear, greatshield, and the Ring of Thorns.

## Attacks

Stomp: Stomps his feet once. Uses this move when a player is near him, can be with either foot.

Triple Stomp Combo: Stomps his feet 3 times in a row, going from different foot each time until 3 stomps are performed.

Slam: Uses one of his hands and slams it onto the ground. Also the same move is performed with his arm.

Swipe: Swipes his arm/hand across the ground.

Fall: The Giant uses its entire body to hit the player by falling to the ground.
