# The Nameless King (DS1)

The Nameless King is the firstborn child of Gwyn, Lord of Sunlight and one of the deities who resided in Anor Londo during the Age of Fire. He was worshipped by humans as the "god of war".

During the war against the Dragons, the eldest son took part in the fighting together with Dragon Slayer Ornstein and his father Gwyn, using sunlight like him to create lightning spears and infuse his weapons to damage the creatures' scales. The god of war distinguished himself as a dragon slayer, using his swordspear imbued with lightning and even creating his personal armor with the scales of his preys. In addition, Ornstein, as his companion during the war, became his first knight and as a god of war, the firstborn created his own covenant of the Warrior of Sunlight, a group of knights who venerated the heir of the sun and supported other fighters in battle, obtaining medals with the symbol of the divinity as trophies. The eldest would recognize their efforts and reward them by teaching them his lightning miracles. The sun warriors would also develop an original miracle based on the story of the Nameless King, Ornstein, and the brave dragonslayers in their service.

After his father Gwyn left to link the First Flame, the throne of Anor Londo would pass to the eldest son of the King; and so the Nameless King inherited the Sunlight of his father. He would thus wear the golden crown, and succeed his father's titles of "king of the gods" and "god of the sun". The Nameless King was said to be inferior to none in terms of military strength. Gwyn's firstborn preserved his father's design and instructions, shepherding humans on the path to the Linking of the First Flame to continue the Age of Fire. In fact, underneath Anor Londo, a city was built exclusively for the undead, with an altar inside for the firstborn of Gwyn, and the aim of guiding a chosen undead who would one day sacrifice themselves to the Flame. Inside the city was the Undead Parish, a church at the center of the cult of the burg and connected to the first test to start on the path of Firelinking and ring the first Bell of Awakening. Furthermore, the town was connected to the Firelink Shrine where Kingseeker Frampt awaited the champion and directed them on their journey.

However, the rest of the firstborn's reign was marked by catastrophes. The Four Kings of New Londo were corrupted by the dark power of Darkstalker Kaathe, starting to sow devastation nearby the city of the gods. Following Knight Artorias's failure to defeat the Darkwraiths, the gods were forced to flood the city, sacrificing all the humans within, and seal it. A similar event happened in Oolacile when, again due to Kaathe, the awakening of the primordial human Manus and his fury led to the collapse of the country when Artorias was unable to defeat him. In addition to the grave situation on the borders of Anor Londo, the city of the gods itself became a dangerous place when a group of cultists began targeting the clan of Gwyn and their kind, and occult weapons capable of killing the gods reached inside the walls of Anor Londo. Furthermore, in the city of the gods Seath the Scaleless had now completely lost his mind, transforming his Archives into a prison and going so far as to kidnap the maidens of Gwynevere to carry out experiments on them.

The eldest son of Gwyn eventually decided to "sacrifice everything" to ally himself with the dragons. In the eyes of the other deities of Anor Londo, archenemies of the dragons, his actions amounted to an unforgivable sin; he was therefore dethroned and exiled from the city. The former king was deprived of his deific status, his statues destroyed and his name erased from the annals of history. Before leaving Anor Londo, the eldest left his sunlight blade miracle and ring on Gwyn's empty tomb in his mausoleum as a final farewell, still paying respects for his father. Now excommunicated from the pantheon of the gods and without a home, the god of war continued to watch closely over his warriors of the sunlight, listening to their prayers and rewarding the proofs of their exploits with his miracles of Sunlight Spear and Great Lightning Spear.
