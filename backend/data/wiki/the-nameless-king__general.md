# The Nameless King (general)

, alongside his Stormdrake known as the King of the Storm, is an optional boss in Dark Souls III. In the first phase of their fight, the player deals damage to the dragon mount, before fighting the King one-on-one in the second phase.

## Location

Found in Archdragon Peak, summoned by ringing the Great Belfry.
