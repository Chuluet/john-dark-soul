# Things Betwixt (DS2)

340px|right|Firekeeper's Dwelling
Things Betwixt is a location in Dark Souls II.

## Description

Things Betwixt is the starting area of the game where the player meets the Old Ladies and creates their character. It also acts as an optional tutorial stage, with helpful messages written on stone tablets.

## Adjacent locations

Majula

Fire Keepers' Dwelling - The only bonfire in the area is behind the Fire Keeper's cottage.

## Characters

Dyna & Tillo - There is a nest in the area with two crows asking for something smooth and silky, similar to Snuggly the Crow of Dark Souls. If certain items are dropped in front of them, the player will receive a randomized item in return.
