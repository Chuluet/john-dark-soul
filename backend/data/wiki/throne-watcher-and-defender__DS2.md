# Throne Watcher and Defender (DS2)

The Throne Watcher and the Throne Defender are dual bosses in Dark Souls II. They are fought at the end of the Throne of Want area, which can be accessed with the King's Ring.

They are both fought at once with individual health bars; when one is slain, their health is restored shortly after by the other, meaning that their respective health points must be reduced to zero in quick succession. If the player possesses the Giant's Kinship, Nashandra's boss fight will trigger immediately after the Watcher and Defender's.

## Description

The Throne Watcher and Defender are fought as a pair. The Defender is a heavily armored warrior while the Watcher is lighter and more agile, both wield a shield and a greatsword.

## Location

They are found in the Throne of Want, behind the King's Gate in Drangleic Castle which can only be opened with the King's Ring.

## Summoning

Vengarl of Forossa and Benhart of Jugo can be summoned for the fight, both summon signs can be found right outside the fog door.

In Scholar of the First Sin, Bradley of the Old Guard can also be summoned for this fight.
