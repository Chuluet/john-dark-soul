# Titchy Gren (DS2)

Titchy Gren is a character in Dark Souls II.

He is voiced by Con O'Neill, who also voiced Mohg, Lord of Blood in Elden Ring.

## Location

Found in Undead Purgatory, next to the bonfire which is located after the Executioner's Chariot arena. If killed, his gravestone will appear at exactly the same location where he's first met at Undead Purgatory.

## Plot

Titchy Gren will induct players into the Brotherhood of Blood covenant, but only if they have previously invaded and successfully killed another player or present to him a Token of Spite. Upon entering the covenant, Titchy Gren will offer the Crest of Blood, and he will sell various wares as well.

His dialogue reveals that he holds a grudge towards the Blue Sentinels, as he claims that the Sentinels are "obsessed with hunting down blood servants".

## Note

He will only drop the Nahr Alma Set's Hood upon death. The Robes as well as the Scythe of Nahr Alma will then be sold by Merchant Hag Melentia.
