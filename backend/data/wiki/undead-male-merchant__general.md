# Undead Male Merchant (general)

The Undead Male Merchant is a character, found in  Undead Burg in a small building near the wooden bridge where the first firebomb-throwing hollows appear. He seems insane but still retains enough of his mind to sell items. He has a creepy laugh and acts erratic, muttering strange things, and near-hollow. The Undead Male Merchant finds companionship in "Yulia", that suggests deep attachment. He's voiced by Miles Richardson, who also voiced Siegmeyer of Catarina.

__TOC__

## Plot

The Male Undead Merchant has little to do with the plot, although he does offer useful services to players early in the game. He also mentions that the Capra Demon, the Taurus Demon and the Hellkite Dragon have taken up residence nearby.

The Undead Male Merchant is the only merchant, discounting those found in the Artorias of the Abyss DLC, who sells Lloyd's Talismans in the game. These items are often useful in PvP, so his services may outweigh the Uchigatana and souls that he drops.

## Lore

The Undead Male Merchant was mistreated in his homeland when he turned undead and so is quite happy with being alone and in Lordran. He implies that abuse inflicted upon the undead is not uncommon. Little else is known about him, but the details we know about him does tell us a few things. He wields an Uchigatana, a weapon commonly associated with skilled swordsmen in the game, like a Former Warrior or Samurai-Inspired Wanderer. If Yulia is his sword, maybe he was once a proud warrior, and his sanity broke after going Undead. Another option makes him a Hollowing Merchant Clinging to His Past. He still sells goods, meaning he remembers his role in some way, but his mind is fractured. His laughter and speech patterns suggest he’s losing control, much like others on the verge of becoming full Hollows. Yulia Was Someone He Cared About is a possibility in his story. It could have been a loved one (wife, daughter, or friend) that he lost to the Undead curse. If so, his attachment to Yulia could be the last thing keeping him from going fully Hollow.
