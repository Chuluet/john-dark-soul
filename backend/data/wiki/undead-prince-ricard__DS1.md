# Undead Prince Ricard (DS1)

Undead Prince Ricard (不死の王子リカール) is a special enemy in Dark Souls.

## Location

Prince Ricard is a hollowed enemy found near the top of a tower in the latter half of Sen's Fortress, beyond the Berenike Knight.

He does not respawn when killed.

## Lore

Ricard's armor implies that he was from Astora. As a prince, he left his homeland and journeyed through many different lands for a long time. His willingness to travel so far, eventually reaching the dangerous land of Lordran, suggests that he was an adventurous and capable warrior. His journey ultimately ended in Lordran, where he eventually Hollowed.
