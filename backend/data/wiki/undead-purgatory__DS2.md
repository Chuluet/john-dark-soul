# Undead Purgatory (DS2)

The Undead Purgatory is a location in Dark Souls II.

## Description

The Undead Purgatory is connected to Huntsman's Copse through a hanging bridge, and it appears to be a prison or torture chamber plagued with Skeletons and Necromancers.

Upon entering the Purgatory, the player will have to face the Executioner's Chariot. After its defeat they will be able to meet Titchy Gren, who may induct the player into the Brotherhood of Blood covenant.
