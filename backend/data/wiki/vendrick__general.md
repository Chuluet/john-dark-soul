# Vendrick (general)

Boss=

|-|Memory=

Vendrick is an optional boss in Dark Souls II and a non-playable character in .

He is voiced by William Houston, who also voiced Marvelous Chester in Dark Souls and Oceiros in Dark Souls III.

## Location

Undead Crypt: Found after defeating Velstadt, passively circling his chambers. He won't attack the Bearer of the Curse unless provoked.
: In the Memory of the King, accessed by interacting with a pile of armor in Vendrick's chambers within the Crypt (Ashen Mist Heart required).

## Plot

After an untold amount of time in confinement, the King of Drangleic remains a hollowed husk of his former self. The player may choose to spare the king, leaving him to wander eternally, or slay him and end his misery.

Using the Ashen Mist Heart, the Bearer of the Curse may also enter the Memory of the King, where they may interact with King Vendrick as he was before he went hollow. Bitter and grieving his failures, Vendrick tells the Bearer to seek the crowns of old kings of yore. Once they have brought him all the crowns, Vendrick will unlock their power, making the Bearer of the Curse immune to the hollowing effects of the curse. He advises the Bearer of the Curse to seek a third path beyond Light and Dark, echoing his brother.
