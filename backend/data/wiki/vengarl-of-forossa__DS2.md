# Vengarl of Forossa (DS2)

|-|Body=Vengarl of Forossa is a character in Dark Souls II. He is voiced by Jason Pitt, who also voiced the Bell Keeper of Alken, Satsuki in Demon's Souls and Valtr, Master of the League in Bloodborne.

The name refers to two separate entities: the Head of Vengarl, a severed head and friendly merchant, and its headless body, a special enemy named the Body of Vengarl. After the Body is slain, Vengarl as a whole becomes a summonable phantom for up to five boss battles, including for Lud and Zallen in Crown of the Ivory King.

## Description

Vengarl of Forossa is uniquely composed of two parts: his disembodied head and his rampaging body. At one point, he was a brutal and bloodthirsty warrior, but his time alone has made him pensive and kind. As he was decapitated in battle, he still wears his ornate helmet, styled in the fashion of a wolf.

After defeating his body, Vengarl can be summoned as a whole against the Ancient Dragon, Throne Watcher and Defender, and Nashandra. In battle, his headless body pairs the Red Rust Scimitar with the Red Rust Sword, while his Summoning completes his scimitar with the Red Rust Shield.

## Head of Vengarl

Upon entering the Shaded Woods, the player will happen upon a shrouded portion of the forest. In one of the corners of these woods, Vengarl's head can be found resting on a pile of rubble. At first, he will ask that his solitude not be disturbed. After further prompting, Vengarl will open up, and eventually offer mercantile services to the player.

As the player converses with Vengarl, they learn that he was born in Forossa, forced to leave when the kingdom fell. He became a sellsword, but feels shame in having once thought only of war. Vengarl sometimes envisions his body's rampage, and warns the player to avoid it at all costs. He will then thank the player for listening to his ramblings and gift them his helmet.

After defeating his headless body and returning to Vengarl, he will thank the player and make his weapons and shield available for purchase. He will also become an available summon for the Ancient Dragon, Throne Watcher and Defender, Nashandra, and Lud and Zallen boss battles.

## Body of Vengarl

Vengarl's body can be found in one of two different locations depending on the version of the game. In the original game, it will be waiting by the Primal Bonfire after defeating The Duke's Dear Freja. In Scholar of the First Sin, it is instead found within the Shaded Ruins, in a room just past Manscorpion Tark's sand pit. At least one Fragrant Branch of Yore is needed to access it, regardless of direction of approach.

The body will wield Vengarl's scimitar and sword, and can deal tremendous damage with its combos. However, it is also slow to turn, allowing players to easily get around to backstab it. Upon defeat, the body will drop Vengarl's Set excluding the head piece, which is given to the player by Vengarl's head.

## Lore

Long ago, Vengarl was a great warrior. Born in Forossa, he eventually became a soldier in the army fighting for the prince of Forossa. He cites the fall of Forossa as occurring during his lifetime, born into the collapse of a kingdom that he was tasked to defend.

When Forossa fell, Vengarl fled and became a mercenary. As time went on, his reputation as a vicious warrior grew. Even amongst his warrior troupe, which was noted as being especially brutal, he was avoided by fellow soldiers.During one battle, he was decapitated; instead of dying, however, he awoke after the battle was over. Ever since then, the silence of the woods has given him much time to think, during which he has reflected on his life as a warrior.
