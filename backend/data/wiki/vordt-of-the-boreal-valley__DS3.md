# Vordt of the Boreal Valley (DS3)

Vordt of the Boreal Valley is a boss in Dark Souls III.

## Location

Found at the bottom of the High Wall of Lothric, opposite to the castle and near the gate leading outside the city, toward the Undead Settlement.

## Description

Vordt is an armored man-turned-beast creature who guards the Wall of Lothric. He wields a large mace.

## Lore

Vordt is a distant descendant of the old gods of Anor Londo. He was among the Irithyllians, hybrids between the race of the gods of Anor Londo and humans, who inhabit Irithyll of the Boreal Valley, the city built for them on the ruins of Anor Londo.

After Sulyvahn imprisoned Dark Sun Gwyndolin, proclaimed himself pontiff and assumed control of the city, Vordt was ordered to serve under the tyrant as a Boreal Outrider Knight, equivalent to being exiled. These knights, before being dispatched to foreign lands, were given the eye rings of the Pontiff, which eventually transformed them into savage, raving warriors who only knew how to serve as mindless guards, ensuring Sulyvahn's total control over them and depriving any means of desertion.

After Prince Lothric refused to link the First Flame and his kingdom fell into ruin due to various internal problems, Vordt was sent to Lothric with a division of Boreal Outrider Knights, with orders to keep a watchful eye over the Dancer of the Boreal Valley. Here he positioned himself to guard the main gate leading inside the city. Like other Outrider Knights, he would end up transformed by the rings of the Pontiff into a frenzied beast, causing his armor to be fused with his hide.

## Summons

Lion Knight Albert - His summon sign is located just before the stairs leading to the boss arena (only if High Priestess Emma is still alive).
Sword Master - If the player has defeated the Sword Master at Firelink Shrine, his summon sign will appear just before the entrance to the arena.
