# Weaponsmith Ornifex (DS2)

Weaponsmith Ornifex is a merchant found in Dark Souls II. After being freed in the Shaded Woods, she relocates to her home in Brightstone Cove Tseldora, where she is one of two characters together with Straid of Olaphis who can turn Boss Souls into Boss Soul Items.

She is a Crow Demon, whom, unlike her kin in Dark Souls, is not hostile towards the player. She is voiced by Anna Koval, who also voiced Tillo, as well as Anna in Elden Ring.

## Character information

Ornifex is a Crow Demon blacksmith residing in an abode at the Brightstone Cove Tseldora. She uses her skills to create all manner of equipment using an ancient technique unique to her people taught to them by Seath the Scaleless. She reserves her art for a select few, but after being freed by the Bearer of the Curse, she will offer her services to repay her debt.

## Location

Found in a locked room under the false floor just past the large Basilisk in the Shaded Woods. She is freed using the Fang Key. Once her dialogue is exhausted, she will move to her home next to the Lower Brightstone Cove bonfire in Brightstone Cove Tseldora.

## Lore

Weaponsmith Ornifex is a Crow Demon and a skilled blacksmith, well versed in the art of forging armaments with souls, an art particular to Ornifex's people. It is said that her people learned this technique from a pale beast that inhabited the land long ago.

Ornifex came to reside in the Brightstone Cove Tseldora, where she set up her workshop. At some point, she was trapped behind a locked door in the Shaded Woods, presumably taken prisoner by the Lion Clan. She was eventually freed by the Bearer of the Curse. In return for this kindness, Ornifex offered them her services as a blacksmith.

Ornifex appears to collect dolls, as her workshop is covered in them.
