# Xanthous King, Jeremiah (DS1)

Xanthous King, Jeremiah (黄の王ジェレマイア) is a character and phantom in Dark Souls.

## Location

Jeremiah invades in the Painted World of Ariamis, near the headstones in the area to the right of Phalanx from the bonfires.

## Description

Jeremiah wields a Notched Whip, which causes Bleed buildup, and wears the Xanthous Set. He uses the Pyromancies Great Chaos Fireball, Chaos Storm and Chaos Fire Whip.

## Plot

Jeremiah invades as a red phantom with the express purpose of killing the player and nothing more. As with every other Red Phantom, Jeremiah will only invade if the player has reversed their Hollowing and has not killed the area boss.

## Lore

A Xanthous Scholar, Xanthous King, Jeremiah is known as the "legendary exile". As he bears the title of "King", and the fact that he utilizes Chaos Pyromancies suggest he was King of Izalith, and therefore husband of the Witch.

He presents a great bump over his head, covered with bandages and soaked in blood, which is similar to the Parasitic Wall Hugger encountered in Blighttown. The similarities, and the fact that the Wall Hugger internal name is "Prince Izalith", indicate that Jeremiah probably carries an insect demon egg.

As his corpse and phantom are both located in the Painted World, it's safe to assume that he was somehow trapped in the painting and unable to get out. The reason for his exile in the Painted World is unknown, but giving the Xanthous Crown to Snuggly the Crow, a Ring of Favor and Protection is given in exchange, implying a connection between Jeremiah and the goddess Fina. Perhaps Jeremiah cheated The Witch of Izalith with Fina, leading to his banishment.

He was probably killed by Priscilla as his corpse is found right next to her. This implies that he tried to kill Priscilla, as the half dragon is shown to only harm in self defense.
