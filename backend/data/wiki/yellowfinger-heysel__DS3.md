# Yellowfinger Heysel (DS3)

Yellowfinger Heysel is a dark spirit and summonable phantom in Dark Souls III.

A Xanthous and Finger of Rosaria, she first appears as an invader, and if conditions are met will be summonable in Farron Keep.

## Locations

Road of Sacrifices
If the player is embered, she invades as a dark spirit in the marshlands area, near the Crucifixion Woods bonfire.
Farron Keep
Invades as a dark spirit in the poisonous swamp, near the brazier found to the right of the main gate.
Can be summoned as a white phantom. Her sign is located next to the tower near the Keep Ruins and will only appear after the player has met the following criteria:
Extinguished the three braziers.
Offered at least one Pale Tongue to Rosaria in the Cathedral of the Deep.
The Abyss Watchers must be alive, or Heysel's sign will not appear.

## Lore

Heysel is a scholar part of the Xanthous, an elusive order of researchers who specialized in long-lost sorceries, particularly those of the lost land of Oolacile which distorded and manipulated light. A sorcerer, she notably uses Chameleon, a sorcery of Oolacile, to sneak up on the individuals she invades.

Heysel is also a devoted follower of Rosaria, Mother of Rebirth and a member of her covenant, Rosaria's Fingers, along with Creighton of Mirrah, Longfinger Kirk and Ringfinger Leonhard; most of the Fingers take on a "Finger" nickname to symbolize their devotion, and Heysel's "Yellowfinger" nickname symbolizes her dual allegiance to both Rosaria and the yellow-clad Xanthous. Although Rosaria's Fingers specialize in invasions, Heysel is polite and idealistic, and her fellow Finger Leonard claims that she "choose[s] to believe that all Fingers share camaraderie", offering her assistance to her fellow Fingers.

Heysel's father was the leader of the Undead Legion's acolytes. Although her father was trusted with both the Farron Hail and Great Farron Dart sorceries, both are described as being "of his daughter, Heysel, [and] refined by a Crystal Sage", indicating that they were originally provided to him by Heysel, who herself uses both sorceries. The connections between Heysel and the two Crystal Sages, who specialize in crystal magic, is unclear, although one of the Sages joined the Undead Legion, which Heysel's father was a part of.

In addition to being home to her father's Undead Legion, the Farron Keep around which Heysel gravitates is also the location of the Golden Scroll chronicling the research of the Xanthous and the location of the Antiquated Set from Oolacile.

## Description

Heysel comes clad in the Xanthous Set and wears the Obscuring Ring, rendering her invisible at a distance.

She's armed with the Heysel Pick, a hybrid weapon acting as both a melee weapon (categorized as a hammer) and a catalyst for sorceries, allowing her to quickly combine casting sorceries while in the middle of an encounter. Throughout the battle, she will occasionally switch to a Light Crossbow instead.

Her main method of attack consists of constantly casting Farron Hail, unleashing a quick barrage of small Magic-based projectiles at great speed. She sometimes combines this attack with a casting of Great Farron Dart, attempting to make her enemies fall off-balance and inflict heavy damage. She may rarely attempt to sneak up on the player using Chameleon.
