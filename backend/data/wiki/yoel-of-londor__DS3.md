# Yoel of Londor (DS3)

Yoel of Londor is a character and merchant in Dark Souls III. He is voiced by David Sterne.

A gentle, soft-spoken pilgrim of Londor who relocates to Firelink Shrine after being met in the Undead Settlement, he offers the Ashen One his wares as sorcerer as well as a unique, "free" way to level up in souls up to five times. Doing so prior to reaching the Catacombs of Carthus is a requirement for the Londor questline (see The Usurpation of Fire ending).

## Location

He is first encountered near the Foot of the High Wall bonfire in the Undead Settlement, on the other end of the bridge from the gate leading into the settlement proper. The area is filled with dead pilgrims, although Yoel, located to the right, is still alive and can be talked to. He will offer to enter into Ashen One's service, and if the player accepts will move to Firelink Shrine. He can be found in the basement, at the end of the corridor on the west wing.

## Lore

Yoel is a former sorcerer from Londor. Although he feels that the sorceries of Londor pale in comparison with those of Vinheim, he knows how to use his skills to draw out strength from humans. One of the pilgrims of Londor whose nature is unclear and travel to Lothric on a quest meant to culminate in death, Yoel failed in his mission, as he lives still among the bodies of his more successful comrades, until the appearance of the Ashen One offers him an unexpected chance for redemption.

He was a friend and follower of Yuria of Londor, one of the three founders of the Sable Church of Londor.

Upon appearing following Yoel's questline and his death, Yuria claims to be "a close friend of [Yoel]" and will declare that thanks to the Ashen One, Yoel has redeemed himself. Yoel' dying words if he is killed before completing his questline confirm that he did know Yuria, and if he is killed after completing his questline (meaning five Dark Sigils have been obtained) he will gleefully thank the Ashen One for this death, confirming that whatever manner he may die to within Firelink Shrine while the Ashen One is away, he is very likely willing.

## Questline

Yoel is first encountered near the beginning of the Undead Settlement, at the edge of a crumbled bridge. The only survivor among many other less fortunate pilgrims like him, he seems enlightened to be in the presence of the Ashen One and asks if he could be allowed in their service. Agreeing to his plea will make him move to Firelink Shrine. There, he can be found on the lowermost level, at the end of the right corridor past Greirat, where he will sell an assortment of Sorceries to the player. Refusing his service will result in Yoel remaining in this spot, waiting for the Ashen One to change their mind.

Selecting the "Talk" option while in his interaction menu will teach the player the "Beckon" gesture. As a former sorcerer, all of his wares are sorceries.

Yoel also offers to level up the Ashen One without the use of souls. By selecting the "Draw Out True Strength" option, he will do so, functioning much like the Fire Keeper. Leveling up this way will place a Dark Sigil in the player's inventory. From this point onward, every time the player dies, they will gain a Hollowing level based on the number of Dark Sigils they possess. At 2, 6, 12 and 15 Hollowing, Yoel will again present the player with the option to level up for free, granting them an additional Dark Sigil each time up to a maximum of 5.

"Drawing Out True Strength" five times is necessary to complete Yoel's questline, which, together with the subsequent Yuria of Londor questline, lead to The Usurpation of Fire ending. It must be done before reaching the Catacombs of Carthus, since if Yoel is still alive when the player reaches said location, he will die the very moment the Catacombs are entered (including in the Settlement if he has not been recruited). If Yoel dies without having "Drawn Out True Strength" five times, or if the player heals their Dark Sigils with the Fire Keeper after she receives the Fire Keeper Soul, the Londor questline will have failed.

Yoel's dead body will be on the same spot he was when the Ashen One last saw him; uncharacteristically, his dead body will always remain, unless the player killed him themselves. If his questline was completed, Yuria will be next to his body, selling his old wares and allowing for the questline to continue. It is of note that killing Yoel after obtaining all five Dark Sigils does not void the questline, although there are no benefits to doing so.
